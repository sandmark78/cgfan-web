## Prompt

A minimalist 3D render featuring abstract geometric shapes floating in a serene, gradient background. The composition includes smooth spheres, perfect cubes, and elegant torus forms arranged in a balanced, asymmetrical layout.

Materials: matte ceramic textures in soft pastel colors — blush pink, sage green, cream white, and pale lavender. Each shape has subtle surface imperfections that add tactile realism.

Lighting: soft studio lighting with gentle shadows, creating depth without harsh contrast. Ambient occlusion adds subtle depth to contact points.

Background: seamless gradient from warm cream at the top to cool gray at the bottom, creating a sense of infinite space.

Style: contemporary digital art, reminiscent of Beeple's early work or modern design studio aesthetics. Clean, sophisticated, gallery-quality.

Technical: rendered in Cinema 4D with Octane Render, 8K resolution, subsurface scattering on materials, ray-traced reflections.

Composition: rule of thirds, negative space used intentionally to create breathing room, focal point slightly off-center.

Mood: calm, meditative, sophisticated — perfect for modern brand identity or editorial design.

Avoid: cluttered compositions, harsh shadows, oversaturated colors, text overlays, watermarks, low-poly aesthetics.
