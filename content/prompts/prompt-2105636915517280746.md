## Prompt

Create one finished vertical 4:5 lifestyle advertising poster for SUNNILO, a fictional roasted makhana snack brand. The featured product is a real orange resealable pouch of SUNNILO Peri Peri Roasted Makhana, and it must be the sharpest, most commanding object in the image.

Use a warm sunlit family living room shot from a low coffee-table viewpoint with a moderately wide lens. In the middle ground, one cheerful child around 8 years old lounges diagonally across a golden-yellow sofa, wearing a cream T-shirt with a small sunshine emblem, mustard shorts, and white socks with thin yellow stripes. One leg lifts casually behind the sofa back. The child smiles toward camera and extends one hand forward, holding the pouch close to the lens. Keep the arm connection natural, with a believable grip and five correct fingers. The pouch sits in the left-center foreground, occupying about one quarter of the image, with its full front panel, top seal, and lower corners visible.

Design the pouch as a credible manufactured snack package: vivid tangerine-orange flexible film, sealed edges, resealable top, slight crinkles under the fingers, correct thickness, subtle satin reflections, and a stable printed front. The original SUNNILO logo is a compact cream badge with a small rising sun. Print only: “SUNNILO”, “ROASTED MAKHANA”, and “PERI PERI”. Add a restrained photo-style cluster of seasoned makhana near the lower front. Do not add nutrition or health claims.
