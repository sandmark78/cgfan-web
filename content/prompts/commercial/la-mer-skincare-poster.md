Create an ultra-premium 2x2 four-panel skincare campaign poster for La Mer, with absolute brand consistency but four completely different fantasy natural environments. Each quadrant must feel like a distinct magical world with its own ecological identity, color atmosphere, and cinematic mood, yet all four must still clearly belong to one unified La Mer visual universe. The product must remain the absolute protagonist in every panel, fully integrated into nature as if it naturally belongs there.

The four environments and their visual metaphors:
- Tropical rainforest — conveys deep nourishment: humid tropical sanctuary, filtered golden light, wet foliage, living moss
- Coastal cliffs — conveys revitalizing balance: ocean cliff renewal, salt mist, mineral-rich rocks, cool open daylight
- Moonlit forest — conveys nighttime repair: moonlit forest ritual, silver-blue haze, damp moss, subtle bioluminescence
- Sea crystals — conveys hydration and radiance: sea-mineral sanctuary, pearl-aqua light, translucent crystals, luminous moisture

The scenes become more than just pretty backdrops; they serve as visual metaphors for the product's key selling points. First, determine the benefits each product aims to convey, then match them with the appropriate climate, lighting, and textures.

The final artwork must feel like a world-class luxury skincare series and like award-winning cinematography frozen into four still frames: hyper-real product photography, exquisite environmental storytelling, elegant natural interaction, subtle fantasy atmosphere, and collector-grade visual refinement. Use refined English title typography, subtle official-feeling branding, and minimal support text in a premium editorial layout.

Core layout: Use a strict 2x2 four-panel composition with balanced spacing, subtle grid gaps, and a unified color palette that shifts slightly per quadrant.
