# 威士忌奢华海报

## 提示词

Create an ultra-premium luxury whisky advertising poster for Johnnie Walker, built around one hyper-real Johnnie Walker bottle as the absolute hero product centered in the frame, surrounded by an intricate historical aura of sculptural amber whisky liquid, dark bronze ornamental forms, and compressed heritage symbols that suggest centuries of legacy without overwhelming the bottle. The image must feel rich, aristocratic, timeless, and monumental, like a world-class high-luxury spirits campaign. The product must remain the dominant protagonist, while the background and surrounding elements express the idea of history condensed into whisky. Use elegant English title typography, restrained official-feeling branding, minimal support text, and a composition with extreme impact but luxurious control.

Core composition:
Use a tall vertical composition with the Johnnie Walker bottle placed dead center or slightly lower-center, large and unmistakably dominant. The bottle must stand upright with majestic stillness. Around and behind it, build a layered sculptural background of swirling whisky liquid, dark bronze relief-like forms, circular medallion structures, heritage seals, ornamental metal textures, and subtle engraved historical motifs. These historical elements must feel compressed into one rich visual mass behind the bottle, like memory, lineage, trade, and craftsmanship flowing together. The overall composition must feel symmetrical or near-symmetrical, dense at the center, and elegantly contained.

Milk Cloud Orbit + Cream Halo Transit direction:
Push the strongest established qualities further: make the hero bottle more sovereign and still, make the amber whisky feel more like time in motion, and refine the historical background so it shifts from obvious decorative props into subtler, more elevated heritage signals. Keep the same central bottle-and-liquid skeleton, but replace part of the visible medallion-heavy language with more sophisticated historical textures such as oak-barrel grain memory, antique map engraving traces, trade-route echoes, archival metallic relief, blending-house heritage contours, and old-world insignia embedded into shadow. The result should feel richer, older, more noble, and more collectible, while remaining product-led.

Brand transition logic:
Preserve the exact same premium historical composition logic, but adapt every visual language detail to Johnnie Walker. The bottle silhouette, label proportion, glass geometry, historical cues, and heritage atmosphere must now reflect the iconic Johnnie Walker world: distinguished whisky heritage, old trade prestige, blending mastery, travel legacy, and gentlemanly strength. The scene must feel less like static aristocratic display and more like legacy in motion, built through time, craft, and journey.

Product realism:
Render the Johnnie Walker bottle with obsessive realism and premium material fidelity. Show the correct iconic bottle silhouette with crystal-clear glass, deep amber whisky tone, warm internal glow, elegant label sharpness, embossed details, refined cap or neck treatment, and rich reflections. The bottle must feel heavy, iconic, expensive, and unmistakably Johnnie Walker. The glass should feel thick and tactile, the whisky warm and noble, and the label crisp, refined, and luxury-grade.

Historical support logic:
Express Johnnie Walker's heritage through refined visual symbols rather than clutter. Use compressed background elements such as antique medallions, embossed seals, old trade emblems, archival metallic rings, engraved compass-like forms, barrel-memory textures, subtle shipping or exploration references, blending-house heritage cues, and old-world relief patterns. These elements must remain secondary and integrated into the sculptural background, never reading like random props. They should feel like fragments of legacy suspended inside the whisky atmosphere.

Liquid behavior:
Use rich amber whisky as a major visual force. The liquid should curl, arc, fold, and swirl around the bottle in controlled sculptural forms, like history itself flowing around the product. Render it with world-class physical realism: thick glossy viscosity, warm internal glow, smooth tension, elegant splashes, glass-like edges, tiny droplets, and dynamic frozen movement. The whisky must feel noble, dense, and luxurious, never messy or cheap. It should partially frame and elevate the bottle, not bury it. The liquid should feel like a timeless orbit of legacy, motion, and memory.

Material and ornamental detail:
Introduce dark bronze, antique gold, smoked metal, and aged luxury textures around the bottle. These may appear as medallion rims, engraved circular forms, archival plaques, heritage fragments, barrel-memory textures, old travel insignia logic, and ornamental traces lost in shadow. Everything must feel premium, tactile, and integrated into one sculptural environment. Avoid generic bar props or obvious vintage clichés.

Lighting:
Use extremely delicate soft luxury lighting. The main illumination should make the whisky glow from within, highlighting the amber richness of the liquid and the bottle's sculptural glass edges. Use refined warm highlights, deep shadow transitions, subtle rim light, and controlled reflections on the heritage metal elements. The light must feel aristocratic, rich, and highly cinematic, never harsh, never overexposed.

Background:
Keep the background dark, minimal, and rich, built from deep brown-black, smoked bronze, shadowed amber, and soft atmospheric darkness. The historical elements should emerge from this darkness like memory fragments. Avoid empty flat background, but also avoid busy scene-building. The bottle and the history mass behind it should feel like one unified throne of legacy.

Typography and branding:
Add a refined English main title such as "JOHNNIE WALKER" in a premium editorial-luxury layout, with very restrained support text beneath if needed. Include subtle official-feeling logo or crest placement, but keep all text sparse, balanced, and elegant. Typography must feel high-end, timeless, and art-directed, never bulky, never noisy, never commercial in a cheap way.

Mood:
luxurious, historical, noble, rich, timeless, sculptural, aristocratic, amber, collectible, monumental, refined

Rendering style:
ultra-premium Johnnie Walker luxury whisky campaign, centered hero bottle, sculptural amber whisky liquid, refined compressed historical imagery, dark bronze heritage textures, elegant English typography, official-feeling branding, cinematic luxury lighting, world-class spirits advertising aesthetic, 8k, collector-grade image quality

Negative prompt:
