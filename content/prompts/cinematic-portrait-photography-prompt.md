## Prompt

Ultra-realistic cinematic close-up portrait of a beautiful young woman lying peacefully among a dreamy field of wildflowers during golden hour. Her face is softly illuminated by warm sunset light, creating gentle shadows and highlights that accentuate her natural beauty.

Composition: shallow depth of field, 85mm lens, f/1.4 aperture, subject in sharp focus with creamy bokeh background.

Lighting: golden hour backlight with subtle rim lighting on hair, soft fill light from the side, volumetric light rays filtering through the scene.

Mood: serene, ethereal, romantic — capturing a moment of quiet contemplation.

Technical: photorealistic skin texture, natural makeup, wind-swept hair, shot on Sony A7R IV with Zeiss Batis 85mm, Kodak Portra 400 film emulation.

Style: Vogue editorial, fashion campaign quality, award-winning photography aesthetic.

Avoid: harsh shadows, oversaturated colors, plastic skin, artificial poses, text overlays, watermarks.
