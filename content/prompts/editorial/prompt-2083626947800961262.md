# 实验室纪录片采访，15秒真实感

**作者**: Saul Goodman  
**日期**: 2026-08-01  
**评分**: 61/80 (8+7+8+8+8+8+7+7=61)

## Prompt

```
Create a photorealistic 15-second observational documentary interview inside a modern scientific research laboratory. The video should feel like authentic behind-the-scenes footage captured by a professional documentary crew, emphasizing realism, subtlety, and natural human behavior.

A brilliant female scientist with dark hair tied in a relaxed low bun, thin black rectangular glasses, and a dark blazer over a neutral shirt prepares for an important interview. Preserve her exact facial identity, hairstyle, clothing, body proportions, and accessories throughout the entire sequence. Authentic skin texture, natural imperfections, and realistic facial details.

0–3 seconds: A locked eye-level 50mm interview shot. She quietly enters frame, gently pulls out a stool, sits down, smooths her blazer, adjusts the lavalier microphone cable, and takes a calm breath. Every movement is slow, deliberate, and naturally timed.

3–7 seconds: She straightens her posture, lightly rolls her shoulders back, adjusts her glasses with one finger, folds her hands in her lap, briefly looks downward, then settles into complete stillness. Small blinks, subtle breathing, and realistic micro-expressions only.

7–11 seconds: Cut to a reverse over-the-shoulder angle revealing the filming environment. A large soft light, camera tripod, monitor cart, microphones, and scientific equipment are visible in the softly lit laboratory. She remains composed and motionless while the camera quietly observes.

11–15 seconds: Return to the original interview framing with an almost imperceptible slow push-in. She takes one controlled breath, slowly raises her eyes to the lens, maintains unwavering eye contact, gives the slightest confident expression, and quietly says, "Alright. I'm ready." Hold on her gaze until the final frame.

Lighting is soft, natural, and motivated by practical sources with cool laboratory ambience. Neutral color palette, realistic exposure, natural depth of field, subtle lens characteristics, and authentic documentary sound design.
```
