## 简介

时尚短发人物编辑肖像框架 — 以短发为核心视觉标识的近距离时尚人像编辑模板。强调自然皮肤纹理、真实发丝质感和故事驱动的场景设计，避免 AI 塑料美感。

## Prompt

Subject: [character / face variation]
Outfit: [top + bottom + accessories]
Setting: [location + story context]
Pose: [simple natural pose]
Lighting: [time / light source / mood]
Color palette: [main colors]
Aspect ratio: 9:16

Create a realistic close-up fashion portrait of an adult young woman with a short, layered dark haircut and a sweet-cool, slightly edgy vibe.

Keep the hairstyle as the key visual identity: airy layered short hair, natural volume, slightly messy ends, loose strands around the eyes, realistic individual hairs, never overly neat or wig-like. The face should feel natural and distinctive, with lively eyes, realistic skin texture, subtle makeup, and no plastic AI beauty look. If a reference image is provided, preserve the overall character type and visual language without copying the exact face.

Use a strong direct-portrait composition rather than ordinary street photography. Frame from the head to around the upper or mid-thigh, with the subject filling roughly 75–90% of the image. Use a straight-on or slightly low camera angle to emphasize the face, short hair, shoulders, waistline, and overall presence.

Place the subject in a visually designed, story-driven environment rather than a generic room. The setting should clearly support the mood while remaining secondary to the person. Good environments include an after-hours gallery, retro hotel elevator hall, late-night laundromat, rooftop greenhouse, or other distinctive real spaces.

Keep the photography highly realistic: natural body proportions, realistic fabric folds, authentic skin and hair texture, believable indoor or outdoor lighting, subtle depth of field, and clean photographic detail.

The final image should feel like a stylish, intimate portrait with a strong character identity — short hair, close framing, expressive eyes, simple fashion, and a memorable setting.
