## Prompt

Create a photorealistic top-down macro photograph of an artist actively drawing intricate fine black ink lines and delicate scrollwork onto [PAPER TYPE] resting on [WOODEN SURFACE / DESK SETTING]. The flat illustration should seamlessly transition into a highly detailed 3D bas-relief miniature world emerging physically from the paper, creating a convincing illusion that the drawn artwork has come alive and broken through the page.

The central scene features [MAIN CHARACTER: AGE / GENDER / HAIR / CLOTHING / POSE], rendered as a finely sculpted miniature figurine with realistic [MATERIAL: CLAY / PAPER / FABRIC / WOOL / RESIN] textures. The character interacts with [PRIMARY OBJECT / COMPANION] and is surrounded by [ENVIRONMENT / LANDSCAPE], including intricate miniature details such as [SECONDARY ELEMENTS: TREES / FLOWERS / GEARS / CORAL / MUSHROOMS / ARCHITECTURE].

Integrate [SPECIAL MATERIAL EFFECTS: WATER DROPS / GLOWING LIGHTS / SNOW / RESIN / PAINT WASHES] naturally into the composition. Fine black ink lines should flow from the artist's drawing hand into the emerging 3D scene, creating a seamless visual bridge between 2D illustration and dimensional sculpture.

Use [LIGHTING STYLE] to create soft, cinematic illumination, realistic highlights, gentle or deep shadows, and strong tactile depth. Emphasize the contrast between the flat paper surface and the raised sculptural elements, with ultra-realistic textures visible in [TEXTURE DETAILS].

Photorealistic, ultra-detailed macro photography, tactile handcrafted textures, realistic depth, shallow depth of field, cinematic composition, natural material imperfections, 8K detail, premium editorial art photography, no distortion, no extra text, no watermark --ar 1:1
