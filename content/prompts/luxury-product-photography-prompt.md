## Prompt

A luxury commercial product photography shot featuring a sleek matte emerald green glass bottle with a polished gold cap, placed on a reflective dark surface. The bottle is illuminated by dramatic studio lighting with soft highlights and deep shadows, creating a premium, high-end aesthetic.

Background: seamless gradient from deep charcoal to rich black, with subtle volumetric light rays adding depth.

Composition: centered product, slight low-angle perspective, shallow depth of field with crisp focus on the bottle label area.

Style: high-end advertising campaign, photorealistic, 8K resolution, shot with Phase One 150MP, Hasselblad H6D color profile.

Mood: sophisticated, exclusive, editorial quality — reminiscent of Dior, Chanel, or Tom Ford fragrance campaigns.

Avoid: cluttered backgrounds, harsh reflections, text overlays, watermarks, low resolution, oversaturated colors.
