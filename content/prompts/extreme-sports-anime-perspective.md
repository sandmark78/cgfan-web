## 简介

极限运动动漫透视插画框架 — 以超广角镜头和戏剧性透视打造的日系动漫关键视觉模板。强调前景放大元素→肢体→躯干→面部→环境的深度序列，配合低角度倾斜构图创造动态张力。

## Prompt

Subject: [character / outfit]
Action: [jumping / falling / crouching / attacking]
Foreground anchor: [sneaker / racket / camera / weapon / other object]
Environment: [skatepark / sports court / rooftop / city canyon]
Color palette: [main colors]
Aspect ratio: 9:16

Create a high-quality extreme-perspective anime illustration with an ultra-wide-angle lens and dramatic foreshortening.

Place one oversized foreground element extremely close to the camera as the main visual anchor. Build a clear depth sequence from foreground object → limbs → torso → face → environment, with strong near-big / far-small perspective.

Use a very low, tilted, or unconventional camera angle depending on the action. The character should feel like they are jumping, falling, lunging, or reaching into the frame rather than posing statically.

Let the environment reinforce the perspective with strong leading lines and a clear vanishing point: railings, court lines, buildings, power lines, roads, bridges, or architectural edges should naturally converge into depth.

Keep the character anatomy accurate and readable despite the extreme foreshortening. Hair, clothing, straps, and accessories can follow the direction of motion to create stronger visual energy.

Style: premium Japanese anime key visual, clean anime linework, painterly anime shading, soft cel shading, cinematic composition, matte natural skin, crisp silhouette, controlled colors, strong depth.
