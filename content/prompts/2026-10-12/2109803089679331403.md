Intricately detailed irises with fine radial fibers, delicate tonal variation, a darker limbal ring, naturally defined pupils, realistic corneal curvature, and physically plausible reflections. Soft peach-pink lips with a delicate rose-nude gradient, realistic lip anatomy, subtle vertical lip lines, natural moisture, restrained specular highlights, and a slightly parted mouth. No excessive gloss, no exaggerated lip volume, no artificial lipstick texture.
