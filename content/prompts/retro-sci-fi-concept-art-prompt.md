## Prompt

A cinematic retro-futuristic cityscape at twilight, inspired by Blade Runner and Syd Mead concept design. Massive neo-brutalist megastructures tower over narrow streets, their concrete facades illuminated by flickering neon signs in Japanese and Chinese characters.

Atmosphere: heavy rain, volumetric fog, holographic advertisements floating in the air, flying vehicles with glowing thrusters weaving between buildings.

Lighting: dramatic chiaroscuro with warm orange neon lights contrasting against cool blue-purple ambient fog. Street-level lighting creates pools of color on wet pavement.

Composition: low-angle perspective looking up at the towering architecture, with a lone figure in a trench coat standing in the foreground for scale.

Style: Syd Mead concept art meets Moebius environmental illustration. Photorealistic materials, cinematic color grading, film grain texture.

Technical: shot on Arri Alexa 65 with Panavision anamorphic lenses, Kodak Vision3 500T film stock emulation, 8K resolution.

Mood: dystopian yet beautiful, melancholic, awe-inspiring — capturing the essence of 1980s cyberpunk vision of the future.

Details: visible rain droplets on camera lens, lens flares from neon lights, atmospheric haze, intricate architectural details, diverse crowd of people in futuristic clothing.

Avoid: clean utopian aesthetics, bright daylight, modern glass buildings, text overlays, watermarks.
