## Prompt

A flower seller closing a nearly empty outdoor market at dusk, gathering loose stems and fallen petals into one final bouquet while the surrounding stalls are already dark. A single hanging lamp lights the flowers and the seller's hands against the deepening blue of the evening. Painterly contemporary style, expressive brushwork, rich floral textures, quiet atmosphere and a subtle cinematic mood.
