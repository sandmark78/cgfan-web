## Prompt

Create a 9:16 high-fashion editorial poster.

Subject: [male / female / fashion model / artist]
Outfit: [fashion styling]
Palette: [red / black / white / custom palette]
Main title: [TITLE]
Design direction: [perspective × typography / blur × sharp detail / profile × oversized type / gesture × editorial layout]
Supporting text: [short phrase]

Build the poster around one strong photographic subject and one dominant graphic idea.

Use oversized typography as part of the composition rather than simple text placed around the image. Let letters interact with the subject through cropping, overlap, foreground/background layering, or scale contrast.

Depending on the selected direction:

• Perspective × typography — use a strong foreground gesture or body movement toward the camera, with dramatic near-far perspective and large type framing the action.

• Blur × sharp detail — keep parts of the portrait softly blurred while one narrow facial region remains extremely sharp, creating controlled focus contrast.

• Profile × oversized type — use a sculptural side-profile portrait against a bold flat-color background, with large serif typography partially hidden behind or crossing the silhouette.

• Gesture × editorial layout — combine an expressive fashion pose, subtle motion echo or secondary silhouette, and integrated magazine-style typography.

Keep the layout clean and visually bold. Use one dominant color, one neutral base, and limited accent colors. Typography should include one large headline, one short supporting line, and only a few small editorial labels.

Maintain realistic skin texture, natural facial anatomy, refined fashion styling, crisp fabric details, controlled studio lighting, strong negative space, and premium magazine-cover quality.
