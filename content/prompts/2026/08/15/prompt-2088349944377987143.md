## Prompt

A woman in an ornate gold brocade robe with dragon-cloud embroidery, hair swept into a tall sharp updo, dramatic makeup, both hands with red threads wound around her fingertips, pulling and manipulating them forward like a fate-weaving incantation — preserve her sharp, striking facial features, translucent porcelain skin, and original intense direct gaze with focused fingertip tension. Behind her, faintly emerging through the smoke, a mandala halo appears — formed of hairline golden filigree lines whose outer edges naturally merge and extend into the crimson threads controlled by her fingertips, as if the red strings she manipulates are the very radiating meridians of the mandala's sacred geometry. Red thread and dark gold filigree interweave into a concentric magic-circle pattern, dusted with fine cinnabar light particles, echoing the intricate ornamentation of Tibetan Thangka art.

The drifting smoke transforms into floating golden thread clouds (Tibetan auspicious clouds), coiling together with the red threads, forming a faint halo of light around her as if a barrier is materializing at her fingertips.
Background: the dim silhouette of ancient wooden beams and pillars remains, but the overall atmosphere deepens into shadow. Warm gold brocade and crimson thread resonate with the dark-gold mandala linework — the cold texture of smoke colliding with the solemn warmth of sacred geometry, a fusion aesthetic of "fate-thread manipulation" and "Tibetan tantric magic circle," commanding intensity meeting ritual solemnity, hyper-detailed, dramatic rim lighting, cinematic close-up composition, ultra-fine linework, 8k illustration.
