## Prompt

Sport / activity: [ ]
Subject: [adult man/woman, appearance and vibe]
Outfit: [ ]
Main action / pose: [ ]
Environment: [ ]
Gear / equipment: [ ]
Headline: [ ]
Subtitle: [ ]
Accent color: [ ]
Aspect ratio: 9:16

Create a 9:16 full-body editorial sports portrait poster combining realistic photography with scrapbook-style collage design.

Make the person the dominant visual, occupying around 75–85% of the poster height, with the full body and shoes visible. Use natural body proportions, realistic skin and hair, believable muscle tone, and a real sport-specific action rather than a stiff model pose.

Use authentic training environments and natural or realistic studio lighting. Clothing, skin, equipment, hands, and body mechanics should look physically believable.

Cut the full person out with a clearly visible irregular white sticker / paper border.

Use a clean off-white textured-paper background with a few torn-paper photos, tape, handwritten notes, arrows, and sport-specific details such as:

 equipment / gear
 movement or alignment details
 training environment
 route, rhythm, breathing, strength, or control notes

Keep the collage secondary to the portrait.

Add a large handwritten headline “[Headline]” and clean subtitle “[Subtitle]”. Use [Accent color] for selected typography, arrows, underlines, tape, and small graphic accents.

Final style: realistic sports portrait + workout identity + editorial scrapbook poster.
