## Prompt

Create a minimalist, premium vintage-style travel poster for [DESTINATION / CITY, COUNTRY], inspired by the visual language of a classic mid-century travel print.

Use a clean, sophisticated composition with a limited, harmonious color palette, subtle aged-paper texture, and a refined screen-printed / paper-cutout aesthetic. The overall artwork should feel like a collectible vintage travel poster rather than a modern digital illustration.

At the top, add elegant serif-style typography reading:

“[CITY]”
in a deep, sophisticated color.

Directly underneath, add:

“[COUNTRY]”
in a smaller complementary accent color.

Main Illustration

Create an instantly recognizable but highly stylized representation of [CITY], featuring [LANDMARK 1], [LANDMARK 2], [LANDMARK 3], and [ICONIC LOCAL ELEMENT / VEHICLE / CULTURAL SYMBOL].

Arrange the elements naturally within a simplified landscape featuring [RIVER / COASTLINE / MOUNTAINS / STREETS / CITY SKYLINE]. Add one or two subtle atmospheric details such as [SMALL BOAT / PEOPLE / BIRDS / TREES / VEHICLE], keeping the composition uncluttered.

Include a simple circular sun or moon positioned behind the skyline, with soft paper-cutout clouds or minimal atmospheric shapes.

Visual Style

Mid-century vintage travel poster aesthetic
Minimalist editorial composition
Flat geometric shapes
Clean, confident linework
Simplified architectural forms
Limited 4–6 color palette
Muted vintage tones with one strong accent color
Subtle paper grain and screen-print texture
Slightly imperfect ink registration
Layered paper-cutout appearance
Elegant art-deco influence
Strong visual hierarchy
Sophisticated, premium collectible-poster feeling
No photorealism
No excessive detail
No gradients or glossy digital effects
No modern 3D rendering

The final composition should feel timeless, artistic, elegant, nostalgic, and instantly identifiable as [CITY], while maintaining a restrained and sophisticated vintage travel-poster aesthetic.

Poster format: vertical 4:5, centered composition, generous negative space, high-end print quality.
