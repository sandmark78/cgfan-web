## Prompt

Create a premium 3D paper-cut diorama inspired by the Instagram logo, using the iconic rounded-square frame and central circular lens as the defining structural elements. The composition should represent {CITY} through a simplified, elegant miniature scene featuring only 3–6 of the city's most recognizable landmarks, architectural icons, and cultural elements. Avoid a dense cityscape. Arrange every element harmoniously with generous negative space, refined proportions, and a clean, sophisticated visual hierarchy.

CENTRAL CIRCULAR OPENING:
Inside the central circular opening, create a simplified scenic view of {CITY}—such as a distant skyline, coastline, mountain range, temple, bridge, monument, or iconic natural landscape. Keep the scene atmospheric and visually restrained, using soft perspective and subtle depth rather than excessive architectural detail.

MINIATURE TRAVELER:
Place a cute handcrafted traveler doll sitting naturally on the top-left edge of the Instagram-inspired frame. The traveler wears casual travel clothing, a woven hat, and holds a small camera, with a compact backpack resting beside them. The character should look like a premium knitted or clay miniature toy, featuring expressive eyes, charming proportions, tactile handcrafted textures, and subtle imperfections that reinforce the handmade aesthetic.

LOCAL DETAILS:
Add a restrained selection of small local elements around the landmarks—such as flowering trees, charming cafés, lanterns, cobblestone paths, vintage street lamps, landscaped gardens, regional vegetation, or subtle seasonal details. Keep these elements sparse and intentional so the composition remains minimal, balanced, and uncluttered.

BOTTOM PLAQUE:
At the bottom of the composition, include an elegant engraved plaque displaying "{CITY}" in refined serif lettering, accompanied by delicate decorative flourishes. The plaque should feel physically integrated into the paper sculpture, as though crafted from premium embossed cardstock or a miniature engraved material.

COLOR PALETTE:
Use a warm, sophisticated pastel palette consisting of cream, ivory, blush pink, peach, dusty rose, coral, terracotta, muted lavender, warm beige, soft taupe, honey brown, muted olive, sage green, and gentle slate gray. Avoid highly saturated, fluorescent, or neon colors. Use smooth tonal transitions, subtle gradients, and harmonious color relationships throughout.

LIGHTING & MATERIALS:
Soft warm ambient lighting with a subtle golden-hour glow. Realistic layered cardstock and premium paper textures, delicate shadows between individual layers, refined paper fibers, softly rounded edges, precise miniature architecture, subtle embossing, tactile handcrafted surfaces, realistic material separation, shallow depth of field, and gentle atmospheric falloff. The final result should feel like an exclusive luxury travel souvenir or collectible handcrafted paper sculpture.

COMPOSITION & STYLE:
Elegant, clean, simplified, uncluttered, premium collectible design, luxury travel keepsake, centered symmetrical composition, carefully balanced negative space, sophisticated miniature scale, high-quality 3D paper sculpture, photorealistic render, physically believable paper construction, Octane-quality rendering, ultra-detailed, 8K, Instagram-inspired visual identity.

STRICT CONSTRAINTS:
No dense cityscape, no excessive landmarks, no visual clutter, no extra text beyond "{CITY}", no watermarks, no logos other than the Instagram-inspired structural frame, and no people other than the single miniature traveler. Preserve the rounded-square Instagram-inspired frame and central circular opening as core elements. Aspect ratio: 4:5.
