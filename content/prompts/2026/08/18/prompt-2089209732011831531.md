## Prompt

FORMAT. 3:4 vertical travel poster - handmade risograph screenprint and cut-paper collage, scanned from physical paper.

PRINT. Warm cream fiber paper (#F1E7D6), visible paper tooth and one subtle fold crease. Paper is the lightest value; no white ink. Use three plates only: black, pale sea teal, and coral. Plate overlaps create the only additional hues. Keep black coverage under 35%.

TYPE. [CITY LANDMARK / TITLE], ultra-condensed heavy grotesque uppercase lettering, arranged in two stacked lines bleeding slightly past both side edges and filling approximately 65% of the frame height. The second line is wider than the first. Black lettering with a 3mm coral misregistration ghost.

FIGURE. Young adult woman, early twenties, wind-lifted hair, standing in the defining pedestrian/public area of the destination. Three-quarter view, turned back over her shoulder, with one hand holding her hair away from her face. She stands in front of the typography and partially covers it — letterforms are cut off at the figure, while the top third of every letter remains clear. Skin rendered as dense black halftone photo-transfer with varying dot density and visible paper showing through the highlights. Hand-cut paper silhouette with a 4mm bare-paper margin and a thin teal offset outline.

COLLAGE FILLS. Clothing and accessories use glued-paper textures with torn edges. Jacket or windbreaker: coral cross-hatch weave. Trousers: pale teal cloud-print. Add a distinctive local visual fragment behind her shoulder, plus a torn kraft-paper scrap attached to or near the bag.

EYE BAND. Flat semi-opaque coral rectangle across her eyes, slightly wider than her face. Eyes remain visible underneath as dark halftone. Edges should be slightly imperfect and physically printed.
