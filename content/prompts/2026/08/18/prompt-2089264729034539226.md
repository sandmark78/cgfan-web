## Prompt

A solitary biomechanical woman in elegant side profile, preserving the intricate skeletal anatomy, exposed vertebral structures, organic mechanical joints, layered ivory bone textures, fine cables, hair strands, facial features, proportions and original pose.

Her formerly black mechanical arm and shoulder plating are transformed into deep cobalt-blue cloisonné enamel, almost black in the shadows yet subtly luminous from within — a profound mineral blue inspired by traditional Tibetan azurite pigment, with polished enamel depth, restrained internal blue radiance and delicate metallic edging. The blue must remain dark, sophisticated and low-key, never becoming bright neon or conventional sci-fi blue.

Her remaining biomechanical body retains its aged ivory-white bone and pale metallic structure, creating a strong material contrast between skeletal organic machinery and cobalt enamel armour.

Behind her floats an enormous, impossibly intricate Thangka-inspired mandala halo, constructed from concentric layers of hairline antique-gold filigree and sacred geometry. The geometry is extraordinarily dense, approaching the edge of visual overload while remaining elegant and readable. Tiny sections dissolve into bioluminescent golden dust.

The surrounding environment exists within a pitch-black sacred void, filled with extremely intricate Tibetan-inspired visual fragments: floating golden thread clouds, faint mountainous silhouettes, barely visible monastery architecture, microscopic ornamental patterns, celestial diagrams and fragmented sacred geometry disappearing into darkness.

Introduce a restrained palette derived from traditional Tibetan mineral pigments — weathered azurite blue, muted malachite turquoise-green, aged cinnabar red, tarnished mineral gold and charcoal-black — all heavily desaturated, darkened and partially swallowed by shadow. No bright primary colours.
