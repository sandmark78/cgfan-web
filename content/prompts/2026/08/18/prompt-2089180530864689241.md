## Prompt

Create a premium 4:5 vertical artistic travel poster for [CITY], [COUNTRY], based on the concept “City as a Neon Ribbon.”

Use a deep matte-black background with dramatic empty negative space. Build the entire composition from expressive hand-painted neon strokes, ink splashes, dry-brush textures, and energetic gestural lines.

A single continuous luminous brushstroke should flow across the poster like a ribbon, twisting and transforming into the most iconic landmark of [CITY]. The landmark should feel partially painted and partially emerging from the glowing stroke, creating a surreal connection between typography and architecture.

Integrate the city name “[CITY]” in huge custom hand-painted lettering, but make the letters interact with the ribbon rather than simply sitting on top of the artwork. Some letters can stretch, break apart, overlap, or dissolve into colorful paint trails.

Surround the main landmark with a few subtle visual symbols of the city: [LANDMARK 2], [LOCAL TRANSPORT], [CULTURAL ELEMENT], [FOOD/OBJECT], [NATURAL ELEMENT] — represented only as simplified neon silhouettes or expressive brush marks.

Color palette: electric pink, hot magenta, cobalt blue, cyan, violet, orange, and acid yellow against pure black. Strong fluorescent glow, rough dry-brush edges, visible paint texture, ink splatters, imperfect handmade strokes, energetic contemporary street-art aesthetic.

Add one small handwritten phrase such as “CITY NIGHTS”, “WHERE THE CITY GLOWS”, or “FEEL THE CITY” in a delicate contrasting script.

Composition should feel bold, sophisticated, youthful, collectible, and editorial — like a limited-edition contemporary travel poster rather than a generic tourist illustration.

No photorealism, no ordinary skyline collage, no generic clip-art, no excessive details. Keep the artwork graphic, dramatic, recognizable, and highly stylized.
