## Prompt

Create a bold editorial campaign poster where oversized typography becomes a functional mechanism inside the composition.

Brand: [BRAND NAME]
Product / Campaign: [PRODUCT OR CAMPAIGN]
Main Word: [SHORT WORD]
Mechanism: [FLUID FLOW / POWER TRANSMISSION / OPTICAL PATH / MECHANICAL MOVEMENT / OTHER]
Subject / Product: [PERSON / PRODUCT / BOTH]
Main Color: [COLOR]
Accent Color: [COLOR]
Campaign Details: [SLOGAN / SPECS / DATE]
Aspect Ratio: [9:16]

Make the main word extremely large, filling around 60–80% of the poster height. The typography should not just sit on the page — it should physically interact with the subject and visibly perform a mechanical function:

1. POUR — Let liquid, powder, or light flow through the letterforms as if they were channels or pipes
2. GEAR — Turn the letters into cogs, pulleys, or transmission systems that transfer energy
3. BEAM — Use the type as a lens or prism that bends, focuses, or splits light
4. TICK — Build clockwork, pistons, or kinetic movement inside the letterforms

Use realistic commercial photography, oversized typography, strong high-contrast colors, and contemporary editorial design.

Make the typography feel engineered, not decorative. Every letter should have a clear mechanical purpose visible in the composition.

Visual style: bold editorial campaign, functional typography, mechanical interaction, high-contrast photography, contemporary design, engineered letterforms.
