## Prompt

Create a bold editorial campaign poster where oversized typography works like an optical tool and visibly changes the subject or scene.

Brand: [BRAND NAME]
Product / Campaign: [PRODUCT OR CAMPAIGN]
Main Word: [SHORT WORD]
Subject: [PERSON / PRODUCT / BOTH]
Optical Effect: [FOCUS / ZOOM / REFRACT / PRISM / OTHER]
Main Color: [COLOR]
Accent Color: [COLOR]
Aspect Ratio: [9:16]

Use realistic commercial photography, oversized typography, strong high-contrast colors, and contemporary editorial design.

Make the main word extremely large and let it perform a clear optical function:

1. FOCUS — Shift from blur to sharp through the letterforms
2. REFRACT — Bend the image through the type as if through glass
3. ZOOM — Turn type into a magnifying lens
4. PRISM — Split light through the letters into rainbow components

The typography should not just sit on the page — it should physically interact with light and the subject, creating visible optical distortions, refractions, or focus shifts.

Visual style: bold editorial campaign, optical typography, high-contrast photography, contemporary design, experimental letterforms.
