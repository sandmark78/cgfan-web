## Prompt

Create an original vertical 4:5 vintage travel-poster illustration for [LOCATION], designed as part of a cohesive collectible travel series.

Composition: Use a warm aged-cream/parchment background with subtle paper grain and generous negative space. Keep the layout clean and premium: large destination caption at the upper left, an oversized antique suitcase dominating the lower center/right, a circular destination stamp on the left, a tiny traveler beside the suitcase, and aged luggage tags in the foreground.

Typography: At the upper left, render exactly:
"I PACKED [LOCATION]."
Make "I PACKED" smaller and "[LOCATION]" dramatically larger, using chunky mid-century hand-printed lettering with distressed edges, worn ink texture, and a muted destination-appropriate color. Beneath it, add:
"MEMORIES TO GO"
in smaller dark hand-lettered type with a short rough underline.

Hero Object: Feature a large open antique leather suitcase with aged brown leather, worn edges, antique brass clasps, hinges, locks, and handle. Inside, create a highly detailed handcrafted miniature diorama of [LOCATION], showcasing its most recognizable landmarks, architecture, landscapes, vegetation, food, and/or transportation. Everything should appear handmade from painted clay, resin, wood, paper, and miniature foliage. The suitcase lid should contain an aged vintage map of [LOCATION] with a small retro postcard or travel sticker.

Traveler: Place one small handcrafted traveler figurine beside the lower-right side of the suitcase, mostly from behind or in profile, looking into the miniature world. Give them rounded collectible-toy proportions, an oversized head, small body, backpack, destination-appropriate clothing, and practical footwear. Keep the traveler clearly secondary to the suitcase.

Details: Add a slightly faded circular vintage rubber travel stamp below the headline on the front. Scatter 2–3 aged luggage tags in the foreground with destination-appropriate colors and tiny hand-lettered labels. Use subtle warm directional light from the upper left, soft shadows under the suitcase and traveler, and a gentle paper-grain overlay across the entire composition.

Visual style: handcrafted miniature diorama, vintage travel poster, mid-century lettering, warm aged parchment, collectible toy figurine, premium editorial layout, tactile paper texture, museum-quality craftsmanship.
