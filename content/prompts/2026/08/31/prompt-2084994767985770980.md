## Prompt

Create a 9:16 premium food advertising poster for a French pâtisserie brand.

Brand: [BRAND NAME]
Dessert: [DESSERT NAME]
Headline: [MAIN TITLE]
Tagline: [3 SHORT ADJECTIVES]
Description: [ONE-SENTENCE PRODUCT DESCRIPTION]
Ingredients: [4 KEY INGREDIENTS]
Craft details: [3 SHORT SELLING POINTS]
Brand line: [SHORT BRAND SLOGAN]
Palette: [MAIN COLORS]
Props: [2–3 RELEVANT PROPS]

Use a refined editorial layout with the branding and headline in the upper section, one hero dessert in the lower-middle section, and a small ingredient and craftsmanship panel near the bottom.

Photograph the dessert from a slightly elevated 35–45° angle. Keep the product realistic, appetizing, clearly structured, and visually dominant. Use natural side light, soft botanical or window shadows, warm textured plaster walls, stone or ceramic surfaces, and restrained styling.

Combine elegant serif typography, a small handwritten accent, and clean sans-serif supporting text. Maintain generous negative space, clear hierarchy, accurate readable text, and a cohesive premium restaurant-brand aesthetic.

Use one dominant product, 2–3 supporting props, and a muted warm palette. Avoid cluttered layouts, artificial lighting, plastic-looking food, overly saturated colors, and generic stock-photo styling.

Visual style: premium food photography, editorial layout, elegant typography, warm natural light, artisan craftsmanship, French pâtisserie aesthetic.
