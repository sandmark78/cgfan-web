## Prompt

Create a sophisticated luxury travel-art poster inspired by vintage world atlas packaging and premium collectible design, featuring [COUNTRY NAME] as the unmistakable central subject.

Use a deep charcoal-black / midnight navy textured background with subtle paper grain and faint antique cartographic contour lines. Place a large, recognizable silhouette of [COUNTRY NAME] in the center, rendered as an intricate three-dimensional hammered gold-leaf relief.

Instead of a flat map, fill the country's silhouette with delicate natural and cultural textures inspired by the destination — pressed leaves, botanical veins, traditional patterns, landscapes, architectural fragments, and subtle handcrafted details, all appearing as if sculpted and embossed into aged gold.

Add 3–5 elegant golden location pins at important regions or cities within the country. Each pin should look like a tiny raised enamel-and-gold map marker with a subtle glowing center.

Around the country, add a few thin curved/dashed golden travel routes, creating the feeling of an exploratory journey. Keep them graceful and sparse rather than cluttered.

Add sophisticated small editorial typography around the map:

[COUNTRY NAME]
A journey through [short poetic phrase]
2–4 small destination names
Tiny secondary descriptive phrases

Use a refined vintage geometric sans-serif typeface, with all typography in muted antique gold, cream, and subtle bronze.

At the bottom, create a premium collection title such as:

DISCOVER
[COUNTRY NAME]
WORLD SERIES

Add tiny luxury-product-style micro typography beneath it, giving the artwork the appearance of a rare collectible travel box, heritage atlas, or premium international discovery series.

Visual style: tactile gold leaf, embossed relief, antique cartography, luxury packaging design, museum-quality craftsmanship, subtle metallic reflections, sophisticated editorial composition.
