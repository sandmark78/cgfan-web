## Prompt

Create a Cannes-level luxury fashion editorial poster for an original couture brand named "ROUGE FIL". Use 100% live-action ultra-realistic photography. Preserve the exact structural logic of a tall vertical Paris fashion composition with one adult female model as the absolute visual hero in the foreground and the Eiffel Tower as a large softly blurred architectural presence behind her. The image must feel iconic, sculptural, collectible, and unmistakably brand-owned.

Style lock: ultra-real luxury fashion photography, Paris couture editorial, shallow-depth-of-field portraiture, soft overcast daylight, premium European magazine campaign, no illustration, no CGI look, no plastic beauty retouching.

Port logic: make the brand super-symbol the fusion of three elements into one unforgettable signature image: a deep crimson couture knit gown, a refined adult woman in poised side-facing elegance, and a single continuous red thread visually binding the dress to Paris through the Eiffel Tower. This red thread must not feel decorative; it must act as the core symbolic device of the campaign, transforming the dress into a living extension of the city.

Core composition: full-body or three-quarter female model centered in the lower two-thirds, photographed at a slight low angle, side-facing or three-quarter facing pose with poised elegance. The Eiffel Tower appears large but softly blurred in the upper third, centered or slightly off-center behind the model.

The red thread extends from the gown hem into the lower frame and visually connects toward the tower, creating a continuous line from garment to landmark.

Lighting: soft overcast Paris daylight, even and diffused, wrapping the model's form with gentle shadows. The sky should be a pale gray-blue typical of Paris.

Color palette: deep crimson as the dominant color from the gown, warm skin tones, pale gray-blue sky, and the iron-gray of the Eiffel Tower. The red must feel singular and powerful against the muted background.

Final image must feel like an iconic fashion poster — sculptural, elegant, and instantly recognizable as a brand statement.
