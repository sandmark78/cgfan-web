## Prompt

Create a Cannes-level luxury fashion editorial image for an original womenswear brand named "SOLARA". Use 100% live-action ultra-realistic photography. Preserve the exact structural logic of the scene: a tall vertical composition inside a dense citrus orchard corridor, one adult female model walking or gliding forward through the center path as the absolute visual hero, surrounded tightly on both sides by lush orange trees loaded with ripe fruit, with a deep teal-blue sky above and strong natural sunlight casting dramatic leaf shadows across her body and dress. The image must feel like a high-fashion magazine cover without any visible text: bold, elegant, sensual, Mediterranean, and unforgettable.

Style lock: ultra-real luxury fashion editorial photography, premium magazine-cover energy, natural outdoor couture campaign, vivid sunlit realism, no illustration, no CGI look, no plastic beauty retouching, no artificial fantasy rendering.

Core composition: full-body female model centered slightly forward in the frame, photographed at a slightly low-to-mid angle, walking or pausing mid-step through a narrow orchard path. Trees and fruit must form a natural vertical tunnel around her, framing the figure symmetrically but organically. The dress occupies a large portion of the lower frame with strong fabric presence.

Lighting: strong natural sunlight from above and slightly behind, casting dramatic dappled leaf shadows across the model's body, face, and dress. The leaf shadows should create a broken, organic pattern across the fabric and skin. The sky visible through the canopy should be a deep teal-blue, contrasting with the warm oranges and greens.

Color palette: vivid warm oranges from fruit, deep greens from foliage, teal-blue sky, and the dress in a bold orange-red that picks up the citrus tones. Skin should be naturally sun-kissed with realistic texture.

Final image must feel like a page from Vogue or Harper's Bazaar — editorial, cinematic, and collectible.
