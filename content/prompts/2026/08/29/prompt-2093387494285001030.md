## Prompt

Create a Cannes-level luxury fashion campaign image for an original womenswear brand named "SERRE LUMIERE". Use a 100% live-action ultra-realistic editorial photography style. Preserve the exact structural logic of the original composition: a tall vertical poster inside a humid vintage glass greenhouse, a low upward camera angle, one adult female model seated high on a weathered wooden ladder at the center, surrounded by large tropical leaves and dense botanical growth, with warm golden sunlight entering diagonally through the glass roof and soft atmospheric mist filling the space. The image must feel ethereal, sensual, elegant, botanical, and highly cinematic.

Style lock: ultra-real luxury fashion editorial photography, greenhouse couture lookbook, soft cinematic realism, naturalistic skin rendering, premium European fashion campaign, no illustration, no CGI look, no plastic beauty retouching.

Orbit logic: strengthen the visual circulation throughout the frame so the eye moves in one elegant loop from the sunbeams in the upper-right glass roof, across the model's illuminated face and collarbone, down the corseted bodice, along the ruffled hem, to the shoes, and back up through the ladder and leaves.

Core composition: full-body or three-quarter female model seated high on a weathered wooden ladder, photographed from a low upward angle. The ladder acts as the vertical spine of the composition. Large tropical leaves frame the model on all sides.

Lighting: warm golden sunlight entering diagonally through the glass roof from the upper right, creating visible sunbeams through the atmospheric mist. The light crosses the model's face, bodice, ruffled hem, and shoes, creating a guided viewing path.

Color palette: warm golden sunlight, deep greens from tropical leaves, weathered wood browns, and the dress in a soft ivory or blush tone that catches the light.

Final image must feel like a page from a luxury fashion magazine — ethereal, cinematic, and collectible.
