## Prompt

Character / role: [young adult character + profession]
World / mission: [fictional story or event]
English title: [title]
Main Japanese headline: [natural Japanese title]
Character action: [dynamic in-between moment]
Outfit: [uniform / racing suit / flight suit / field gear]
Palette: [3–4 coordinated colors]
Archive image: [secondary character shot]
Scene image: [mission / vehicle / environment]
Aspect ratio: 3:4

Create a 3:4 retro Japanese character magazine poster inspired by late-1990s to early-2000s editorial design.

Use one large photorealistic character as the dominant visual, occupying around 55–70% of the page. Capture the character in a real moment of movement rather than a static model pose: walking, turning, removing a helmet, reacting to something, or interacting with equipment. Keep the face sharp, youthful and expressive, with realistic clean skin, natural hair movement and believable clothing materials.

Build the layout around a huge English masthead, oversized vertical Japanese typography, bold secondary headlines, small editorial text, 1–2 character archive inserts, and one environmental or mission image near the bottom. Let the character overlap and partially cover the typography so the person feels integrated into the page.

Use Japanese + English only. Japanese should read naturally rather than look like random Chinese characters. Avoid meaningless placeholder text.

Keep the composition dense but controlled, with clear hierarchy between oversized type, medium headlines and small archive information. Use a restrained 3–4 color system that matches the character outfit and story. Keep the background neutral or slightly cool rather than heavily yellowed.

The final image should feel like a real lost Japanese character magazine cover from a fictional universe — story-driven, dynamic, tactile and highly collectible.
