## Prompt

ASPECT RATIO: 4:5

[DESTINATION] = [CITY / COUNTRY NAME]

Create a bold, sophisticated vertical 4:5 travel art poster for [DESTINATION], drawn entirely in a refined modernist hand-illustrated style. The design should feel like a premium, gallery-quality travel print — elegant, confident, and timeless.

STYLE & AESTHETIC
- Hand-drawn illustration with clean, confident linework and subtle texture (think vintage travel posters reimagined with a contemporary minimalist sensibility).
- Color palette: rich, destination-inspired tones (e.g., warm terracotta, deep ocean blue, sun-bleached sand, or lush forest green) balanced with neutral creams and soft off-whites. Avoid oversaturation — aim for a curated, editorial feel.
- Typography: elegant serif or geometric sans-serif for the destination name, paired with refined supporting text. Lettering should feel intentional and harmonious with the illustration.

COMPOSITION
- Focal point: a single iconic landmark, natural feature, or cultural motif of [DESTINATION], stylized with artistic simplification (not photorealistic).
- Background: subtle atmospheric elements (e.g., distant mountains, cityscape silhouette, or abstract patterns) that evoke the location's mood without cluttering the frame.
- Foreground: minimal decorative accents (e.g., local flora, geometric shapes, or subtle textures) to add depth and visual interest.
- Layout: balanced and breathable, with clear hierarchy between illustration, typography, and negative space.

MOOD & TONE
- Evokes wanderlust, curiosity, and a sense of refined adventure.
- Should feel both aspirational and approachable — like a poster you'd proudly display in a boutique hotel or modern home.
- Avoid clichés or overly touristy imagery; instead, capture the essence of [DESTINATION] through thoughtful artistic interpretation.

TECHNICAL DETAILS
- High-resolution, print-ready quality.
- No watermarks, logos, or distracting elements.
- The poster should work as a standalone piece of art, not just a promotional item.

DELIVERABLE
A single, cohesive 4:5 vertical poster design that celebrates [DESTINATION] with sophistication, artistry, and timeless appeal.
