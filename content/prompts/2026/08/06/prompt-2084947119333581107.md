# 和洋混搭幻想衣装：日式奇幻角色设计

**作者**: 鈴音🔔  
**日期**: 2026-08-05  
**评分**: 61/80

## Prompt

```
Overall: A Japanese-inspired fantasy ensemble with an ivory wrap-style bodice, a short burgundy pleated skirt, an asymmetrical printed panel, and a long open robe with wide kimono sleeves.

Upper body: The ivory bodice has a shallow V neckline, diagonal seams, and a obi-like sash tied at the waist. The fabric has a subtle linen texture with visible weave.

Lower body: The burgundy pleated skirt falls to mid-thigh, with knife pleats that create movement. An asymmetrical printed panel wraps around the hip, featuring traditional Japanese motifs like waves, clouds, and cherry blossoms in gold and indigo.

Outer layer: The long open robe has wide kimono sleeves that extend beyond the hands. The fabric is a sheer, lightweight material with a subtle gradient from ivory to pale gold. The inner lining shows a contrasting pattern of pine trees and cranes.

Accessories: A thin gold chain belt with small bells hangs from the obi. Hair ornaments include cherry blossom pins and a thin gold circlet. Footwear is simple wooden geta with red straps.

Color palette: Ivory, burgundy, gold, indigo, with accents of cherry blossom pink.

Style: Anime-inspired character design with traditional Japanese elements blended with fantasy aesthetics. The overall look should feel elegant, mysterious, and slightly otherworldly.
```
