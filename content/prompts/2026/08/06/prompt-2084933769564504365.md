# 烤串美食海报：炭火烧烤品牌视觉

**评分**: 68/80

## Prompt

```
Create a world-class premium Chinese barbecue skewer campaign poster for a self-created luxury dining brand called "炙焰签馆", designed as an official flagship restaurant campaign image that merges stronger food realism, a more complete brand world, and a more mature high-end barbecue identity system. The image must fully preserve the exact high-impact visual logic of a premium skewer poster: one realistic human hand entering from the upper area holding multiple bamboo skewers, the skewers fanning downward into the center and lower portion of the frame, a dense and elegant storm of chili powder and cumin dust surrounding the meat, a large Chinese title block dominating the right side, a slim official logo area in the upper-left, and a small refined campaign label in the lower-left. The final artwork must feel more official, more luxurious, more appetizing, more culturally elevated, and more like a mature high-end Chinese barbecue brand campaign, while keeping the skewers as the absolute hero product.

Core composition:
Use a tall vertical poster with a pure black-charcoal background and subtle ember-red warmth near the lower area. In the upper-center to upper-left area, place one realistic hand gripping several bamboo skewers with natural pressure and elegant anatomy. The skewers must fan outward and descend diagonally into the center and lower-middle of the frame, with the grilled meat occupying most of the visual weight. Around the hand and skewers, create a dramatic but controlled cloud of falling chili powder, cumin dust, toasted spice particles, and fine oil mist. The right side must carry a bold Chinese title composition with strong official visual authority, while the left side keeps a restrained brand mark and a small standardized descriptor system.

Brand direction:
Reinvent the visual as the flagship campaign for a luxury Chinese barbecue brand called "炙焰签馆". The poster must communicate charcoal-fire craftsmanship, spice precision, premium lamb selection, flame aroma, and the elevation of Chinese street-skewer culture into a sophisticated modern restaurant brand. The entire page must feel like a seasonal signature launch from a mature premium grill house, not a casual food flyer.

Master title system:
Use Chinese as the dominant title language with restrained English support. Create a premium title structure such as:
炙焰签馆
炭火羊肉串
Add a minimal English subtitle:
ZHIYAN SKEWER HOUSE
Signature Charcoal Lamb Skewers

Also integrate a mature brand-system field set in elegant English:
Series Name: Ember Fire Collection
Signature Dish: Charcoal Lamb Skewers
Flavor Note: chili heat, cumin aroma, roasted juiciness
House Selection: Night Fire Signature
Brand Seal: Zhiyan Skewer House

Typography must feel official, bold, premium, and highly art-directed. The Chinese headline should dominate the right side with expressive but controlled brush-calligraphy energy. English support should remain thin, minimal, and international. All small fields must feel like a real premium restaurant campaign system, not decorative captions.

Hero food:
Show a set of premium charcoal-grilled lamb skewers as the main signature dish, with subtle variety in fat ratio, cut size, and roasting level across the skewers. The meat must be extremely realistic: caramelized edges, rendered fat sheen, charred blisters, cumin granules, chili powder adhesion, juicy fiber separation, slight smoke softness, and believable roasted color variation from deep amber to charred mahogany. The skewers must feel freshly lifted from live fire, physically hot, aromatic, and richly seasoned.

Orbit-level food realism:
Push the realism of the meat much further. Strengthen char-edge crispness, fat translucency, oil highlights, cumin seeds, chili powder cling, grilled fiber separation, and slightly uneven roasted color variation. Emphasize the roasted tension where lean meat and fat meet, so the skewers feel truly fire-kissed and intensely appetizing. Add only the faintest heat shimmer and smoke softness where useful, while keeping the whole image clean and premium.

Hand realism:
The hand must be fully real and de-AI: correct finger anatomy, believable grip pressure on the bamboo sticks, natural knuckle folds, subtle skin texture, realistic fingernails, and warm directional highlights. The gesture should feel confident and intentional, like a grill master presenting the house signature skewers. No distortion, no awkward grip, no broken anatomy.

Spice atmosphere:
Build a cinematic spice storm around the skewers using suspended red chili powder, cumin dust, toasted spice particles, and tiny droplets of seasoned oil. The spice cloud must feel energetic but elegant, dense but controlled, amplifying heat and appetite without turning messy. Add a subtle layer of falling spice over the lower surface and near the meat edges.

Port-level brand completion:
Elevate the entire poster into a complete flagship visual for an upscale barbecue brand. Add a subtle official brand seal, collection naming, and a unified campaign identity that ties the whole composition together. The image should feel like the hero poster of a full seasonal launch for 炙焰签馆, with the same language and discipline a luxury dining group would use in official advertising. The supporting fields should read like a coherent brand system rather than scattered notes.

Supporting campaign label:
In the lower-left, add a small refined campaign CTA-style label such as:
Chef's Signature Release
or
Reserve the Fire Tasting
Keep it subtle, premium, and beautifully integrated into the page.

Background and staging:
Keep the background pure and minimal, black-charcoal with faint ember warmth below. The lower surface may be a dark grill plate or abstract spice-dusted stage with subtle texture, but must remain clean and premium. No restaurant interior, no plates, no casual table clutter, no excessive props.

Lighting:
Use extremely refined cinematic food lighting with warm directional glow from the lower-right and soft shadow control from the upper-left. The meat must show rich oil highlights, spice texture, char contours, and dimensional volume. The spice dust should catch light selectively in glowing red-orange particles. The overall lighting must feel dramatic, expensive, and highly appetizing.

Material realism:
Render every element with master-level realism: grilled meat fibers, fat translucency, char crust, cumin particles, chili dust, bamboo skewer grain, skin texture, oil mist, and subtle smoke softness. The final image must feel physically believable, de-AI, and commercially powerful.

Color direction:
Use a premium palette of charcoal black, ember red, chili crimson, toasted cumin brown, roasted amber, warm copper highlights, and restrained ivory typography. Keep the whole poster dark, saturated, elegant, and brand-complete.

Commercial goal:
The final artwork must feel like an award-level official Chinese barbecue brand campaign: intensely appetizing, visually explosive, culturally elevated, highly premium, and commercially unforgettable. Preserve the exact hand-held-skewer, spice-burst, dark-background, bold-Chinese-title poster logic while elevating it into a more official, more luxurious, and more mature high-end barbecue campaign image.
```
