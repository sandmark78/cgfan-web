## Prompt

```
Create a playful and unexpected black-and-white logo for an original fictional brand.

Use a pure white background and design one compact mascot symbol with a bold silhouette, clear negative space, and a small visual trick that becomes noticeable on a second look.

Combine two ideas into one inseparable mark, such as:
- a creature passing through an object
- a character hiding inside a folded structure
- an animal fused with a letter or everyday item
- a speech bubble transformed into a mascot

Add a few restrained details such as thin structural lines, small dots, short hatching, clipped corners, asymmetrical eyes, or localized halftone texture. Keep the details subtle and purposeful rather than decorative.

The character should feel odd, witty, slightly awkward, and full of personality—not childish, overly cute, or perfectly symmetrical.

Place an original English brand name below the symbol. Customize one or two letters so the wordmark subtly echoes the icon, while remaining clear and readable.

1:1 square composition, centered logo, approximately 45%–55% of the canvas width, with generous white space.
```
