## Prompt

Create a sophisticated minimalist art poster inspired by [COUNTRY NAME].

Use the recognizable silhouette/outline of [COUNTRY NAME] as the central visual element, but transform it into an elegant piece of contemporary editorial artwork rather than a conventional map.

Inside the country silhouette, create a delicate miniature world using only a few carefully selected iconic elements from the country — one famous landmark, a distinctive landscape, a cultural object, a native plant or flower, and one subtle local symbol. Arrange them with generous negative space so the composition feels calm, intentional, and luxurious.

Use a warm ivory paper background with subtle natural paper grain. Draw everything with refined hand-drawn ink lines, imperfect organic contours, and delicate illustration details. Add only 2–3 muted accent colors inspired by the country's visual identity, keeping the overall palette restrained and sophisticated.

Outside the silhouette, leave almost completely empty space.

At the bottom, place the country name in elegant minimalist typography, with a tiny understated subtitle such as:
"a little world within"

Style: contemporary museum poster, Scandinavian minimalism, Japanese editorial illustration, luxury travel stationery, refined hand-drawn artwork, subtle vintage texture, elegant composition, sophisticated negative space, delicate linework, premium art print, timeless aesthetic.

No clutter, no photorealism, no excessive text, no flags, no generic tourist collage, no heavy borders.

Vertical poster composition, 4:5 aspect ratio, highly detailed but visually minimal.
