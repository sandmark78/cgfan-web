## Prompt

Turned four summer drinks into full fashion campaigns!

Oversized products, fresh ingredients, glossy typography, and four different poses:

1. MINT GLOW — lime and mint sparkle
2. PEACH DRIFT — white peach oolong
3. BERRY MOOD — berry yogurt smoothie
4. CITRUS POP — grapefruit and orange blossom fizz

One visual system. Four flavors. Four different moods.

Create giant fashion product posters: oversized products treated like luxury fashion items, with fresh ingredients, glossy typography, and dramatic lighting. Each drink gets its own mood and color story while maintaining a cohesive visual system.
