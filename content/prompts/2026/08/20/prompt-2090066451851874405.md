## Prompt

Turn type into architecture, and the poster comes alive!

A visual system built from concrete, glass, metal, shadow, and space.

1. P — Curve: The letter P becomes a curved architectural form, with the bowl of the P creating a sweeping concrete curve.
2. Y — Intersect: The letter Y becomes an intersection of architectural planes, with the fork creating a branching spatial junction.
3. T — Fold: The letter T becomes a folded plate structure, with the horizontal bar creating a cantilevered overhang.
4. E — Layers: The letter E becomes a layered section model, with each horizontal bar representing a different floor plate.

Great for typography posters, editorial design, exhibition visuals, and branding!
