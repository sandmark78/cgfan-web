## Prompt

```
I love working on these ideas. They are a lot of fun. This one is for License Plates as tiny road trips 

  S0: input(COUNTRY, REGION, DECADE)
  S1: analyze_road_culture -> extract architectural styles, vehicle types, signage, landscape palettes, roadside business typology
        - transportation geography
        - vernacular architecture
        - regional color grading
  S2: compose_license_plate -> generate a vintage-style license plate with:
        - embossed metal texture
        - faded paint and rust details
        - state/region name and slogan
        - registration number format matching the decade
  S3: build_miniature_scene -> create a 3D diorama inside the plate frame:
        - tiny roadside diner, gas station, or motel
        - period-correct vehicles (cars, trucks, motorcycles)
        - landscape elements (desert, mountains, coast, forest)
        - signage with regional typography
  S4: render -> isometric view, soft natural lighting, shallow depth of field, tilt-shift effect to enhance miniature feel

Output: 2x2 grid showing 4 different regional license plate dioramas
```
