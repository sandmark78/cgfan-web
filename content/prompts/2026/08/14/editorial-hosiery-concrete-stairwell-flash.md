## Prompt

Create a realistic editorial hosiery portrait in a raw concrete stairwell.

Variables:
Model: [style]
Outfit: [top + bottom]
Hosiery: [color + opacity + pattern]
High heels: [style + color]

An adult female model sits on the edge of a higher stair, torso slightly turned and leaning back, one hand supporting her body and the other near her waist.

Use a strong, elongated pose: extend the leg closest to the camera far forward from the hip to create a dramatic diagonal line, with the second leg naturally bent and crossed behind it. Keep both heels fully visible.

Shoot vertically at 9:16 from a low stair-level camera angle. Let the legs dominate the lower frame while leaving large areas of raw concrete wall as negative space.

Use strong on-camera direct flash, flash-dominant exposure, bright flash-lit skin and legs, darker surroundings, hard shadows, visible concrete texture, and a raw editorial snapshot feel.

Keep the hosiery color, transparency, seams or patterns clearly readable and realistic. Elegant styling, natural skin texture, correct anatomy, refined feminine beauty against rough architecture.

Avoid soft studio lighting, ordinary seated poses, weak leg perspective, excessive hosiery shine, plastic skin, distorted legs or heels.
