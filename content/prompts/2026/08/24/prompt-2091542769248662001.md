## Prompt

Use the supplied image as the absolute authority for the character’s identity, species, anatomy, proportions, facial structure, silhouette, hairstyle/head shape, gender presentation, expression, markings, colors, materials, motifs, accessories, prosthetics, cybernetics, masks, and other distinctive traits. Preserve unmistakable recognizability. If an eye is hidden or covered in the reference, keep it hidden.

Extend the character’s palette, symbols, materials, geometry, patterns, cultural/technological cues, and design language across the entire image, including clothing, architecture, signage, lighting, and atmosphere. The background must be uniquely designed for this character, never generic.

Create a dramatic mid/upper-thigh-to-head editorial portrait in a monumental brutalist sci-fi environment. Use a slightly low viewpoint with a controlled 10–18° Dutch angle. The pose should feel sensual, confident, and provocative, but do not prescribe one exact pose. Keep the face, torso, and silhouette clearly readable. Do not show feet.

The character is directly exposed to heavy rain. Show visible rainfall, droplets, wet skin, damp hair/materials, runoff, mist, puddles, reflective architecture, and backlit rain streaks. Rain must feel physically integrated into the scene.

The character must be unmistakably adult. Keep the image tasteful and non-explicit, with all intimate areas fully covered.

Female

* Wears a narrow, semi-sheer bandeau top.
* Wear extremely low-rise, loose oversized baggy, unbuttoned pants derived from the character’s design language.
* Keep the styling daring but fully covered.

Male

* Topless, unless a permanent identity-defining torso element must remain.
* Wear extremely low-rise, loose oversized baggy, unbuttoned pants derived from the character’s design language.
* Keep the styling daring but fully covered.

Preserve permanent identity-defining garments, armor, masks, markings, jewelry, prosthetics, cybernetics, and non-human features where they conflict with styling changes.

Create a vast character-specific brutalist sci-fi environment of slabs, suspended bridges, shafts, stairs, platforms, machinery, signage, and distant structures. Echo the character’s shapes, symbols, materials, colors, anatomy, armor, or cultural motifs throughout the architecture. Use a much brighter exterior or distant space for strong graphic backlighting.

Render as a hand-inked European sci-fi bande dessinée with scratchy pen lines, sparse crosshatching, loose construction marks, semi-realistic anatomy, strong perspective, controlled foreshortening, and flat cel-shaded tones.

Add a distressed 1970s–1980s retro-print finish: aged paper grain, imperfect ink, faded pigments, scuffs, speckling, subtle bleed, registration errors, worn signage, and weathered surfaces.

Use crushed shadows, narrow rim light, wet-surface highlights, glowing mist, and backlit rain for a mysterious, sensual, dystopian atmosphere.
