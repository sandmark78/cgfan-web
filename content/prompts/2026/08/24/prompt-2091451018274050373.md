## Prompt

Create a breathtaking 4:5 vertical luxury travel artwork for [COUNTRY], inspired by high-end editorial covers, vintage European travel posters, fine-art photography, and atmospheric painting, with a completely original concept.

CONCEPT — “THE COUNTRY BEHIND THE GLASS”
A gigantic, crystal-clear architectural glass window floats vertically within an elegant dreamlike landscape. The glass acts as a portal into the soul of [COUNTRY]. Inside it, create a richly layered miniature world featuring:

One unmistakable iconic landmark as the hero element

Characteristic architecture, rooftops and streets

Dramatic natural scenery appropriate to [COUNTRY]

A subtle national flag naturally integrated into the scene

Traditional/local transportation

Native flowers and vegetation

Tiny human figures for scale

1–2 distinctive cultural objects or details

Atmospheric birds, clouds and distant scenery

Everything should feel physically contained within one enormous transparent glass portal, with a few elements subtly extending through the glass into the foreground.

MAGICAL REFLECTION
At the bottom, create a perfectly calm, mirror-like surface. Instead of a normal reflection, show an alternate dreamlike version of [COUNTRY]—softer, more mysterious and slightly watercolor-like. Connect both worlds seamlessly at the horizon.

Add realistic glass effects: subtle rainbow dispersion, edge highlights, sunlight caustics, floating illuminated dust, faint condensation, microscopic glass texture, and delicate reflections of the architecture and sky.

COMPOSITION & TYPOGRAPHY
Use a refined luxury editorial composition with generous negative space. Make the landmark visually dominant while allowing smaller cultural details to reveal themselves on closer inspection.

Upper section text:

[COUNTRY]
THE SOUL OF [COUNTRY]
A JOURNEY THROUGH TIME • 2026

Use minimal, elegant high-fashion serif typography, perfectly centered and naturally integrated into the artwork. Keep the poster uncluttered.

ATMOSPHERE & COLOR
Early morning after light mist. Warm ivory sunlight filtering through pale clouds. Sophisticated tones of champagne, cream, muted blue, dusty green and warm stone. Cinematic atmospheric haze, soft volumetric sunlight, natural depth and subtle lens compression.

STYLE & QUALITY
Blend ultra-realistic architectural detail with the delicacy of fine-art watercolor and the polish of a luxury fashion editorial. Premium large-format photography aesthetic, realistic materials, fine textures, delicate shadows, controlled highlights, sophisticated color grading, dreamlike realism, timeless luxury.

Avoid: harsh saturation, cartoon styling, excessive fantasy, visual clutter, artificial-looking architecture, distorted landmarks, generic scenery, or unnecessary decorative elements.

FINAL FEEL:
A secret window into the soul of [COUNTRY]—where its architecture, landscape, culture, history and atmosphere exist together in one impossible, poetic and unforgettable moment. Museum-quality luxury travel magazine cover, cinematic fine-art photography, sophisticated, nostalgic, timeless and visually breathtaking.
