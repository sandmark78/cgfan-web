## Prompt

Create a hyper-realistic holographic vinyl sticker using only {{COMPANY_NAME}} and/or the uploaded {{LOGO_IMAGE}}. All styling, material, lighting, camera, and composition details below should remain fixed for every generation.

Logo & Sticker Design:
- Use the uploaded logo or the official logo associated with {{COMPANY_NAME}} exactly as provided
- Do not redesign, reinterpret, distort, recolor, or alter the logo's shapes or proportions
- Use only the brand's icon/logo mark—never the wordmark, brand name, typography, or lettering
- Create a custom die-cut border that precisely follows the outer silhouette of the logo mark
- Add a crisp, clean white outline along the cut edge

Material & Finish:
- Hyper-realistic high-gloss laminated vinyl sticker
- Metallic holographic foil with shifting cyan, magenta, violet, gold, and green reflections
- Include slightly raised ink texture, delicate specular highlights, faint micro-scratches, and realistic visible sticker thickness around the edges
- The top-right corner should be naturally lifted/peeled back, revealing the matte adhesive backing and internal vinyl layers underneath

Scene & Photography:
- Flat, solid black studio background
- Soft, subtle contact shadow beneath the sticker
- Controlled ambient reflections for a premium product-photography look
- Shot on a full-frame DSLR/mirrorless camera with an 85mm macro lens, f/5.6, ISO 100
- Softbox key light positioned upper-left with a subtle rim light from the right
- Tack-sharp focus across the entire sticker surface
- True-to-life contrast and photorealistic material rendering
- 4K quality, vertical 3:4 aspect ratio

Overall aesthetic: premium collectible product photography, physically realistic and highly detailed.
