## Prompt

Create a flagship commercial advertising poster for an original milk biscuit snack brand named "VELLO MILK CRISP", combining Orbit energy with Port symbol power. Use ultra-realistic commercial photography, not illustration, not 3D render. Keep the same vertical split-world structure: a dull low-energy left side and a vivid energized right side, with one oversized snack pack across the lower center acting as the absolute visual hero and the symbolic switch between the two emotional states. The final image must feel like a Cannes-level youth FMCG campaign: bold, playful, product-led, instantly memorable, and highly ownable.
Style lock: premium lifestyle snack advertising, clean split-comparison composition, youth-oriented commercial photography, bright branded packaging realism, product-first hierarchy, strong narrative transformation, polished global ad finish.
Core composition: one clear vertical divide separates two emotional worlds. Left side: warm muted brown study-room atmosphere, the same young man slouched, bored, mentally drained, chin resting on one hand. Right side: vivid cool-blue energized world, the same young man smiling, uplifted, relaxed, naturally biting the biscuit with visible enjoyment. Across the lower center foreground, place one oversized horizontal snack pack bridging both worlds. The pack must be larger, sharper, and more dominant than any other object. The product should visually read as the switch that transforms the scene from dull to alive.
Port logic: turn the hero pack into the brand’s super-symbol. The combination of the oversized blue wrapper, milk splash, biscuit pieces, and the left-right emotional contrast must feel like one ownable brand device. The viewer should remember the pack as a “mood upgrade button” at first glance. The pack must feel iconic, central, and unmistakable.
Orbit logic: build a strong left-to-right transformation path using doodle arrows, milk splash motion, biscuit placement, hand gesture direction, and gaze flow. The eye should begin in the left-side boredom zone, hit the hero pack in the center, then burst outward into the right-side joyful zone. The motion must feel clean, graphic, and energetic, never cluttered.
Product concept: redesign the snack as an original milky cream biscuit called "VELLO MILK CRISP". The pack should be shelf-ready, youthful, and globally marketable, with a saturated royal-blue and cream identity, rounded white lettering, appetizing biscuit imagery, and minimal claims such as "crispy milk biscuit" and "triple snack packs". All text must be original.
Male character: one realistic young adult male appearing in two emotional states. Natural anatomy, believable curly hair, realistic face proportions, correct hands, five fingers, natural skin texture, no plastic look. Left version: tired, bored, slightly slumped, low-energy expression. Right version: bright, playful, smiling, naturally holding and biting the biscuit. Clothing should support the contrast: muted neutral casualwear on the left, vibrant cool-toned hoodie or casual styling on the right.
Lighting: left side uses warmer, dimmer, flatter indoor lighting with softer contrast. Right side uses brighter, cleaner, cooler commercial lighting with stronger separation and more vibrant highlights. The hero pack must receive the cleanest studio key light with crisp print detail and strong edge clarity. Milk splash should catch bright glossy highlights.
Materials and realism: realistic biscuit pack with crisp folded edges, satin-gloss wrapper reflections, believable biscuit texture, semi-translucent creamy milk splash, natural desk objects and room props kept secondary, and a few biscuit pieces near the pack for appetite cue. Every element must feel physically coherent and premium.
Typography and layout: use original English text only, never copy existing wording. Add one bold upper headline such as "SNACK UPGRADED." or "POP THE BORING." Keep one short lower-right slogan such as "MILK JOY. ZERO DULL." Typography must be bold, youthful, and secondary to the hero pack and the transformation story.
Color strategy: left side warm dusty browns and muted amber tones; right side vivid blue and cool electric highlights; hero pack in saturated royal blue, white, cream, and biscuit-gold; milk-white accents as freshness triggers. Keep the palette disciplined and high-contrast.
Quality and physical rules: extremely sharp hero pack, readable label, correct hand anatomy, believable split-scene perspective, clean edge separation, no clutter overload, no warped pack, no deformed face, no extra fingers, no muddy lighting, no dead black patches. The product pack must remain the strongest visual sales trigger and the clearest emotional-switch symbol at all times.
Negative prompt: copied branding, copied slogan, weak product hierarchy, blurry pack, unreadable label, distorted hands, extra fingers, fused fingers, plastic skin, deformed face, messy room clutter, muddy blue lighting, chaotic layout, cheap social-media flyer aesthetic, cartoon rendering, CGI toy look, low-resolution packaging, product losing dominance.
