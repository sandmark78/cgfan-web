## Prompt

Create a premium commercial packaging poster for an original fast-food brand named "EMBER STACK", with a stronger Port direction that turns the packaging itself into a brand super-symbol. Use ultra-realistic commercial product photography, not illustration, not 3D render. Keep the same tabletop combo-meal structure, but make one tall hero combo box the architectural centerpiece of the entire composition, so the packaging and the meal items together form an instantly recognizable brand world. The final image must feel like a Cannes-level global fast-food launch visual: iconic, craveable, premium, and highly ownable.
Style lock: high-end fast-food packaging campaign, premium studio tabletop photography, strong brand-first hierarchy, crisp packaging realism, clean commercial lighting, globally scalable premium burger-brand aesthetic.
Core composition: one tall hero combo box stands in the rear center as the dominant symbolic structure, slightly taller and more visually commanding than all other packages. Around it, arrange a tightly controlled product family: one secondary branded box, one fries carton, one drink cup, one bottled beverage, one small side box, two small sauce containers, and two oversized burgers in the front hero zone. The burgers must remain the strongest edible triggers, but the tall center box must act as the unmistakable brand monument. The composition should feel like a premium branded altar of fast food, clean and highly legible.
Port logic: the tall center package must function as the brand’s super-symbol. It should feel almost like a storefront facade or signature totem for "EMBER STACK", with bold logo placement, refined iconography, and proportions that make it memorable even in silhouette. The box should not feel like generic packaging; it should feel like the identity of the brand made physical.
Brand design direction: build a unified identity for "EMBER STACK" using luxurious charcoal-black packaging, warm amber-gold typography, and restrained red-orange accents. The print system should feel bold, clean, modern, and premium, with minimal icons and disciplined typography. Every pack should look like part of one coherent brand family.
Hero food design: the two burgers in the foreground must look irresistible and premium, with glossy brioche buns, thick beef patties, melted cheddar, fresh lettuce, sliced tomato, onion, and rich sauce layering. Fries should be golden and crisp. The cup and bottle must complement the combo. The side item can be nuggets, cheesy bites, or loaded pops. Everything must feel freshly served, warm, tactile, and highly craveable.
Lighting: use controlled studio product lighting with soft frontal key light and subtle side highlights. Keep the center hero box crisp and legible while the burgers remain richly appetizing. Packaging edges, bun gloss, cheese melt, fry texture, and cup highlights must all read clearly. Shadows should be soft, grounded, and premium.
Materials and realism: realistic printed cardboard boxes with matte or satin finish, clean folds, sharp corners, believable ink density, and premium tactile quality. The burgers must show realistic bread pores, meat texture, cheese melt, and fresh vegetable moisture. Fries must show crisp edges and a slight oil sheen. Cups, lids, bottles, and sauce tubs must all obey realistic scale and material behavior.
Background and atmosphere: keep the background minimal and controlled, using a deep charcoal studio black or very dark gradient. No distracting scenery. The scene should feel like a luxury tabletop reveal of a complete branded meal system.
Typography and packaging text: use original English text only, never copy any existing wording. Packaging can feature phrases such as "EMBER STACK", "GRILL HOUSE COMBO", "DOUBLE SMASH", "CRISP FRIES", and "FIRE SIP". Keep all text minimal, modern, and premium. No extra poster headline beyond the branding built into the product system.
Color strategy: dominant charcoal black, warm amber gold, toasted bread brown, cheddar yellow, lettuce green, tomato red, and restrained cream-white type. The palette must feel premium, bold, and globally marketable.
Mood: iconic, satisfying, premium, craveable, contemporary, globally scalable, strong brand memory, elevated fast-food launch campaign.
Quality and physical rules: extremely sharp packaging, readable labels, realistic food scale, clean box geometry, believable material reflections, no warped cups, no deformed burgers, no messy clutter, no muddy blacks, no dead shadow blocks, no cheap takeaway look. The tall center package must be remembered as the visual symbol of the brand, while the burgers remain the appetite engine.
Negative prompt: copied branding, copied packaging text, cheap fast-food flyer aesthetic, cluttered composition, blurry labels, warped boxes, deformed burgers, fake cheese, plastic-looking fries, unreadable packaging, muddy lighting, dead black patches, excessive props, cartoon rendering, CGI toy look, low-resolution product detail, weak product hierarchy, generic packaging silhouette.
