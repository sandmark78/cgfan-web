## Prompt

Create a premium commercial packaging poster for an original fast-food brand named "EMBER STACK", with a stronger Port direction that turns the packaging itself into a brand super-symbol. Use ultra-realistic commercial product photography, not illustration, not 3D render.

Core composition: one tall hero combo box stands in the rear center as the dominant symbolic structure, slightly taller and more visually commanding than all other packages. Around it, arrange a tightly controlled product family: one secondary branded box, one fries carton, one drink cup, one bottled beverage, one small side box, two small sauce containers, and two oversized burgers in the front hero zone.

Port logic: the tall center package must function as the brand's super-symbol. It should feel almost like a storefront facade or signature totem for "EMBER STACK", with bold logo placement, refined iconography, and proportions that make it memorable even in silhouette.

Brand design direction: build a unified identity for "EMBER STACK" using luxurious charcoal-black packaging, warm amber-gold typography, and restrained red-orange accents. The print system should feel bold, clean, modern, and premium.

Hero food design: the two burgers in the foreground must look irresistible and premium, with glossy brioche buns, thick beef patties, melted cheddar, fresh lettuce, sliced tomato, onion, and rich sauce layering.

Lighting: use controlled studio product lighting with soft frontal key light and subtle side highlights. Keep the center hero box crisp and legible while the burgers remain richly appetizing.

Materials and realism: realistic printed cardboard boxes with matte or satin finish, clean folds, sharp corners, believable ink density, and premium tactile quality. The burgers must show realistic bread pores, meat texture, cheese melt, and fresh vegetable moisture.

Color strategy: dominant charcoal black, warm amber gold, toasted bread brown, cheddar yellow, lettuce green, tomato red, and restrained cream-white type. The palette must feel premium, bold, and globally marketable.
