## Prompt

9:16 vertical, ultra-photorealistic cinematic portrait, ancient Chinese architecture in torrential rain × melancholic wandering woman × restrained oriental aesthetics × high-end fashion editorial photography × quiet cinematic realism.

A beautiful young East Asian woman walks alone through a narrow ancient Chinese courtyard during an intense rainstorm, full-body composition, slightly off-center framing, surrounded by monumental dark wooden architecture and traditional curved tiled roofs disappearing into cold rain and mist.

She wears layered historical Chinese robes made from heavy wet raw silk, translucent gauze and subtle woven fabrics, predominantly weathered ivory, charcoal black and muted ink gray, with an extremely dark oxidized cinnabar-red sash around the waist. The fabrics are completely soaked by the rain, becoming heavy, wrinkled and naturally clinging in places, with realistic textile texture and weight.

Her black hair is arranged in a loose historical updo, partially undone by the rain. Wet strands stick naturally to her face and neck while others drift gently in the wind.

She lowers her head slightly with a quiet, distant, melancholic expression — beautiful but tired, introspective and emotionally restrained, as though she has just left somewhere she knows she will never return to.

One hand loosely gathers the wet fabric of her robe; the other hangs naturally beside her holding a thin rain-soaked branch with only a single faded pink blossom.

Heavy rain pours violently from the ancient tiled eaves. Thick foreground rain streaks, detailed middle-ground rainfall and distant architecture almost completely swallowed by pale blue-gray rain haze create strong atmospheric depth.

The wet stone pavement is covered with shallow puddles and subtle reflections. Only a few damaged, rain-soaked pink petals remain scattered across the ground — no decorative sea of flowers.

Lighting is extremely cinematic and restrained: cold overcast daylight and blue-gray ambient rain light dominate the scene, while a very subtle warm glow from the wooden building on the right brushes across one edge of her face and wet clothing. Approximately one third of her face remains naturally in shadow.

Large areas of dark negative space, foreground wet rocks partially obscuring the frame, imperfect observational composition, quiet visual storytelling, realistic skin pores, rainwater on skin, wet hair, physically accurate soaked fabric, subtle film grain, deep blacks, controlled highlights, muted colors, sophisticated tonal separation.

Ancient Chinese visual language × cinematic still photography × oriental haute couture editorial × loneliness after the rain.
