## Prompt

Create an ultra-premium surreal fashion-tech advertising poster for a fictional eyewear brand called 'LUMA FRAME', preserving the structural logic of a luxury social-profile interface printed on matte white paper, with one central realistic woman violently bursting through a torn opening into real space. The eyewear must remain the absolute visual hero. Use a strict vertical poster composition styled like a premium mobile social profile page. At the very top, place a wide horizontal monochrome banner showing only a pair of sharply detailed female eyes. Below it, build a clean white interface with circular avatar, fictional profile name, minimal stats. The torn-paper breakthrough event in the center.
