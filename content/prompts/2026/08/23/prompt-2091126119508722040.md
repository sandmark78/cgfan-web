## Prompt

For each uploaded photo, create a separate high-end designer poster. 3:4 vertical composition, divided into two sections with strict 1:1 height ratio. Upper half preserves original photo with subtle high-end photographic color grading. Lower half extracts the most recognizable subject and reconstructs into exquisite three-dimensional origami and paper-sculpture collage. Use folding, faceting, layering, nesting, and paper-piece assembly. Reorganize composition according to subject's visual center of gravity, contour flow, and volumetric proportion. Use large areas of negative space for breathing room.
