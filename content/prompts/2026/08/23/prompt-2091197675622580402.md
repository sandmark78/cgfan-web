## Prompt

Create a premium vertical 4:5 minimalist travel poster inspired by modern paper sculpture. Feature one iconic architectural silhouette emerging from or floating against a calm oversized circular moon/sun form. Use generous negative space, soft tactile background, delicate layered paper-cut elements, extremely clean geometry, subtle 3D depth. Color palette: 3-5 soft colors with harmonious muted tones. Overall aesthetic: luxury contemporary art print + Japanese-inspired minimalism + handcrafted paper sculpture.
