## Prompt

Create a sophisticated vertical 3:4 travel poster as a handcrafted layered papercut landmark collage. Identify 3-4 most iconic landmarks genuinely located in the city. Preserve each landmark's authentic silhouette and proportions. Create one unified editorial composition using varied scale, overlap, foreground/midground/background depth. Build the scene from 4-7 shallow paper layers: sky, distant environment, secondary landmarks, Hero, city context, foreground. Use matte handmade paper, subtle fibers, clean organic cut edges, flat colors, and delicate 1-3mm shadows. Use a refined 6-9 color muted palette. Style: premium travel poster + contemporary editorial illustration + handcrafted papercut + subtle mid-century influence.
