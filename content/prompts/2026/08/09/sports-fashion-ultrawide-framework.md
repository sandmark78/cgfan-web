## 简介

运动时尚超广角摄影框架 — 以 0.5× 超广角镜头从极低角度拍摄运动时尚人像，前景元素极度放大创造戏剧性透视。韩系运动街头风格，明亮自然光+蓝天+城市运动场景。

## Prompt

Subject: [male / female, appearance]
Sport: [skateboarding / boxing / basketball / BMX / other]
Foreground element: [skateboard / glove / sneaker / wheel / sports gear]
Outfit: [Korean sporty streetwear]
Scene: [court / skatepark / rooftop / urban sports space]

Create a photorealistic 9:16 sports-fashion portrait with an extreme 0.5× ultra-wide perspective.

Shoot from an ultra-low camera angle, very close to the subject. Push one foreground element extremely close to the lens so it appears oversized and creates dramatic near-to-far perspective, while keeping the subject's face clearly visible in the midground.

Use a dynamic crouching, sitting, leaning, or athletic pose with strong diagonal body lines. Keep the styling youthful, Korean-inspired, sporty and fashionable rather than professional competition wear.

Bright natural daylight, vivid blue sky, realistic shadows, urban sports environment, strong depth and immersive camera tension.

Keep all anatomy and sports equipment physically correct: natural arms and legs, five fingers, correct joints, believable contact points, accurate wheels, boards, balls or equipment. Strong wide-angle distortion is welcome, but avoid broken anatomy, floating objects or impossible geometry.

Realistic smartphone 0.5× photography, natural skin texture, crisp details, no CGI look, no excessive retouching.
