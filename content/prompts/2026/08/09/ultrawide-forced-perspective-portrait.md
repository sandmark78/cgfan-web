## 简介

超广角强制透视人像摄影框架 — 以 0.5× 超广角镜头从极低角度拍摄，将手/鞋/道具极度放大作为前景锚点，构建从前景→面部→背景的深度序列。强调主动互动姿态与真实皮肤质感。

## Prompt

Subject: [adult character, appearance, hairstyle]
Outfit: [clothing, shoes, accessories]
Scene: [location and background]
Foreground element: [hand, shoe, prop]
Pose: [crouching, sitting, leaning, stepping]
Lighting: [daylight, direct flash, sunset]
Mood: [playful, sporty, cool, cinematic]
Aspect ratio: [9:16]

Create a photorealistic 0.5× ultra-wide portrait with strong forced perspective.

Place the camera very low and close to the ground. Bring one hand, shoe, or prop close to the lens so it becomes dramatically enlarged, while keeping the subject's face clear and close to the center to avoid distortion.

Build clear depth from the oversized foreground element to the face, body, and background. Use court lines, roads, railings, rooftops, or architecture to strengthen the perspective.

The pose should feel active and interactive, as if the subject is reaching into the lens. Keep realistic skin texture, natural hair movement, accurate clothing folds, and believable lighting.

Avoid extra fingers, distorted hands, warped faces, duplicated limbs, plastic skin, stiff poses, text, logos, and illustration styling.
