## Prompt

Create an ultra-premium hybrid travel-fashion campaign poster that merges Orbit-style interaction enhancement with Transit-style international editorial polish, centered around one unforgettable three-part super-symbol: the same hand-drawn girl, four real scenic destinations, and four distinctly playful pose-actions. The composition must be a clean 2x2 four-panel grid. In every panel, the girl is fully illustrated by hand in the exact same character design and fashion identity, while everything else is completely real and photographic. The final artwork must feel like a Cannes-level global travel editorial campaign: stylish, witty, vibrant, highly shareable, and flawlessly integrated. Do not copy any existing title, slogan, or text.
Core super-symbol composition:
Build the entire image around one instantly readable visual equation:
1. one identical illustrated travel girl,
2. four different real-world scenic environments,
3. four clearly different fun travel poses.
Use a strict 2x2 grid with clean borders and strong panel balance. Each panel must feel distinct in location and action, but the whole poster must remain unified through the same illustrated character, the same drawing language, and a consistent premium travel-fashion tone. The viewer should understand the concept immediately at a glance.
Character consistency:
Use one iconic hand-drawn girl repeated across all four panels with exactly the same face logic, hair color, illustration style, body proportions, outline treatment, and fashion identity. She should feel youthful-adult, stylish, playful, and highly brandable, with bold colored hair, oversized fashion accessories or expressive stylized eyes, graphic clothing, and chunky fashion shoes. She must never drift into four different character designs. She is one same visual mascot traveling through four real destinations.
Real-vs-illustrated rule:
The girl is the only illustrated element. Everything else must be real photography: streets, buildings, beaches, railings, terraces, trees, mountains, paving, sea, sky, cafes, sunlight, shadows, textures, reflections, and environment details. The contrast must be crisp and intentional: one drawn girl inside four completely real locations.
Orbit enhancement:
Push the physical interaction much harder. The illustrated girl must feel truly grounded and physically present in each real location. Her feet must align perfectly with real pavement, sand, stairs, wood decks, or stone floors. Her body tilt, arm direction, and stride must react to the real perspective and gravity of each location. Add stronger contact logic: foot planting, edge overlap, partial occlusion, perspective scaling, and scene-matched cast shadows. She must look like she is genuinely walking, leaning, sitting, posing, or balancing inside the real scene, not pasted on top.
Transit enhancement:
Refine the whole image into an international top-tier travel editorial language. Unify the four locations with polished global color grading, cleaner air, brighter but controlled highlights, and premium retouching. The real scenes should feel luminous, cinematic, and magazine-ready. The illustrated girl’s palette should be vivid but sophisticated, harmonized with the real environments. The final image should feel like a luxury fashion-travel collaboration, not a casual social collage.
Four-scene narrative:
All four actions must be clearly different and instantly legible.
Illustration style:
Use premium hand-drawn fashion-cartoon illustration with bold black outlines, high-quality flat color blocks, controlled retro-pop charm, and slight editorial exaggeration. The girl should feel polished, fashionable, and premium. You may add only a very small amount of hand-drawn accent marks such as stars, sparkles, motion marks, or doodle waves around her, but keep them restrained so the core symbol remains: drawn girl inside real places.
Lighting integration:
The illustrated girl must adapt perfectly to the real lighting in each panel. If the scene has golden side light, cool ocean reflection, soft mountain haze, or urban shadow, the drawing must reflect the same light direction, shadow edge, and color temperature. This is essential. Her highlights and shadows must match each real location so the integration feels seamless and luxury-grade.
Color system:
Use a polished travel-editorial palette built from real environmental tones plus the girl’s iconic fashion colors. Her outfit may combine sunshine yellow, turquoise, coral, pink, fresh green, cobalt, or sky blue, but the tones must be refined and internationally stylish. The four real locations should each retain their own atmosphere while still belonging to one unified campaign color world.
Typography and layout:
If title-like graphic elements are used, keep them minimal, original, hand-drawn, and integrated across the four-panel poster as premium editorial decoration only. Do not copy any wording from the reference. Preserve enough negative space so the grid stays elegant and readable.
Image finish:
The final artwork must feel like a world-class hybrid travel-fashion campaign: iconic, crisp, witty, internationally polished, and highly memorable. Preserve photoreal clarity in the four real scenic locations, perfect character consistency in the illustrated girl, and impeccable integration across all four panels. 8k, flagship advertising quality.
