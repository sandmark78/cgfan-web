## Prompt

Don’t write this as “a model with doodles behind her.” That misses the whole trick.
The control point is fusion: the linework has to move from the wall onto the clothes, then onto the body edges, so the person feels like she is emerging from the illustrated world.
🧿 hand-drawn black and cobalt doodles crossing from the background onto the garment seams and body contours
This makes the illustration feel attached, not pasted. You see the lines following the waist, skirt edge, legs, and shoes, so the fashion silhouette stays readable while the mural becomes part of her styling.
🌿 dense Mediterranean doodle mural with controlled negative space around the full-body silhouette
This keeps the detail from swallowing the model. The background can be busy, but the empty space around her face, shoulders, and legs protects the pose and keeps the poster editorial.
