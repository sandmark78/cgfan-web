## Prompt

10h
Dynamic action portrait of a young Chinese warrior woman （use the uploaded image as reference) with long jet-black hair flowing wildly in the wind, captured mid-motion in an elegant dual-sword battle stance, wielding one straight sword in each hand, body twisting dynamically with powerful yet graceful martial-arts movement. Her flowing sleeves, layered robes, ribbons, and hair sweep dramatically in the same direction, creating strong circular motion and cinematic energy.
She wears extraordinarily ornate layered Chinese fantasy armor built around a luxurious cloisonné aesthetic: brilliant mirror-polished bright silver armor plates, highly reflective platinum-silver metallic surfaces, intricate embossed filigree and engraved relief ornamentation, combined with deep cloisonné enamel blue panels — rich lacquered cobalt-blue, extremely deep yet luminous, glossy vitreous enamel with subtle inner glow and reflected highlights, NOT matte navy fabric, NOT dark steel.
Fine oxidized antique-gold filigree wire traces the borders of the blue enamel panels, armor seams, decorative motifs and ornamental medallions — aged brass-gold, slightly tarnished and restrained, never bright yellow gold.
Her outer robes are flowing soft ivory-white silk, lightweight and translucent at the edges, billowing violently through the air, layered over deep luminous enamel-blue decorative textile and armor sections. Very subtle cinnabar-red lining appears only inside selected folds as a restrained warm accent.
Behind and above her coils a colossal spectral Chinese dragon forming an enormous circular arc around the warrior, preserving a powerful serpentine silhouette. The dragon is rendered primarily in brilliant polished silver-white, with intricate sculptural scales, horns, whiskers and flowing mane, combined with deep glossy cloisonné enamel-blue scale accents matching the woman's armor. Fine antique-gold filigree wirework subtly traces selected dragon ornaments and scale boundaries.
The dragon's body partially dissolves into enormous swirling pale silver-white energy tendrils, liquid-metal mist, translucent smoke and flowing cloud-like currents, forming a circular vortex around the woman. No orange fire, no conventional flames — all supernatural energy is silver-white, pearl-white and translucent metallic mist.
Plain warm-grey studio backdrop, minimal environment, allowing the warrior and dragon silhouette to dominate the composition. Dramatic directional rim lighting catches every polished silver edge and produces sharp specular reflections across the armor, sword blades and dragon scales, while the deep enamel-blue surfaces remain dark, saturated, glossy and internally luminous.
Extremely intricate craftsmanship, Chinese cloisonné-inspired decorative language, luxury sculptural armor, realistic metal reflections, realistic silk texture, cinematic fantasy photography, dynamic freeze-frame movement, controlled high contrast, hyper-detailed, photorealistic skin, natural facial texture, 8K hyperrealistic.

mirror-polished bright silver + luminous deep cobalt cloisonné enamel blue + soft ivory-white silk + restrained oxidized antique-gold filigree + tiny cinnabar-red accents.
No gunmetal, no matte navy blue, no black armor, no bright yellow gold, no orange flames, no excessive red, no plastic CGI surfaces.
