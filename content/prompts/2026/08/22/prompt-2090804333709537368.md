## Prompt

【CITY】: ___
【SIDE A CONCEPT】: ___
【SIDE B CONCEPT】: ___
【SIDE A ELEMENTS】: ___
【SIDE B ELEMENTS】: ___
【SIDE A COLORS】: ___
【SIDE B COLORS】: ___
【MAIN TITLE】: ___
【CONCEPT LABEL】: ___ / ___
【TAGLINE】: ___

Create a premium 9:16 editorial city poster that transforms 【CITY】 into two contrasting but connected urban worlds.

Build two dense city environments from opposite sides of the frame. Side A grows horizontally inward from the left edge, while Side B grows inward from the right edge, creating the feeling of two enormous urban worlds facing each other.

Keep only a narrow 12–18% atmospheric corridor in the center using sky, mist, light, water or haze appropriate to the city. The opening should feel naturally created by the two worlds pressing together, not like a clean vertical line.

Each side must have its own distinct historical period, architectural language, color temperature, lighting mood, atmospheric density, and cultural identity. Side A and Side B should feel like two completely different eras or dimensions of the same city, yet share subtle visual echoes that make them feel connected.
