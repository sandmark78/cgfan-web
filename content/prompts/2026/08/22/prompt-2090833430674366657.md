## Prompt

Create a premium vertical wildlife campaign poster for a fictional eco-travel and conservation brand called VERDANT REALM, combining Orbit-style visual rhythm with Transit-style editorial restraint. The image must feel rare, calm, graphic, and internationally premium. The green peafowl must remain the absolute visual hero, and the entire bird must be visually contained within three tall vertical cutout frames. The peafowl must not extend outside these three vertical panels.

Core composition:
Use a warm off-white minimalist background in the upper half and a misty forest landscape in the lower half. At the center of the poster, place three tall vertical rectangular crop panels standing side by side with even spacing. The realistic adult green peafowl is revealed only inside these three panels, as if the subject exists entirely within a three-window documentary viewing frame.
