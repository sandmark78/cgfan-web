## Prompt

graphite and colored pencil illustration on cream textured paper, a girl in paint-stained overalls, mismatched socks, and loose braids sitting cross-legged inside a hollow baobab made into a tiny library. Curved shelves hold worn books, jars of pressed beetles, painted wooden eggs. A brass lamp casts warm amber light over the large illustrated atlas in her lap. a tortoiseshell cat squeezes through the round doorway. Outside, lavender dusk settles over a quiet village.
