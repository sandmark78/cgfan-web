## Prompt

Create a 9:16 high-fashion editorial poster series featuring the same original character across all four images.

Character:
A young East Asian man with sharp refined features, a slim jawline, narrow dark eyes, tousled black hair, natural skin texture, and silver earrings. His expression is contemplative and mysterious, with a quiet intensity.

Styling:
High-fashion avant-garde clothing, structured silhouettes, monochromatic palette (black, charcoal, ivory), layered textures (wool, silk, leather). Accessories include minimal jewelry and polished boots.

Setting:
Brutalist concrete architecture with dramatic shadows, or minimalist studio with single directional light source. Background should complement the character without competing for attention.

Lighting:
Cinematic chiaroscuro, strong directional light from upper left, deep shadows, film noir atmosphere. Soft fill light to preserve detail in shadows.

Mood:
Sophisticated, enigmatic, timeless. The character exists in a space between reality and dream, familiar yet unreachable.

Composition:
Four distinct poses and framings:
1. Close-up portrait, eyes slightly off-camera
2. Medium shot, three-quarter view, hands in pockets
3. Full body, standing against architectural element
4. Profile silhouette, dramatic backlighting

Technical:
Shot on medium format camera (Hasselblad H6D-100c), 80mm lens, f/2.8, ISO 100. Film emulation: Kodak Portra 400 with pushed contrast.

Final aesthetic: Vogue Hommes Japan editorial, sophisticated and editorial, suitable for luxury brand campaign or gallery exhibition.
