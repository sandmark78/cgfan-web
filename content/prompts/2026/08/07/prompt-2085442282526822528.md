# 文化微缩字母：城市国家可视化系统

**作者**: Gadgetify  
**日期**: 2026-08-06  
**评分**: 58/80

## Prompt

```
2x2 grid, do this for 2 countries and 2 cities 16:9

Input A is a Country or Region
Analyze the location to generate 4 Design Assets:

1. The Monogram (The Shape):
   The first letter of the Country's English name (e.g., M, J, I, B).

2. The Palette (The Color):
   The defining architectural or environmental colors.
   (e.g., Morocco -> Chefchaouen Blue & Ochre. Japan -> Natural Wood & Red. Italy -> Terracotta & Cream).

3. The Industry (The Narrative):
   A specific cultural craft or production chain.
   (e.g., Argan Oil/Spices, Tea Production, Wine Making, Coffee Harvesting).

4. The Architecture (The Style):
   The building style inside the letter (e.g., Kasbah, Pagoda, Villa).

The Shadowbox:
Goal: A "Cultural Dollhouse" Typography Shot.

The Structure:
The "Monogram" (Step 1) is constructed as a thick, deep Open-Faced Shadow Box.

The Material:
The casing is matte grey metal or neutral wood, acting as a frame.

The Setting:
The letter stands upright on a vintage Paper Map of the specific region. The country name is visible on the map in the foreground.

The Interior (The Vignettes):
The Partitioning: The hollow space inside the letter is divided into multiple floors or rooms using shelves.

The Flow: The scenes depict a "Source to Market" narrative, utilizing the geometry of the letter (e.g., sliding down the diagonal of a 'V' or 'M').

Section 1 (Source): Harvesting raw materials (e.g., Picking olives, tea leaves, or grapes).
Section 2 (Process): Processing the goods (e.g., A press, a fermentation vat, a drying rack).
Section 3 (Market): Selling the product (e.g., A Souk stall, a Sushi bar, a Cafe).

The Narrative Scale (The Inhabitants):
Scale: 1:87 scale (HO Scale) miniature figures.
Costume: Figures are dressed in regionally appropriate attire.
```
