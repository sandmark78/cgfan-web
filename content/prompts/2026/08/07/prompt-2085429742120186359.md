# MONUMENTAL Vol.02：粉彩几何时尚摄影

## Prompt

```
MONUMENTAL // VOL. 02

Monumentality, reimagined in pastel.

The perspective remains. The mood evolves.

A continuation of MONUMENTAL, where oversized proportions and impossible viewpoints transform fashion into architecture. This chapter trades concrete for candy colors, hard edges for flowing silhouettes, while preserving the same sense of scale and visual dominance.

Pastel geometry. Sculptural styling. Dramatic low angles.

Every frame explores how perspective alone can reshape the way we experience fashion.

Shot from ground level inside a monumental architectural space, every frame stretches scale, exaggerates proportion, and transforms simple silhouettes into graphic statements. The clean contrast of monochrome styling, sculptural surroundings, and soft skylight creates a visual dialogue between the human form and industrial architecture.

The low angle isn't a gimmick, it's the narrative. It turns architecture into a stage, fashion into structure, and body language into composition. Every pose is designed to interact with the space, proving that perspective can be just as important as styling.
```
