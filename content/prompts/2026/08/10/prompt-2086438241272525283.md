## Prompt

[FORMAT]
16:9

[SCENE / WORLD]
A celestial East Asian megastructure world suspended above an endless cloud ocean, with architecture operating at geological scale rather than building scale. Monumental palace corridors, giant open pavilions, colossal moon gates, cliff-edge terraces, and palace foundations functioning like artificial mountains. One sprawling windswept ancient pine or one massive vertical waterfall interrupts the rigid timber geometry. No visible ground plane. Sacred stillness, impossible height, deep layered atmosphere, architecture framing architecture framing nature.

[COLOR PALETTE]
#436681 muted gray-blue atmospheric field
#829AA5 soft blue-gray cloud ocean and haze
#263D40 deep cool architectural and tree shadows
#6E4536 restrained warm timber structure
#BD673B localized amber edge light
#DED4C5 soft ivory-gray highlights

[STRUCTURAL ATMOSPHERE]
Use layered atmospheric concealment as a core structural device:
- a cloud ocean erasing the ground plane
- drifting mist partially swallowing architectural bases and distant cliffs
- vertical veils or water-curtain-like membranes selectively transmitting sky and clouds
- ancient tree trunks or giant columns creating spatial occlusion
- palace-edge waterfalls generating spray that obscures lower structure

The true scale should remain only partially measurable.

[HUMAN SCALE MARKERS]
Use 1 to 12 extremely tiny robed figures only as spatial scale markers.
They may appear as:
- a lone solitary figure on a terrace or cliff edge
- sparse ceremonial groups along a railing or corridor

Faces must be unreadable. Gestures restrained. Silhouettes narrow and vertical. Robes in pale ivory, gray-white, or muted blue-gray, with dark hair silhouettes. No portrait emphasis.

[COMPOSITION]
Create one single coherent scene set high above the clouds, using one dominant monumental composition only. Choose one of the following:
- an ultra-long horizontal corridor
- a giant-column interior facing a distant moon gate
- an open ceremonial terrace with nested frames
- a palace edge releasing immense waterfalls into the cloud sea
- a cliff-edge pavilion with a windswept pine extending into vast negative space

[ARCHITECTURE / NATURAL RELATIONSHIP]
The architecture should feel ancient, immense, and layered, with repeated rooflines, giant columns, carved beams, and restrained decorative density near the center.
Natural forms should oppose architectural order:
- a sprawling horizontal pine canopy
- a giant cliff mass
- or falling water

[LIGHTING]
Soft atmospheric lighting.
A cool gray-blue environment should dominate roughly 75–85% of the frame.
Localized warm amber-orange grazing light should touch only:
- architectural edges
- selected columns
- some timber surfaces
- occasional tree bark
- terrace highlights

[IMAGE QUALITIES]
Preserve calm negative space, large depth, solemn ceremonial stillness, and elegant large-form readability.
Cinematic high-end fantasy environment concept art.
Refined matte-painting sensibility.
Large depth of field.
Atmospheric softness should come from cloud, mist, and spray rather than shallow lens blur.
Clear monumental silhouettes with progressive detail falloff into depth.
Subtle glow around clouds and bright mist.
Restrained detail density.
