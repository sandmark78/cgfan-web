## Prompt

9:16 vertical full-body fantasy character illustration, dark samurai gothic aesthetic, high-fashion warrior design, painterly concept art with expressive ink-brush energy.

A beautiful young East Asian female warrior standing in a powerful three-quarter pose against a minimal warm pale beige-gray background. She has delicate refined facial features, porcelain-pale realistic skin, narrow dark eyes, subtle muted-red eye makeup, soft natural lips, and thin understated eyeglasses. Her expression is calm, cold, detached and quietly dangerous.

Her extremely long black hair explodes upward and outward in the wind, individual strands dissolving into loose ink-like strokes. A striking silver-white streak of hair sweeps horizontally across her face, partially crossing one eye, creating strong directional movement.

She wears elaborate black-and-antique-silver samurai-inspired gothic armor, combining Japanese armor construction with dark fantasy haute couture. Huge sharply raised black shoulder plates, sculptural high collar, layered lacquer-black arm guards, engraved silver fittings, ornate skull-like metallic motifs, fitted black armored corset, asymmetrical hip armor, black thigh-high armored boots and intricate silver knee guards.

The armor is glossy but weathered, with subtle scratches, tarnished silver edges, engraved ornamental metalwork and restrained reflections. Beneath the armor are torn charcoal-gray fabric layers and long sweeping black-gray skirt panels.

Several pieces of vivid saturated crimson-red cords, knots and torn tassels hang from her waist and thigh armor. The red is exceptionally intense and clean — almost cinnabar / blood-crimson — becoming the strongest chromatic accent in an otherwise nearly monochromatic image. Small restrained crimson accents also appear around the eyes and armor details.

She holds a long slender black katana downward in one hand while the other hand grips the dark wrapped hilt of another sword at her hip. The weapons are elegant, extremely elongated and understated rather than oversized.

Her enormous black-gray cloak and skirt dissolve outward into broad, energetic dry-brush ink strokes, sweeping dramatically around the lower half of the composition. Fabric edges partially disappear into abstract painterly gestures, creating the feeling that the warrior herself is emerging from a traditional ink painting.

Strong visual contrast between highly resolved face and armor details and deliberately unresolved expressive brushwork around the silhouette. Sophisticated controlled chaos, localized areas approaching visual abstraction while the character remains perfectly legible.

Minimal background, generous negative space, no scenery, no typography, no symbols, no logo, no watermark, no star icon.

Palette dominated by lacquer black, charcoal gray, gunmetal, antique silver and warm pale gray, punctuated exclusively by brilliant deep crimson-red accents.

Japanese dark-fantasy concept art, contemporary Asian fashion illustration, sumi-e inspired gestural brushwork, elegant gothic samurai aesthetic, extremely intricate armor craftsmanship, realistic metallic reflections, delicate skin rendering, dynamic wind movement, sophisticated composition, cinematic character design, painterly realism, information-dense but visually controlled, masterpiece character concept art.

Aspect ratio 9:16.
