## Prompt

UNIVERSAL PROMPT - JAPANESE MINIMALIST MANGA EDITORIAL POSTER | 3:4

Transform each uploaded photograph into its own premium Japanese minimalist manga editorial poster. Process every image independently, never combining images or reusing previous compositions, colors, or decorations.

FORMAT: Vertical 3:4 canvas, divided horizontally into exactly two equal 50% sections, connected naturally without borders or divider lines.

TOP 50% — ORIGINAL PHOTOGRAPH: Preserve the original subject's identity, facial features, pose, clothing, proportions, distinctive details, perspective, lighting, shadows, and natural colors. Allow only subtle photographic enhancements. Never distort, redraw, replace, or invent subjects or objects.

BOTTOM 50% — MANGA INTERPRETATION: Simplify the main subject into expressive Japanese manga ink illustration, preserving its recognizable silhouette and defining features. Use bold hand-drawn contours, varied line weights, dry-brush strokes, broken ink lines, selective black shadows, and subtle halftone textures.

MINIMALIST COMPOSITION: Maintain approximately 70% clean negative space in the lower section. Keep the illustrated subject small, inset from all edges, and balanced through sophisticated asymmetry. Choose a unique composition suited to each photograph. Never overcrowd the frame.

COLOR RULE: Extract one dominant accent color, optionally two harmonious colors, exclusively from the current photograph. Combine them with black ink and a white or near-white paper background. Never introduce unrelated colors or reuse previous palettes.

Use subtle paper grain and restrained print textures. Add motion lines only when appropriate. Avoid excessive decorations, gradients, dense textures, borders, comic panels, and full-bleed dark backgrounds.

NO TEXT: No titles, Japanese characters, slogans, speech bubbles, sound effects, logos, or watermarks unless explicitly requested.

FINAL CHECK: Exact 3:4 ratio, precise 50/50 division, authentic upper photography, recognizable lower manga illustration, generous negative space, source-derived colors, and no unwanted text or clutter.

STYLE: Bold yet minimal, expressive yet restrained, combining authentic photography with sophisticated contemporary Japanese manga editorial design.
