## Prompt

UNIVERSAL PROMPT - PREMIUM FOOD × TYPOGRAPHY EDITORIAL POSTER | 9:16

Create a premium, photorealistic food editorial poster featuring [FOOD / DISH NAME], combining luxury commercial food photography with oversized, elegant editorial typography.

FOOD PRESENTATION: Showcase the dish as the hero subject, emphasizing its authentic ingredients, signature textures, natural colors, preparation details, and irresistible visual appeal. Style it with refined plating, realistic proportions, appetizing highlights, and meticulous attention to culinary authenticity.

TYPOGRAPHY & COMPOSITION: Feature [DISH NAME] in enormous, sophisticated, high-contrast serif typography inspired by Bodoni or Didone typefaces. Use a carefully selected color that complements the food and background. Integrate the dish with the headline, allowing it to overlap selected letterforms naturally, moving between foreground and background to create convincing three-dimensional depth. Maintain clear readability and strong visual hierarchy.

INGREDIENT DETAILS: Incorporate authentic ingredients associated with the dish through subtle visual elements, carefully placed garnishes, or refined editorial annotations. Include a concise ingredient line: [KEY INGREDIENTS]. Add a delicate culinary interaction where appropriate, such as a spoon lifting a bite, a fork cutting through the dish, a sauce drizzle, or fine ingredient particles falling through the composition.

COLOR PALETTE: Use a cohesive palette inspired by the dish, combining a sophisticated background tone, rich food-inspired colors, subtle highlights, and restrained metallic accents where appropriate. Maintain tonal harmony, luxurious contrast, and a premium editorial finish.

EDITORIAL DETAILS: Include an elegant italic subtitle, [SUBTITLE / CULINARY TAGLINE], alongside small, purposeful labels such as “CRAFTED WITH CARE,” “[COUNTRY / REGION OF ORIGIN],” and “[DISH CATEGORY].” Adapt all wording to the subject. Keep supporting text minimal, meaningful, correctly spelled, and professionally aligned.

LIGHTING & TEXTURE: Use refined studio lighting, realistic shadows, natural reflections, subtle depth of field, and hyperrealistic surface details. Capture the authentic textures of every ingredient, from delicate crumbs and creamy layers to glossy sauces, crisp edges, or fresh garnishes, as appropriate to the dish.

ART DIRECTION: Luxury culinary magazine meets premium restaurant advertising. Sophisticated, contemporary, visually striking, meticulously art-directed, and commercially polished. Balance oversized typography, realistic food photography, and generous negative space without overcrowding the composition.

OUTPUT REQUIREMENTS: Vertical 9:16 aspect ratio, ultra-detailed, high-resolution, print-quality aesthetic, photorealistic food styling, refined typography, physically believable depth, and a clean, cohesive layout.

AVOID: Watermarks, random logos, gibberish text, misspellings, excessive decorative elements, artificial food textures, unrealistic proportions, impossible object placement, distorted typography, cartoonish rendering, and cluttered composition.
