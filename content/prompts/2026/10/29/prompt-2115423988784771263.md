## Prompt

Create a premium overseas luxury chocolate advertising poster for original confectionery brand AURÉLIS D’OR, centered on one monumental gold-wrapped hazelnut praline as the hero product. The image is a cinematic commercial fantasy landscape: a giant crinkled golden chocolate sphere rests on a refined cliffside valley, while a wide glossy chocolate ribbon road spirals upward toward it in a powerful S-curve. The atmosphere is early morning champagne gold, soft, luminous, and luxurious. The concept is a sweet journey toward gold: the product becomes destination, architecture, and desire.

Use a vertical full-bleed poster with a wide scenic view and slightly low sweeping perspective from the chocolate road in the foreground toward the giant praline in the upper-right midground. The foreground chocolate road enters wide from the lower-right edge, curves through the center, then climbs toward the product. The left side keeps open pale sky and distant valley haze for restrained brand typography.

The giant gold-wrapped praline dominates the upper-right third, perched on a lush rocky terrace above a champagne-lit valley. The chocolate road forms an elegant S-shaped path, wraps around the cliff base, and leads the eye directly to the praline. Tiny adult visitors appear only as scale cues on terraces and path. Controlled hazelnut pieces, wafer crumbs, and small chocolate fragments scatter along the road. Product, road, sunrise glow, and logo create one clean diagonal reading path.
