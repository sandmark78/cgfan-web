## Prompt

Create a premium 4:5 vertical vintage editorial travel poster for [COUNTRY / CITY / LOCATION / SUBJECT].

TEXT-TO-IMAGE ONLY — generate the entire artwork creatively from the location or subject alone. No reference image required.

CORE CONCEPT
Design a sophisticated collectible travel poster inspired by mid-century American travel advertising, combining a highly detailed illustrated cityscape with oversized luxury editorial typography and subtle vintage print textures.

COMPOSITION
Place the destination name prominently across the upper section in enormous elegant high-contrast serif typography, allowing the letters to interact naturally with the skyline. Highlight selected letters with a warm ochre/golden accent while keeping the remaining typography deep charcoal black.

Below the typography, create a richly illustrated panoramic scene automatically based on the most recognizable visual identity of [COUNTRY / CITY / LOCATION / SUBJECT]. Feature its most iconic landmark or architectural element as the dominant focal point, supported by secondary buildings, bridges, streets, waterfronts, mountains, monuments, or cultural details that genuinely belong to the destination.

Add a subtle vintage sun disk, atmospheric clouds, distant silhouettes, and natural environmental details where appropriate. The scene should feel expansive, elegant, and carefully composed rather than crowded.

EDITORIAL DETAILS
Include small refined destination information around the typography:
— region/state/country
— recognizable secondary landmarks
— approximate coordinates
— one or two short location descriptors

Keep these details understated and typographically balanced. Automatically determine appropriate information from the destination.

VISUAL STYLE
Mid-century travel poster × engraved architectural illustration × vintage editorial design × refined American tourism print.

Use finely detailed ink and engraving-style linework, delicate crosshatching, halftone textures, slightly imperfect print registration, subtle paper grain, and restrained hand-printed imperfections.

COLOR PALETTE
Warm ivory paper background, charcoal black, muted architectural gray, dusty blue or destination-specific atmospheric tones, and one strong ochre/golden accent. Automatically adapt the accent colors to the character of the destination while preserving the vintage print aesthetic.

TYPOGRAPHY
Large elegant high-contrast serif display type, sophisticated editorial spacing, oversized lettering, restrained secondary sans-serif or serif information, strong hierarchy, and generous negative space.

FINAL AESTHETIC
A premium museum-shop travel poster that feels collectible, timeless, architectural, sophisticated, slightly nostalgic, and authentically printed on aged ivory paper.

Avoid photorealism, glossy digital rendering, modern gradients, excessive colors, generic landmarks, cluttered layouts, cartoon styling, excessive text, logos, watermarks, UI elements, and contemporary advertising aesthetics.

FORMAT
4:5 vertical composition, highly detailed, sharp architectural linework, balanced negative space, premium print quality, cohesive typography and illustration.
