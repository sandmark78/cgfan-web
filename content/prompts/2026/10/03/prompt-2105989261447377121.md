## Prompt

Create a premium 4:5 vertical photorealistic miniature diorama for [FOOD / OBJECT / SUBJECT].

TEXT-TO-IMAGE ONLY — generate the entire scene creatively from [FOOD / OBJECT / SUBJECT] alone. No reference image required.

CORE CONCEPT
Transform the outer form of [FOOD / OBJECT / SUBJECT] into a tiny, realistic architectural world. Keep the recognizable natural exterior, texture, color, shape, and material of the subject, while revealing a charming miniature interior hidden inside it.

COMPOSITION
Show the subject as a partially opened miniature structure, with one clearly visible opening or cutaway that allows the viewer to look directly inside. The exterior should remain unmistakably recognizable as the original subject.

Inside, create a fully furnished miniature space inspired by the character of the subject and its visual identity. Automatically determine the most fitting room or environment — such as a tiny library, studio, café, workshop, bedroom, shop, greenhouse, or other imaginative interior.

Make the interior feel physically built inside the subject, not like a separate room placed next to it. Include tiny furniture, shelves, books, objects, warm practical lighting, and carefully scaled architectural details.

MATERIAL CONTRAST
Create a strong tactile contrast between the exterior material of [FOOD / OBJECT / SUBJECT] and the interior construction. The outside should retain its authentic organic or physical texture, while the inside can use miniature wood, painted surfaces, fabric, paper, ceramic, or other appropriate materials.

DETAIL & SCALE
Emphasize the dramatic difference between the full-sized exterior form and the tiny furniture inside. Show realistic material transitions, thickness at the cut edge, small imperfections, believable construction, and intricate miniature details.

LIGHTING & STYLE
Warm cinematic miniature photography, soft evening illumination, subtle shadows, shallow depth of field, realistic macro perspective, highly detailed textures, calm cozy atmosphere, premium handcrafted diorama aesthetic.

BACKGROUND
Use a simple warm cream or neutral studio background so the miniature structure remains the sole focus.

FINAL AESTHETIC
Photorealistic miniature architecture × surreal object transformation × handcrafted diorama × cozy interior design × macro photography × tactile material realism.

Avoid people, floating objects, unrealistic proportions, generic containers, plastic-looking textures, excessive decoration, multiple separate scenes, text, logos, watermarks, split-screen layouts, or collage effects.

FORMAT
4:5 vertical, single cohesive composition, close 3/4 perspective, interior clearly visible, highly detailed, photorealistic, premium editorial quality.
