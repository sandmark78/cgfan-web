## Prompt

A tiny world of a famous city contained inside an old vintage matchbox — cinematic, surreal, and highly detailed.

Create a surreal miniature world of [CITY/LANDMARK] built entirely inside an open vintage matchbox. The matchbox sits on a dark wooden table, while the tiny city rises out of it like an impossibly detailed miniature diorama.

Make the iconic [LANDMARK] the central focal point, surrounded by miniature streets, tiny cars, pedestrians, local buildings, street lamps, trees, and atmospheric details unique to the location. A few matchsticks are scattered naturally around the box, with one glowing ember casting a warm cinematic light across the miniature city.

The outside of the matchbox remains old, worn, and tactile, with subtle vintage typography and travel-stamp details inspired by [CITY].

Style: surreal miniature photography, hyper-detailed diorama, cinematic macro lens, realistic materials, dramatic depth of field, warm atmospheric lighting, tiny human details, subtle haze, premium editorial photography, magical realism, physically believable miniature scale, 4:5 vertical composition.
