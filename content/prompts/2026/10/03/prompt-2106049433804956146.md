## Prompt

A luxury wine poster for fictional house VELORIA NOIR, designed as a minimalist high-impact advertising image built around two perfectly symmetrical red-wine glasses colliding at the center. Preserve the exact structure: a front-facing, centered, rigorously balanced composition where one crystal glass rises from the lower left and the other from the lower right, mirrored in angle, height, bowl size, wine level, rim position, and splash force. Their rims meet exactly on the center axis, launching a sculptural burst of dark ruby wine upward into a single iconic liquid totem. The wine is the absolute visual leader.

Use a pale warm ivory or refined stone-white background with strong negative space and museum-level stillness. The glasses are large, elegant, hyperreal, and partially cropped by the lower frame, but perfectly mirrored across the vertical centerline. Their bowls hold deep red wine with clean curved surfaces, visible meniscus, realistic refraction, thin crystal edges, and equal liquid volume. The collision point is precisely centered. From that midpoint, the wine erupts into the unmistakable silhouette of a charging bull or horned wine-spirit. The bull must feel born from the liquid itself: strong neck, lifted chest, clear head angle, subtle horn definition, fluid shoulder mass, and a tapering lower body dissolving into splash strands and droplets. It should stay elegant, premium, and physically plausible, never cartoonish or muddy.

The wine material is the emotional core: dark garnet, ruby, and black-cherry tones with translucent edges, glossy highlights, dynamic thickness variation, suspended droplets, and fine splash mist. Freeze the impact at the perfect instant, with mirrored force lines rising from both glasses into the central liquid creature. Keep the splash controlled, clean, and premium, with symmetrical energy and no chaotic scatter. The glasses remain elegant supporting anchors, while the liquid-born bull becomes the heroic symbol of force, taste, and identity.
