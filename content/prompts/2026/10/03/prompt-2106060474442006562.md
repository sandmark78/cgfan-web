## Prompt

A launch poster has to sell one bottle first, then prove it belongs to a system.
🍋 upper lifestyle panel built around one held lemon bottle, with lemons, tea leaves, and a single golden liquid ribbon orbiting only that hero SKU
The lifestyle image stays focused. Freshness feels active, but every visual cue still points back to the lemon bottle.
🧊 lower retail panel using the same bottle shape and label architecture in an evenly spaced flavor row, with lemon placed first and brightest
This turns variety into a clear range instead of a crowded shelf. The lead SKU remains memorable while the rest build brand scale.
