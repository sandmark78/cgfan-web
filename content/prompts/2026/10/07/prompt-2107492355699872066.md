## Prompt

Create a Cannes-level conceptual lifestyle poster for fictional premium fruit-fashion brand SUNTHREAD BANANA CO., merging product realism, playful anthropomorphism, and gallery-clean graphic design into one unified hero image. The core concept is edible couture: a single ripe banana becomes a fashion figure through form, posture, and styling, standing alone against a luminous monochrome field like a collectible runway object. The banana must remain the absolute visual leader.

Use a vertical full-bleed poster with a front-facing centered hero composition, strong negative space, and disciplined minimal balance. Preserve the exact structure: one anthropomorphic banana character centered in the frame, brand mark in the upper left, bold main title and support line in the lower half, and no clutter anywhere else. The figure occupies the middle third, leaving large breathing zones above and below for premium visual silence. The silhouette must read instantly from a distance like a fashion campaign object on a monochrome set.

Design a realistic ripe banana transformed into a couture-inspired character without losing fruit integrity. Two full yellow peel sections act like tailored trouser legs, slightly separated with believable curvature, subtle seam-like stitch graphics, and structured silhouette logic. The peeled upper section reveals a soft ivory fruit interior that reads like a torso. Two slim curved brown arms emerge from the upper sides, hanging loosely with elegant asymmetry. The top stem becomes a minimal crown-like head marker with no face. Add very small premium sneaker-like footwear at the base, cleanly integrated and correctly scaled. The figure must feel witty, sculptural, and fashion-forward, not childish or cartoonish.
