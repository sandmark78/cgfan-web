## Prompt

Create a premium 4:5 vertical surreal travel artwork for [COUNTRY / CITY / LOCATION / SUBJECT].

Create the entire scene from the written destination alone, automatically selecting its most recognizable architecture, landmarks, landscape, atmosphere, and cultural character.

CORE CONCEPT

Blend a photorealistic historic cityscape with a futuristic long-exposure transformation. The real architecture remains sharply recognizable while selected buildings stretch vertically into elegant trails of white, cream, gold, and warm amber light, creating a surreal double-exposure effect.

COMPOSITION

Build one seamless vertical cityscape around the strongest visual identity of [COUNTRY / CITY / LOCATION / SUBJECT].

Place a dramatic glowing sun or atmospheric light source in the upper portion against a cool blue-gray sky. Below it, combine authentic historic architecture, streets, bridges, natural surroundings, and a suitable waterfront or reflective surface when appropriate.

Let several prominent structures transition upward into elongated vertical bands of architectural light, as if captured during an extreme long exposure. The effect should grow naturally from the buildings rather than appearing as a separate graphic layer.

REFLECTION & ATMOSPHERE

Where water is naturally present, create a calm mirror reflection of the architecture, sun, light trails, and surrounding landscape. Keep the reflection slightly softer and more distorted than the main scene.

Use cinematic haze, realistic atmospheric depth, sharp architectural textures, detailed foliage, glowing windows, and subtle motion blur.

COLOR

Contrast a muted blue-gray sky with warm golden, amber, orange, or copper highlights inspired by the destination. Keep the palette sophisticated and cinematic rather than neon or overly saturated.

STYLE

Photorealistic architectural photography × surreal double exposure × futuristic long-exposure photography × vertical light streaks × cinematic travel artwork.

The futuristic elements should feel physically integrated into the real architecture, creating an impossible but believable transformation between historic identity and modern energy.

FINAL AESTHETIC

Dreamlike, atmospheric, sophisticated, cinematic, highly detailed, and unmistakably connected to [COUNTRY / CITY / LOCATION / SUBJECT].

Avoid generic architecture, invented landmarks, random skyscrapers, excessive neon, heavy glitch effects, cartoon styling, artificial reflections, clutter, collage effects, text, logos, watermarks, borders, and flat digital illustration.
