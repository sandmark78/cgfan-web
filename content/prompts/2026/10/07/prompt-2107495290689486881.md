## Prompt

A performance poster works when the athlete sells the feeling, but the product owns the frame.
🪽 “oversized hero sneaker in the lower center, three-quarter profile; athlete reduced to a smaller diagonal stride above it”
The shoe becomes the first read. The runner supplies speed and proof without competing for the purchase decision.
✨ “keep the runner’s lifted knee, extended leg, and dust trail aimed toward the shoe’s toe-to-heel axis”
Motion now lands on the product. The athlete and the typography both direct attention into the engineered details.
