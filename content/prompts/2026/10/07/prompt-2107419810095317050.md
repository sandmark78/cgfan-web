## Prompt

Create a premium 3:4 vertical vintage travel poster for [COUNTRY / CITY / LOCATION / SUBJECT].

TEXT-TO-IMAGE ONLY — create the entire artwork from the destination alone. No reference image required.

CORE CONCEPT
Reimagine [COUNTRY / CITY / LOCATION / SUBJECT] as a cohesive collectible vintage travel poster, using hand-painted gouache and watercolor with a sophisticated mid-century tourism aesthetic.

COMPOSITION
Use a warm cream paper background with generous negative space. Place a huge hand-painted destination name across the top in bold, imperfect deep-red uppercase lettering.

Behind the main scene, add a large glowing golden-yellow sun or atmospheric disc. Build the destination in clear overlapping layers — foreground, water/road/landscape, primary landmark, secondary architecture and nature, distant skyline or mountains, then sky.

Automatically choose 4–6 of the destination’s most recognizable visual elements. Make ONE iconic landmark the dominant focal point and use the others to support the story. Add a characteristic local transportation element when naturally appropriate.

VISUAL STYLE
Hand-painted gouache × watercolor travel illustration × vintage postcard × elegant editorial poster.

Use visible brushstrokes, subtle paper grain, imperfect painted edges, layered washes, simplified graphic forms, crisp silhouettes, selective fine details, and slightly exaggerated but believable proportions.

No photorealism.

COLOR
Use warm cream as the base with a destination-specific palette built around deep red, golden ochre, teal/turquoise, sky blue, leafy green, terracotta, and warm architectural neutrals. Keep the colors rich, harmonious, and distinctly vintage.

ATMOSPHERE
Bright, optimistic, nostalgic, and aspirational. Add only a few purposeful clouds, birds, foliage, people, or atmospheric details to give the scene life without creating clutter.

FOREGROUND
Create a strong local foreground element — such as a street, boat, train, tram, cable car, road, waterfront, vegetation, or characteristic landscape — with painterly shadows, reflections, and tiny human details.

FINAL AESTHETIC
A unified hand-painted travel illustration that feels like a premium vintage tourism poster from a collectible series. Strong hierarchy, layered depth, recognizable silhouettes, balanced asymmetry, elegant typography, and a clear sense of place.

Avoid generic tourist imagery, unrelated landmarks, collage-like composition, excessive tiny details, 3D rendering, glossy CGI, photorealism, modern digital effects, logos, watermarks, and clutter.

FORMAT
3:4 vertical, single cohesive illustration, premium print quality, sophisticated vintage travel-poster finish.
