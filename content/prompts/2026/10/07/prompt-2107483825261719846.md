## Prompt

Create a Cannes-level luxury skincare poster for fictional winter-repair beauty brand VEILORÉ, merging macro skin realism, translucent treatment texture, premium product design, and gallery-clean editorial typography into one unified hero image. The core concept is winter recovery made visible: ultra-close female facial fragments become a tactile landscape of dry-season skin, softly covered by translucent treatment veils, while one serum bottle stands at the center as the source of repair, protection, and quiet radiance. The product must remain the absolute visual leader.

Use a vertical full-bleed beauty poster with an extreme macro editorial composition, disciplined by rule of thirds but centrally anchored. Preserve the exact structure: one closed eye in the upper third, partial cheek and nose bridge through the middle, soft natural lips in the lower third, all cropped into abstract skin planes. Across these zones, a sheer semi-transparent hydration veil stretches in overlapping lightly wrinkled layers, softly luminous and revealing the skin beneath. At the exact center, place one large upright serum bottle, fully readable and integrated into the facial landscape without breaking the emotional flow. Typography sits in a refined centered stack around the bottle. The composition must feel intimate, tactile, sculptural, and controlled.

Show one realistic adult woman only, presented as close-cropped facial fragments rather than a full portrait. Her skin must feel de-AI and physiologically believable: visible pores, subtle winter dehydration texture, fine under-eye creasing, soft tonal variation, realistic lashes, believable lip texture, and natural elasticity. No heavy makeup, no glossy fashion artifice. The treatment veil should feel like a serum-soaked cosmetic membrane, breathable and delicate, softening the skin while preserving real detail. The beauty language should sit between clinical repair and couture skincare.
