## Prompt

Road workers repaint a zebra crossing in the middle of the night, their floodlights turning the dark street into a stage of sharp white stripes, wet asphalt and long shadows. Buckets, rollers and reflective jackets add rhythm to the scene while the repeated lines of the crossing become the main visual structure. Tempera-and-pastel painting style, matte layered colour, strong graphic composition, crisp edge contrast and a moody urban atmosphere.
