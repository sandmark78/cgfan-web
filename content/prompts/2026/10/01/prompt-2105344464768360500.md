## Prompt

A Cannes-level fried chicken campaign poster for fictional premium fast-food brand GOLDEN ROOST, merging emotional appetite, material precision, and curatorial graphic control into one unified 2x2 social-ad grid. Preserve the exact structure: four rounded-corner square cards on a pale buttery-cream background, each set on a saturated red-to-orange studio field with the same mascot-and-product universe, product-first hierarchy, bold headline zones, and tiny bottom information strips. The food remains the absolute visual leader across all four panels.

Top-left panel: a fashion-forward anthropomorphic rooster mascot lounges beside an oversized branded bucket overflowing with hyperreal fried chicken, wearing tactile orange street-luxury outerwear and tinted sunglasses. Top-right panel: a metal tray loaded with fried chicken pieces and fries is held from both sides by entering hands, tight and direct, with a bold headline above. Bottom-left panel: replace the black person with an adult Western white girl, photoreal and internationally commercial, joyfully biting into a large piece of fried chicken; she has fair or lightly sun-kissed skin, realistic pores, natural lips and teeth, soft hair, elegant hands with five normal fingers, correct anatomy, warm orange-red styling, and genuine delight. Bottom-right panel: the rooster mascot returns larger in the foreground while a branded bucket of fried chicken tenders erupts upward in a controlled burst of crumbs and crisp fragments.

Create a fully original English-only brand system. Use a small GOLDEN ROOST logo in the upper area of each panel, with short original headlines such as “The Crunch Worth Waiting For.”, “Hungry Yet?”, “Bite Into Joy.”, and “Fresh. Loud. Ready.” Keep typography bold, rounded, well spaced, and integrated into each card without covering the hero food. Add minimal order-strip information at the bottom edge of each card only as graphic structure.
