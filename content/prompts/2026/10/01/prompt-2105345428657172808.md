## Prompt

An ensemble poster becomes iconic when the product hierarchy behaves like a stage.
✨ one central hero pack elevated on the vertical axis, framed by a radiant halo; secondary packs mirrored at lower scale on both sides
The hero reads first as the lead figure. The range feels expansive without becoming a row of equally loud packages.
🪿 use ornate framing, medallions, and smoke ribbons as a symmetrical border system, leaving the pack fronts clean and unobstructed
The decoration creates ceremony around the products instead of competing with their labels. The result feels mythic but still shelf-ready.
