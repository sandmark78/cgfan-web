## Prompt

Create a premium 4:5 vertical travel-editorial artwork for Dubai × Venezia — a poetic fusion of Dubai, United Arab Emirates and Venice, Italy.

PURE GENERATION.
Invent the complete artwork from the written destination or subject alone. The written destination is the only input. Create every visual element from your own knowledge and imagination.

MASTER STYLE LOCK:
Hand-painted watercolor on warm cream textured paper. Minimalist travel-editorial poster. Transparent watercolor blended with soft gouache. Soft imperfect brushwork, visible paper grain, organic painted edges, muted natural colors, generous negative space, quiet nostalgic atmosphere, sophisticated handcrafted art-book quality. Flat illustrated artwork with painterly depth. No 3D rendering, no glossy finish, no cartoon style, no digital photorealism.

DESTINATION INTERPRETATION:
Automatically identify the strongest visual identity of Dubai and Venice and merge their most recognizable characteristics naturally into one cohesive destination story. Combine the soaring modern presence of the Burj Khalifa and Dubai skyline with the historic charm of a traditional Arabian souk, warm lanterns, palm trees, patterned textiles, spices, pottery, and local market details. Blend these elements organically with the Venetian atmosphere of narrow historic canal streets, elegant old European buildings, arched bridges, flower-filled balconies, Venetian architecture, calm reflective water, wooden mooring poles, and traditional black gondolas.

Create ONE unified scene rather than a collection of separate elements. The fusion should feel believable, artistic, and naturally composed rather than like two separate pictures placed together.

COMPOSITION:
Use an elegant vertical editorial composition with one dominant focal point and several smaller supporting details. Establish clear foreground, middle ground, and background layers using watercolor washes and simplified shapes.

Place a graceful Venetian-style canal flowing through the central foreground and middle ground, with several elegant black gondolas moving quietly along the water. Let the canal lead the eye toward a historic architectural passage and softly glowing buildings. In the distant background, reveal the unmistakable Burj Khalifa rising dramatically above the city, creating a poetic contrast between Dubai's futuristic skyline and Venice's timeless architecture.

Frame the scene with traditional Arabian market stalls and warm wooden shopfronts on the lower sides, displaying colorful woven carpets, patterned textiles, ceramic vessels, brass lanterns, spices, dates, dried fruits, and handcrafted objects. Add subtle Venetian balconies, arched windows, flower boxes, stone bridges, and old façades around the canal.

Use hanging lantern strings and delicate star-shaped lights above parts of the market street, inspired by the Dubai reference, while allowing warm reflections to appear naturally across the canal water. Include a few palm trees near the market architecture and restrained Mediterranean greenery around the Venetian buildings.

Leave generous warm cream space around important forms. Keep the silhouette clean and immediately recognizable. Avoid overcrowding.

LOCAL STORY:
Show an authentic everyday moment connected to both destinations. Include subtle human activity only when it strengthens the story: people walking through the market, a local vendor arranging spices and pottery, travelers crossing a small stone bridge, a gondolier guiding a boat, people sitting quietly at a small café table, and visitors exploring the narrow passage.

Blend traditional Emirati/Arabian market life with the relaxed rhythm of Venetian canal life. Include people in natural, understated poses rather than making them the main subject.

Favor ordinary local atmosphere over generic tourism imagery.

LIGHT & ATMOSPHERE:
Automatically choose lighting that suits the destination and scene: soft golden late-afternoon light transitioning into a gentle warm evening glow. Use a dreamy sunset sky with pale peach, dusty blue, warm cream, muted lavender, and soft golden clouds.

Allow warm lanterns to glow subtly through the market while the Venetian canal catches delicate reflections of the surrounding architecture and evening lights. Keep the atmosphere peaceful, nostalgic, cinematic, and slightly dreamlike without becoming overly dramatic.

Use atmospheric watercolor washes to create depth while maintaining a calm, delicate feeling.

COLOR LANGUAGE:
Build the palette naturally from Dubai and Venice. Use warm cream, muted ochre, terracotta, dusty blue, soft green, faded red, warm grey, muted turquoise, antique gold, soft peach, and subtle golden tones where appropriate.

Use deeper warm browns and muted reds for Arabian textiles and market details, soft blue-grey and turquoise reflections for the Venetian water, pale stone tones for historic buildings, and restrained metallic gold for lanterns and architectural highlights.

Keep the colors slightly desaturated, harmonious, and naturally layered.

TYPOGRAPHY:
At the bottom of the artwork, add three restrained editorial text elements:

DUBAI × VENEZIA
WHERE DESERT DREAMS MEET FLOATING CITIES
A meeting of golden souks, timeless canals, and endless horizons.

Use elegant minimal typography with generous spacing. Keep it secondary to the artwork and ensure all wording is clean and correctly spelled.

FINAL AESTHETIC:
Premium handmade watercolor travel poster × contemporary editorial illustration × nostalgic travel memory × quiet everyday life × sophisticated art-book design.

The finished artwork should feel like a beautifully remembered meeting between two iconic worlds: Dubai's futuristic ambition and Arabian heritage flowing naturally into Venice's historic canals, gondolas, bridges, and timeless architecture. Make the result atmospheric, personal, refined, understated, and unmistakably connected to both destinations.

AVOID:
Any editing workflow, source material, references, existing compositions, copied imagery, collage construction, unrelated landmarks, generic scenery, excessive detail, oversaturated colors, harsh outlines, glossy digital effects, CGI, 3D objects, cartoon characters, clutter, excessive typography, logos, watermarks, interface elements.

FORMAT:
4:5 vertical portrait composition, warm cream paper, handcrafted watercolor texture, generous negative space, premium print-quality finish.
