## Prompt

ASPECT RATIO: 4:5 VERTICAL PORTRAIT. Create an ultra-detailed, photorealistic miniature diorama of an entire city contained inside a luxurious antique study. Show a complete, intricately constructed miniature metropolis with recognizable landmarks, skyscrapers, roads, bridges, tiny vehicles, parks, and pedestrians, surrounded by towering dark wooden bookshelves, antique globes, brass telescopes, framed historical maps, ornate Persian rugs, carved furniture, and a magnificent crystal chandelier. Warm golden lighting and soft daylight illuminate the miniature architecture, revealing realistic textures, tiny windows, intricate facades, and extraordinary handcrafted details. Capture the entire room from a wide-angle, slightly elevated viewpoint, balancing the miniature city in the foreground and middle ground with the elaborate antique interior in the background. Rich walnut wood, antique gold, deep burgundy, cinematic shadows, realistic scale contrast, architectural photography, museum-quality craftsmanship, exceptional depth and detail. The city must look physically built inside the room, not floating or digitally overlaid. Ultra-photorealistic, cinematic, high resolution, no cartoon styling, no illustration.
