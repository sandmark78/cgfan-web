## Prompt

Create an ultra-detailed miniature diorama of [PLACE / CITY / LANDMARK], transforming the real location into a tiny, handcrafted architectural model.

SCENE:
Recreate the destination’s most recognizable buildings, streets, transportation, surrounding landscape, and everyday activity with remarkable architectural accuracy. Include miniature vehicles, tiny people, trees, street furniture, and location-specific details to make the scene feel alive.

MINIATURE STYLE:
Make everything look like a meticulously constructed physical scale model, with realistic miniature materials, finely detailed architecture, tiny textures, and carefully crafted structures. Preserve the location’s authentic identity and distinctive features.

CAMERA & COMPOSITION:
Use an elevated three-quarter aerial view, showcasing the entire landmark and its surroundings. Apply a macro-photography aesthetic with shallow depth of field, a sharp central subject, and softly blurred distant areas. Maintain realistic proportions and convincing miniature scale.

LIGHTING & ATMOSPHERE:
Warm natural daylight, soft shadows, subtle atmospheric depth, cinematic color grading, and realistic material textures. The result should resemble professional tilt-shift photography of an elaborate architectural model.

Automatically interpret [PLACE / CITY / LANDMARK] and include its most iconic features, appropriate transportation, local architecture, and recognizable surroundings.

Avoid generic buildings, inaccurate landmarks, oversized objects, cartoon styling, excessive blur, plastic-looking textures, and distorted perspective.

FORMAT: Vertical 4:5 composition, high resolution, photorealistic miniature craftsmanship.
