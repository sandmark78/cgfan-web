## Prompt

The magnifying glass works when it enlarges appetite—not when it becomes a gimmick.
🔍 large magnifying glass entering from the lower left, enlarging one tight section of crispy chicken and glossy sauce with true optical magnification
You get a clear reason to look closer: sharper crumb, richer sauce, more texture. The food feels more tangible without being distorted.
🌯 upright wrap anchored along the right third, with the branded paper sleeve covering only the lower half and the open filling kept fully visible
The packaging builds brand presence while the exposed top still does the selling. Product, paper, and headline each keep their own territory.
