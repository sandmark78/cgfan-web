## Prompt

A vertical luxury swimwear advertising poster for the original resort brand “LUNARA COAST”. Build the image as a minimal product-detail fashion photograph structured by three layers: warm sunlit adult skin as the sculptural field, an ivory-white side-tie swimwear knot as the product hero, and compact black typography as the brand identity. The visual language is realistic analog resort photography: golden-hour side light, soft sea haze, tactile ribbed fabric, subtle salt-sheen skin, sharp knot structure, shallow depth, quiet negative space, and restrained editorial luxury.

Vertical 9:16 crop, close side-detail commercial framing from lower ribs to upper hip, shot from a respectful side angle at natural torso height. Focus on garment construction, tie function, fabric contact, and logo spacing, not private body parts. The body must clearly read as adult through mature proportions, realistic waist-to-hip transition, stable posture, and natural skin texture. No face required.

Place the adult model’s side torso as a warm vertical mass along the left two-thirds of the frame. The ivory side-tie knot sits slightly right of center as the sharpest focal point. Two cord loops and two long cord ends create a clean graphic system: one loop curves horizontally into pale ocean negative space, while the dangling cords fall nearly vertical toward the lower right. The ocean and cream sky are reduced to a soft blurred field on the right. Set the brand text in the upper-right negative space, compact and aligned. The poster should read as skin block, white tie structure, and black wordmark before anything else.
