## Prompt

A full-bleed vertical 2:3 overseas resort-sport swimwear advertising poster for the original brand LUMEN COURT, shot as realistic high-fashion outdoor photography with minimal commercial typography. The upper half is a large uninterrupted cobalt sky used as clean brand negative space.

A lightweight ivory lace-textured open sleeve cover-up rests off the shoulders as a styling layer, framing the swimsuit without hiding its construction.
