## Prompt

What looks casually tangled is actually one continuous line directing the entire poster.
✨ one continuous tagliatelle strand descending from fork to base, with believable torsion and weight
This gives the composition a spine. Instead of scattered pasta, you get one readable movement that pulls the eye from the fork, through the headline, to the lemon finish.
🧊 pasta wrapping around letter stems with front-back occlusion and contact shadows
The key is not “pasta on typography.” It’s precise hide-and-reveal: a ribbon disappears behind a letter, catches light in front of the next one, and makes the type feel physically occupied.
