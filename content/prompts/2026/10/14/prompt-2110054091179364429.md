## Prompt

💚 monumental ring pushed close to the lens, photographed from an ultra-low upward angle
This turns the ring into the foreground horizon of the image. Its curve fills the frame, its gemstones catch the strongest light, and the product reads before the viewer even registers the model.
✨ model placed behind the ring, slightly softer, with one relaxed hand resting on its upper arc
The model gives the object human scale without competing for attention. A calm hand also proves the ring’s weight and surface—provided the fingers stay clean, relaxed, and fully believable.
