## Prompt

Create a premium vertical poster ad for a fictional Mediterranean dairy maison, "SOLIVIA". The absolute hero is a real glass jar of whipped labneh, centered in the lower third on a sunlit carved limestone pedestal, with the front label fully readable. Build the image as a Cannes-level food campaign where product and serving ritual become one sculptural gesture: a dense ribbon of whipped labneh rises directly from the open jar, twists upward in a refined continuous S-curve, and lifts a freshly folded flatbread wrap at the crest as if the cream itself carries the bite into the air.

Use a slightly low-angle luxury product shot, with the jar large and dominant, the cream ribbon on the central vertical axis, and the flatbread suspended in the upper-left quadrant. The jar must remain the heaviest visual object. The cream arc should feel controlled, elegant, and physically plausible, with soft peaks, tensile flow, and subtle directional momentum rather than chaotic splash energy. The folded bread should show one clean appetizing bite revealing whipped labneh, tomato slices, and green herbs inside. Keep it warm-toned, lightly blistered, soft yet structured, with believable crumb and toasted surface texture.

The jar must feel collectible and shelf-ready: thick clear glass, precise rim thickness, premium printed label, clean open mouth, dense luminous white labneh, and subtle internal cream texture visible through the glass. The front label must read exactly "SOLIVIA" and "Whipped Labneh". Branding should feel globally premium and Mediterranean, using deep cobalt blue, soft cream white, and restrained olive-gold accents. The jar should look cool, fresh, and tactile without frost or messy condensation.
