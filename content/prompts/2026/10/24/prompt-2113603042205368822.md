## Prompt

Zidan 子丹
The Winter of 1998

It was snowing heavily that winter.

You came looking for me, asking me to take some photographs of you. You grabbed my hand and dragged me up the hill behind our university. All the way there, you kept kicking snow into the air, laughing like a little girl. I still remember how happy you were.

The following spring, just after the New Year, we broke up on the university playground. You walked away in tears. I stood there alone, in the middle of that empty field.

I told an ant a secret.

But she whispered it to a dandelion.

And now,

every breeze across that old playground

carries the news

that you and I

are no longer together.
