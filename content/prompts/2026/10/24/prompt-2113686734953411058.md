## Prompt

This works because the poster turns shadow into the real motion system.

🏁`strict overhead view, top-cropped race car, solitary driver walking away through vast white negative space` This makes the departure feel quiet and cinematic. The car becomes what he leaves behind.
⚫`long diagonal shadows, white racing suit with black technical panels, helmet carried low at the side` This keeps the product readable without adding noise. You see suit construction, scale, and post-race mood at once.
