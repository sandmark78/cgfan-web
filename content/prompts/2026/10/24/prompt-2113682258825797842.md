## Prompt

A full-bleed vertical luxury footwear and hosiery poster for the original overseas brand "NOCTELLE", merging a controlled press-call narrative with precise footwear and sheer-tights detail. The hero products are the NOCTELLE "VELVET STEP HEEL" and "PRESSLINE TIGHTS". The style is clean white-studio fashion photography with a reduced, asymmetrical media setup: only a few cropped cameras and microphones enter the frame, all directing attention to the model’s legs and black satin heel without visual chaos.

Medium full-body studio framing on a white seamless background, slightly low eye-level. The adult model stands centered but slightly right. One leg bears weight; the other bends upward so the black heel, ankle strap, and sheer tights become the main readable product zone. The model wears a minimal black one-shoulder mini dress. Her lifted leg creates a diagonal from lower left toward center, placing the shoe near the lower-left focal area. One hand hovers near the lifted shoe without touching it; the other rests naturally along the dress. Her head turns slightly toward the right-side microphones, expression calm and editorial.

Hero products: NOCTELLE "VELVET STEP HEEL", a black satin low-heel slingback with a pointed almond toe, slim elastic back strap, subtle curved vamp, and small sculpted heel; paired with NOCTELLE "PRESSLINE TIGHTS", sheer black tights with even transparency, smooth knee tension, and soft matte finish. The shoe must show controlled satin highlights, crisp edges, and a clean heel silhouette. The tights must reveal natural skin tone through dark sheer fabric, especially at the knee, calf, and ankle.
