## Prompt

Create a premium, ultra-realistic 3D typography poster featuring the exact word "[YOUR TEXT]", sculpted entirely from thick, fluffy wool yarn.

MAIN TYPOGRAPHY
Form the letters into chunky, bold, tightly packed shapes crafted from soft, off-white cotton fibers. Give each letter a dense, plush, tactile appearance with intricate individual strands, realistic fiber variation, and subtle natural irregularities. Mount the typography on a vibrant red knitted rectangular patch featuring clearly visible stitches, fuzzy edges, raised yarn patterns, and an authentic woven texture.

Ensure the text is spelled exactly as provided, with every letter clearly distinguishable, correctly formed, and fully readable. The typography must remain the dominant visual element.

BACKGROUND & LIGHTING
Use a pure black background with generous negative space around the central composition. Apply dramatic studio lighting to emphasize the contrast between the creamy white yarn, vivid red knitted patch, and deep black surroundings. Create realistic contact shadows, subtle ambient occlusion, dimensional highlights, and convincing depth.

EDITORIAL DETAILS
Add small, minimalist, monospaced white typography in the top-right corner, arranged neatly like a refined editorial design annotation. In the bottom-left corner, include a tiny, charming illustrated mascot accompanied by a short, understated descriptive caption. Keep both elements secondary, precisely aligned, and visually unobtrusive.

MATERIAL REALISM
Render every material with exceptional tactile accuracy, including individual wool fibers, soft fuzz, tightly looped stitches, subtle textile imperfections, and realistic light interaction. The result should feel like a meticulously handcrafted textile artwork photographed in a professional macro studio.

COMPOSITION & OUTPUT
Vertical 4:5 aspect ratio. Centered composition, balanced visual hierarchy, generous negative space, crisp focus on the main typography, high contrast, sophisticated editorial art direction, premium experimental graphic design, photorealistic 3D rendering, and exceptionally detailed materials.

FINAL REQUIREMENT: Prioritize exact spelling, immediate readability, realistic knitted construction, and a striking visual contrast. Avoid extra words, distorted letters, clutter, excessive decorative elements, or anything that competes with the main text.
