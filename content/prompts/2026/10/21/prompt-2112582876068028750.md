## Prompt

Create a Cannes-level commercial food poster for a fictional premium Korean claypot brand, centered on a hyper-real hero product: a steaming black earthen casserole of rich golden tofu stew, presented as a tactile, appetite-driven luxury food object. Preserve the exact chiaroscuro hierarchy, object placement, vertical poster rhythm, hand-to-food interaction, steam movement, negative-space structure, and typographic balance of a dramatic single-pot food ad. The food must remain the absolute visual center.

Vertical 2:3 full-bleed poster. A heavy black earthen pot sits frontally in the lower half on a clean wooden stand over a warm wood tabletop. A human hand enters from the upper right, holding a long pale ladle diagonally downward toward center, lifting one spoonful of tofu and broth above the pot. Keep the spoonful suspended in the upper-middle zone, aligned with rising steam. Reserve the upper-left quadrant for vertical Korean title typography and compact copy blocks. Background remains a deep near-black brown field with controlled empty space to amplify steam and product silhouette.

Invent the brand as 온담, a premium Korean claypot dining brand. The hero dish is 황금 순두부 전골, a luxurious golden tofu casserole with thick savory broth, soft tofu cubes, and slow-simmered depth. The pot must feel authentic, weighty, handmade, slightly weathered, with kiln-fired texture, matte charcoal glaze, heat marks, and tactile rim detail. The lifted spoonful should show large tender tofu pieces coated in glossy golden broth, with one small drip falling back toward the pot. The food must look genuinely edible, hot, rich, and meticulously styled.
