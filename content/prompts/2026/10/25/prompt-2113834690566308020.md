## Prompt

Create a charming, ultra-detailed 3D crochet miniature weather card for [CITY / COUNTRY], featuring the date 24 October 2026 and the local temperature range [MIN–MAX °C].

SCENE:
Transform the destination into a whimsical handmade yarn world. Recreate its most recognizable landmarks, traditional architecture, streets, shops, and cultural details as miniature knitted and crocheted structures. Include tiny yarn-crafted people in locally appropriate clothing, market stalls, trees, vehicles, and everyday activities.

CROCHET AESTHETIC:
Everything should appear handcrafted from soft wool, chunky yarn, and delicate crochet stitches. Use rounded shapes, visible yarn loops, plush figures, embroidered details, and intricate miniature craftsmanship.

WEATHER:
Represent the actual local weather with crocheted elements such as a golden yarn sun, fluffy wool clouds, raindrops, or snowflakes, matching the conditions for 24 October 2026. Display the date and temperature range clearly at the top.

COLOR & LIGHTING:
Use a palette inspired by the destination, warm natural lighting, soft shadows, gentle depth of field, and a cozy storybook atmosphere.

COMPOSITION:
Vertical 4:5 format, immersive miniature diorama, recognizable landmarks, lively foreground details, and balanced architectural layering.

Automatically adapt the scenery, cultural details, clothing, and weather symbols to the chosen destination.

Avoid plastic-looking materials, generic architecture, clutter, incorrect text, watermarks, and logos.

STYLE: Premium handcrafted crochet art, cozy miniature world, collectible 3D weather card.
