## Prompt

Fine jewelry can command a poster without getting bigger. Give its smallest details a quiet surface to shine against.
🌑 Midnight-navy matte-satin bust, softly modeled contours, subdued surface reflections.
The face and hand retain their sculptural shape without bright glare competing with the delicate chains.
✨ Crisp warm reflections on individual gold links, tiny white glints confined to the stones.
Each chain becomes a readable gold line against the navy. Keeping the sparkle small preserves the jewelry’s fine scale.
