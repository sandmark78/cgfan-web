## Prompt

A Cannes-level luxury jewelry campaign poster for a fictional fine-jewelry house, AURICHE, featuring two hyper-real heritage bangles as the absolute hero. The image must unify nocturnal atmosphere, heirloom-grade material richness, and curator-level restraint. Two exquisitely crafted bangles hang from a sculptural dry branch in a moonlit desert night, suspended in the center-right with commanding clarity and luminous presence. The environment should feel ancient, intimate, and cinematic, but the jewelry remains the undisputed focus.

Use a vertical poster layout, asymmetrical but balanced. A weathered branch enters from the upper-right and extends diagonally toward the center, naturally supporting the two bangles. The jewelry carries the main visual weight in the center-right, while the upper-left and middle-left remain dark breathable negative space for typography. A small elegant crescent moon sits in the upper-left sky. The lower frame contains a restrained desert foreground with fine sand texture and a subtle winding light path guiding the eye toward the jewelry. Keep the composition sparse, premium, and sculptural, with no clutter.

The hero pieces are two collectible heirloom bangles in antique champagne-gold finish, richly engraved with floral and geometric motifs, micro-beaded borders, and inset ruby-red and emerald-green cabochons. They must feel dense, precious, and hand-finished, with immaculate circular form, believable weight, precise carving, crisp bezels, polished inner curvature, and subtle tonal variation. One bangle hangs slightly forward, the other slightly behind, creating elegant overlap while keeping both fully legible. The design should communicate ceremonial beauty, generational permanence, and refined old-world craftsmanship.
