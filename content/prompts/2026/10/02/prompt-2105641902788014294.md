## Prompt

A luxury fragrance campaign poster for fictional perfume house AURÉLUNE, merging emotional softness, material precision, and curatorial abstraction into one unified three-tier olfactory image. Preserve the exact structure: three horizontal stacked fragrance-note panels, each built around the same premium perfume bottle as the absolute visual leader, with distinct ingredient worlds for top, heart, and base notes. The composition is clean, editorial, luminous, and note-driven, with each band reading like one chapter of the scent while the bottle remains central and dominant throughout.

Top panel: a warm amber-honey atmosphere with a smooth golden gradient background and a thin stream of glossy honey descending into the bottle zone. The bottle stands centered and sharply lit, with translucent honey glow and soft reflections. Add the centered heading “TOP.” with a smaller line such as “honey veil”. The mood is bright, molten, and seductive.

Middle panel: the bottle sits between or before a giant ripe peach split open to reveal juicy flesh and velvety skin, framed by warm peach-gold bokeh. The peach is oversized, tactile, softly glistening, and sensual, with visible fuzz, moist flesh, and natural fibers. Add the centered heading “HEART.” with a smaller line such as “sun peach”. This band is lush, ripe, and intimate, while the bottle stays the hero.
