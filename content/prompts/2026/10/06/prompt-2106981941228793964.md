## Prompt

Create a premium vintage Indian postage-stamp travel poster for [COUNTRY / CITY / LOCATION / SUBJECT].

TEXT-TO-IMAGE ONLY — generate the entire artwork creatively from [COUNTRY / CITY / LOCATION / SUBJECT] alone. No reference image required.

CORE CONCEPT
Transform the destination into a collectible vintage postage stamp, presenting its most iconic architecture, culture, landscape, and local character as a finely engraved travel illustration.

STAMP DESIGN
Create ONE centered postage stamp with authentic perforated edges and a subtle cream paper texture. Automatically select the most recognizable landmark or combination of visual elements that represents the destination.

Use detailed engraved linework, delicate crosshatching, vintage print texture, and slightly imperfect ink registration for an authentic old-world postal aesthetic.

INDIAN IDENTITY
Give the stamp a refined Indian postal character with:
- “भारत INDIA” as the header
- ₹100 denomination
- destination name in elegant Latin lettering
- the destination name again in its appropriate regional script

Adapt the regional script and cultural details accurately to the chosen location.

COLOR & COMPOSITION
Use a restrained destination-inspired palette with 3–5 complementary vintage colors. Keep the cream paper visible around the artwork and maintain strong visual hierarchy.

Include only meaningful local details — architecture, landscape, traditional motifs, vegetation, transportation, or cultural elements that genuinely belong to the destination.

STYLE
Vintage Indian postage stamp × engraved illustration × heritage printmaking × collectible travel ephemera.

The result should feel authentic, minimal, timeless, sophisticated, and museum-worthy rather than like a modern digital poster.

Avoid photorealism, glossy rendering, excessive colors, generic landmarks, invented cultural details, clutter, modern graphic effects, multiple separate stamps, logos, watermarks, and excessive text.

FORMAT
One stamp per image, vertical composition, perforated border, cream paper texture, highly detailed engraving, elegant typography, premium collectible finish.
