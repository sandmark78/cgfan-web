## Prompt

A travel poster feels like an invitation when the road does the storytelling.
🌿 “one narrow ochre road, continuous from the safari vehicle in the foreground through the forest to the distant waterline”
This gives the eye one unbroken route to follow. The landscape stops feeling like an aerial view and starts feeling reachable.
✨ “keep the road warm and legible against cooler jungle greens; let it narrow gradually into atmospheric haze”
The road reads clearly without overpowering the terrain, and its changing scale makes the wilderness feel vast.
