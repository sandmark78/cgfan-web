## Prompt

Create a premium 4:5 vertical on-location travel sketch of [COUNTRY / CITY / LOCATION / LANDMARK], drawn in a small artist’s watercolor sketchbook.

TEXT-TO-IMAGE ONLY — generate the entire scene creatively from [COUNTRY / CITY / LOCATION / LANDMARK] alone. No reference image required.

CORE CONCEPT
Capture the chosen landmark as a spontaneous sketch made directly on location by a skilled travel artist. Preserve its recognizable silhouette, proportions, architecture, and defining features, but interpret them with quick, economical marks rather than fully rendering every detail.

COMPOSITION
Show ONE natural street-level viewpoint, choosing the most characteristic perspective for the landmark. Include only a small amount of surrounding architecture, greenery, street elements, and a few tiny gestural figures where appropriate.

Let the drawing naturally dissolve into untouched white paper around the edges. Keep the landmark as the clear focal point while allowing parts of the scene to remain unfinished.

SKETCH STYLE
Use loose observational urban-sketch techniques:
- light wandering graphite construction lines
- occasional confident dark ink accents
- quick transparent watercolor washes
- visible brushstrokes, blooms and pigment overlaps
- irregular, broken and doubled lines
- simplified architectural details
- areas where watercolor crosses or escapes the linework
- generous untouched paper

Concentrate detail around the landmark’s most recognizable architectural features and allow secondary areas to fade into suggestive marks.

COLOR
Use a small, restrained watercolor palette naturally derived from the real landmark and its surroundings. Keep the paper predominantly white and allow subtle variations, uneven washes, and accidental edges to remain visible.

FINAL AESTHETIC
Authentic travel sketchbook × European urban sketching × loose watercolor × graphite observation × handmade artistic spontaneity.

The image should feel like a beautiful 20–30 minute sketch made while sitting in front of the landmark — observant, imperfect, intimate, and alive.

Avoid photorealism, polished architectural rendering, overly precise linework, fully painted backgrounds, uniform detail, digital smoothness, heavy shading, excessive colors, decorative borders, text, logos, and watermarks.

FORMAT
4:5 vertical, single cohesive sketchbook page, natural street-level perspective, white paper, loose unfinished edges, expressive watercolor and graphite texture.
