## Prompt

Create a Cannes-level four-panel hospitality campaign poster set for fictional Creole cocktail house ROUGE BAYOU, merging hyper-real food photography, nightlife atmosphere, editorial typography, and luxury restaurant branding into one unified 2x2 campaign grid. The set must feel like one coherent social-media and poster-ready system, not four separate ads. Preserve the exact structure: top-left food hero, top-right cocktail hero, bottom-left menu spread, bottom-right interior mood. All four panels must share one brand world, one logo family, one footer rhythm, and one palette of deep black, crimson red, ember orange, and warm gold.

Overall art direction: premium nightlife hospitality identity, bold but clean, seductive but controlled, with products always leading the frame. Use a consistent circular logo mark reading “ROUGE BAYOU” with the descriptor “CREOLE & COCKTAIL HOUSE”. Typography across the set combines three coordinated layers: elegant handwritten script for emotional words, tall condensed sans-serif for major impact words, and a clean small sans-serif for support details, footer metadata, and menu lists. All copy must be original English, commercially believable, and tightly integrated. The mood is urban, warm, intimate, stylish, and high-energy.

Top-left panel, food hero: a dramatic close food photograph of a premium Creole seafood plate in shallow depth of field, featuring richly seared blackened fish or salmon over seasoned rice, glossy sauce, charred edges, fresh herbs, and a warm lemon wedge. The background is a moody dark restaurant blur with red-black ambience. Logo near the top. Main headline: “SOUTHERN FIRE / CREOLE SOUL”, with one keyword oversized as the bold anchor. Supporting copy suggests bold Creole dishes, crafted cocktails, and after-dark warmth. Footer includes refined address rhythm, opening hours, and subtle icon markers.
