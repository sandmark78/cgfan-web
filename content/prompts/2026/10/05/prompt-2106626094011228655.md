## Prompt

Create a highly aesthetic, cinematic poster of [LANDMARK] transformed into an unmistakable October atmosphere — rich burnt orange, deep rust, muted amber, and warm brown tones, crisp autumn air, swirling maple leaves naturally framing the landmark, soft golden-hour sunlight, subtle mist, cozy street lamps glowing, wet stone streets reflecting warm light, a few scattered pumpkins and autumn details integrated tastefully into the environment.

Keep the landmark instantly recognizable and visually dominant, while making the entire scene feel like a frame from a premium autumn movie. Add subtle film grain, realistic paper texture, atmospheric depth, dramatic shadows, sophisticated editorial composition, and a slightly nostalgic 35mm photography feel.

No Halloween clichés, no cartoon look, no excessive decorations. Make it elegant, cinematic, mysterious, and highly shareable — the kind of October visual that feels like “the landmark entered its autumn era.”

Minimal refined typography: “OCTOBER” with a small location name underneath. Vertical 4:5 poster composition, premium travel-editorial aesthetic, viral Pinterest/Instagram visuals
