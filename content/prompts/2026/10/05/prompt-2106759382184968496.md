## Prompt

Create a Cannes-level commercial beer poster for fictional premium lager brand AURIC DRAFT, merging explosive beverage realism, sculptural liquid motion, and gallery-clean advertising precision into one unified hero image. The core concept is release under pressure: a single uncapped glass beer bottle stands exactly centered, freshly opened, cold, glowing, and dominant, while a powerful golden beer burst expands horizontally behind the neck like a controlled crown of energy. The bottle must remain the absolute visual leader.

Use a vertical full-bleed poster with a front-facing centered hero shot, strong symmetry, and disciplined rule-of-thirds balance. Preserve the exact structure: one dominant long-neck bottle in the middle, a horizontal liquid explosion behind the upper bottle, a luminous dark-to-gold backlit field, and a bright crystalline ice base in the lower foreground. The bottle stands perfectly upright, centered, and slightly elevated out of the ice bed, with generous negative space around the shoulders and burst edges to maximize readability and luxury restraint. The image must feel immediate, iconic, and high-impact from distance.

Design a premium long-neck lager bottle filled with radiant golden beer, built with thick chilled glass, subtle embossing, precise curvature, dense condensation droplets, and a custom label system in brushed charcoal, muted cream, and aged gold. The fictional brand is “AURIC DRAFT” and the signature line is “COLD UNLEASHED”. The bottle must be fully uncapped, with no cap attached and no floating cap anywhere in the frame. The glass mouth must be clearly visible, showing a cold wet inner rim, precise glass lip thickness, and a just-opened breath of foam and carbonation. The label should feel original, elegant, and unlike any real beer brand.
