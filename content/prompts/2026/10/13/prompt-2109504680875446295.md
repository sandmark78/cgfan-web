## Prompt

Create a stunning collectible art poster featuring [CITY / COUNTRY / LANDMARK], illustrated entirely with expressive colored-pencil strokes and rich wax-crayon textures on warm, textured paper.

THE CENTRAL IDEA: One continuous, imperfect hand-drawn line travels across the composition, gradually forming the recognizable silhouette of [LANDMARK]. The line continues into surrounding architectural details, winding streets, distant mountains, trees, and tiny everyday scenes inspired by the real location.

Keep the landmark architecturally accurate and instantly recognizable. Use bold, imperfect pencil strokes, visible crayon layering, subtle scribbles, crosshatching, and beautiful areas of unfinished sketchwork.

DESIGN: Sophisticated editorial travel-poster composition, oversized central landmark, playful hand-drawn details, generous negative space, asymmetrical balance, and elegant vintage typography displaying the location’s name.

COLOR PALETTE: Limited but striking combination of cobalt blue, burnt orange, butter yellow, forest green, and warm ivory. Colors should feel hand-layered, tactile, and slightly imperfect.

Add tiny hand-drawn stars, directional arrows, abstract map marks, and miniature location-inspired symbols without overcrowding the design.

The final artwork should feel like a rare art-print poster created by an imaginative contemporary illustrator — modern, nostalgic, artistic, visually distinctive, and instantly eye-catching.

Portrait 4:5 composition, print-ready appearance, no photorealism, no 3D rendering, no glossy digital gradients, no generic AI fantasy elements, no watermark.
