## Prompt

UNIVERSAL PROMPT - RETRO POP ART COMIC COLLAGE PORTRAIT | 3:4

Using the uploaded photograph as the primary reference, transform the subject into a striking vintage pop-art comic book collage poster.

COMPOSITION: Create an intricate arrangement of multiple rectangular comic panels featuring different close-up crops of the same subject’s face, eyes, eyebrows, hair, ears, nose, lips, and facial details. Use varied panel sizes and orientations, separated by thick, bold black borders. Include a few small graphic panels with hand-drawn symbols, such as crossed-out eyes or a simple heart, for an authentic editorial collage aesthetic.

ART STYLE: Classic retro comic-book illustration, 1960s pop art, Japanese manga-inspired ink drawing, bold black linework, high-contrast shadows, fine halftone dots, vintage screen-print textures, subtle distressed paper grain, and hand-inked detailing.

COLOR PALETTE: Vivid scarlet red background, warm cream and pale beige skin tones, deep solid black ink, and minimal off-white highlights. Maintain a limited three-color print aesthetic with strong contrast.

REFERENCE ACCURACY: Preserve the subject’s recognizable facial features, hairstyle, facial hair, skin tone, and overall identity from the uploaded photograph. Every panel must depict the same person, with consistent facial characteristics. Use dramatic close-up crops and varied framing while keeping the portrait visually coherent.

LAYOUT & FINISH: Fill the canvas with an asymmetrical, tightly packed comic-panel grid. Use clean, thick black dividers, sharp graphic composition, authentic vintage print imperfections, and a bold underground-comic editorial mood. No photorealistic rendering; the entire portrait must look hand-drawn and printed.

OUTPUT: High-resolution, 3:4 vertical portrait poster, edge-to-edge composition, crisp ink details, professional graphic design, visually striking retro pop-art aesthetic. No watermark, no additional text, no logos.
