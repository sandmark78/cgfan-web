## Prompt

Create a premium vertical destination-poster ad for a fictional travel-pass brand, "AEROPASS BALI". The hero is a real collectible Bali travel pass ticket held in the foreground by a human hand, with the destination experience revealed through the ticket itself. The pass must remain the absolute visual leader: a tactile paper object, a gateway, and a curated memory artifact. Fuse product realism, destination storytelling, and editorial restraint into one clean, high-impact image.

Use a frontal, slightly top-down editorial product shot on a warm archival paper-beige background. A left hand enters from the lower-left, holding a horizontally oriented premium pass across the center. The pass occupies the main width and stays the sharpest, most dominant object. The scene should glow richer than the outer paper field but remain physically framed by the ticket.
