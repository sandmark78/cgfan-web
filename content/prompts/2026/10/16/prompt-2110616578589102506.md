## Prompt

Create a whimsical 4:5 vertical travel illustration of [COUNTRY / CITY / LOCATION], inspired by mid-century European storybook art.

Feature one iconic local landmark with slightly crooked architecture, playful proportions, and recognizable details. Add two or three authentic local elements, loosely connected into one charming scene.

Use lively black ink, scratchy pencil lines, rough charcoal shading, grainy colored pencil, and small opaque gouache accents. Let lines wobble, overlap, and remain imperfect.

Palette: Turquoise, vermilion red, charcoal black, and pure white. Keep the background completely white with generous irregular negative space.

Include one small animal traveler near the bottom, wearing a simple outfit and making a playful, location-inspired gesture. Keep it secondary to the architecture.

Create a spontaneous, asymmetrical composition with sparse local plants, expressive scale, and unmistakably handmade textures.

Avoid: Multiple landmark collages, beige or aged-paper backgrounds, polished vector art, glossy 3D, gradients, excessive detail, lettering, borders, and watermarks.

FORMAT: Full-frame 4:5 portrait, pure white background, tactile mid-century picture-book illustration.
