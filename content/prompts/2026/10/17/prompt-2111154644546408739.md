## Prompt

Create a premium vertical commercial poster for a fictional heritage-luxury retail destination brand, MIRAGE CHOWK. Build it as a product-first architectural installation where a monumental premium shopping bag dominates the center-right of a serene contemporary atrium. Its front face opens through a grand arched cutout into a luminous miniature old-city marketplace, contrasting modern retail minimalism with warm cultural depth. The image should feel like a real flagship launch campaign: collectible, refined, emotionally rich, and visually immediate, while keeping the giant branded bag as the absolute primary subject.

Use a vertical poster format at eye level to slightly low angle, with a large pale sky-blue negative-space field in the upper-left for typography. Preserve the structural rhythm: elegant headline block on the left, giant shopping bag slightly right of center, a hanging side tag near the upper-right handle, a small seated viewer in the lower-left foreground, sparse books and a coffee cup in the lower-right foreground, subtle plants at the margins, and a polished floor with soft reflections. The bag should feel monumental and calm, while the inner arch opening acts as the portal pulling the eye inward. Keep clear breathing room and a premium hierarchy.

MIRAGE CHOWK is a fictional heritage-forward retail landmark. The bag is warm ivory luxury paperboard with precise rigidity, tactile paper grain, soft matte coating, micro-embossed branding, delicate champagne-gold trim, and muted crimson woven handles. Its structure must feel physically perfect, with believable folds, thickness, edge tension, and subtle natural wear. The front arch opening is a sculptural retail window. Inside sits a curated miniature bazaar avenue: arcaded facades, carved balconies, floral vines, textile storefronts, jewelry displays, lanterns, food-street accents, and a distant monumental gate or palace-like landmark, all aligned on a central vanishing axis. The interior world should feel denser and warmer, but still disciplined so the bag remains dominant.
