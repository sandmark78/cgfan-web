## Prompt

Create a premium vertical commercial poster for a fictional overseas juice brand, "VELVABERRY". Design it as a Cannes-level product-first beverage ad where one strawberry juice bottle becomes both the hero package and the engine of the graphic system. The bottle must look soft, overfilled, and under delicious internal pressure, visually inflating into giant sculptural typography that bursts outward from its right side. The result should feel bold, playful, tactile, and highly branded, with clean luxury execution.

Use a close-up low-to-mid angle product composition against a deep black-to-burgundy gradient background with a concentrated crimson glow behind the product. Place one oversized bottle in the left-center foreground, slightly tilted, occupying most of the left half. The bottle body visibly swells and stretches toward the right, seamlessly transforming into a huge inflated slogan built from the same pressurized material language. Keep the lower-right reserved for a compact official info badge, and preserve enough negative space around the top and right edge for the enlarged typographic mass to breathe. The bottle remains the heaviest object even as the typography expands from it.

The hero bottle is a short rounded squeeze-style juice bottle with a ribbed cap, made of soft-touch semi-gloss plastic in saturated strawberry red with subtle berry-coral undertones.
