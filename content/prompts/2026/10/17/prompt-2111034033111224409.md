## Prompt

Create a premium vertical commercial poster for a fictional pastry brand called "AURELLO". Design it as a Cannes-level surreal food ad set on a Mars-like cocoa desert, with one giant chocolate-hazelnut filled croissant as the absolute visual hero. Preserve the core poster logic: warm reddish planetary terrain, a monumental croissant spanning the middle-left to center, a broken cocoa-rock cave fused to its right side, and thick molten chocolate-hazelnut filling pouring from the cave like glossy lava into a rich foreground pool. Add small distant moons, restrained white doodle accents, a bold logo block at the top center, and a compact campaign text block in the lower third. The croissant and molten center must dominate instantly.

The croissant must be hyper-real, oversized, and irresistible: deeply laminated flaky layers, golden-brown blistered crust, buttery sheen, airy honeycomb interior, and an abundant molten chocolate-hazelnut core. The cocoa-rock cave should feel sculpted from compressed cocoa soil and dark pastry crust, with subtle carved branding reading "AURELLO" and a smaller carved phrase, "DEEPER CORE". The molten filling must feel dense, silky, luxurious, and slow-flowing, with rich specular highlights, believable viscosity, and thick pooling behavior.

Build the environment as a stylized interplanetary dessert landscape: cracked terracotta-cocoa dust, scattered rocky fragments, broken chocolate curls, a few whole and halved hazelnuts, sparse crumbs, and two small distant moons or asteroid-like bodies in the warm sky. Add one tiny premium pastry cart in the lower-right foreground as a playful secondary accent, carrying miniature dessert elements, but keep it clearly subordinate. Use only a restrained number of doodle-style white appetite marks and discovery symbols around the croissant and molten flow.
