## Prompt

Prompt:

Use my uploaded female face as the ONLY identity reference for the main model. Preserve my exact facial identity and natural appearance — facial structure, skin tone, eyes, eyebrows, nose, lips, cheekbones, jawline, facial proportions, hairline, and natural skin texture. Keep me instantly recognizable. Do not beautify, reshape, feminize, masculinize, age, de-age, or alter my facial features.

Create a premium photorealistic luxury fashion-week editorial poster featuring me as the main female fashion model. Design it as a sophisticated contemporary magazine campaign rather than copying the reference exactly.

Main portrait: I am standing confidently in a modern upscale fashion setting, body turned slightly toward the camera while my face looks elegantly to the side. Shoulder-length soft wavy brunette hair with natural volume and subtle movement. Calm, confident high-fashion expression.

Outfit: Replace the beige suit with a soft ivory tailored oversized blazer, satin champagne camisole underneath, high-waisted wide-leg charcoal trousers, delicate gold layered necklace, small gold earrings, and an elegant structured burgundy handbag. Sophisticated minimalist styling, premium designer aesthetic.

Poster composition: Create a vertical editorial fashion poster with one large full/three-quarter-body hero portrait occupying the right side and two smaller editorial photographs arranged on the left side, showing different poses and close-up details of the same woman. Make each image feel like part of one professional fashion campaign.

Background: Warm ivory, muted taupe and subtle champagne tones, with elegant studio lighting and soft architectural shadows. Clean luxury aesthetic, refined and expensive-looking.

Typography: Add sophisticated editorial typography at the top:
"THE MODERN EDIT"
"FASHION WEEK 2026"

Smaller text:
"Presented by DigiGraphics"
"An Evening of Contemporary Style"
