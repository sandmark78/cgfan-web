Create a 9:16 vertical food editorial poster combining highly realistic food photography with bold, professionally designed typography.

Use a warm cream or ivory background, subtle paper texture, clean editorial composition and minimal props.

Keep [HEADLINE] large, rigid, sharp and readable. The typography acts as a real physical environment while the food reacts to it:

1. SET — chocolate hardens on contact with cold type
2. SIZZLE — butter melts and foams on hot letters
3. POOL — olive oil gathers inside the counters of the type
4. RISE — brioche dough expands into every available gap

Really like where this food physics series is going.

Great for food branding, editorial posters and visual prompt experiments.
