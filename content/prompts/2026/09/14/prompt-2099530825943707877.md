## Prompt

Create a 9:16 vertical poster series in English, with a high-end editorial style inspired by brutalist architecture, monumental perspective typography, realistic human figures, and three-dimensional spatial graphics.

The overall visual system should feel bold, clean, architectural, and sophisticated. Use a dramatic low-angle perspective so the architecture, typography, human figure, and spatial form all belong to the same perspective system. Keep the buildings monumental and geometric, with raw warm-gray concrete, a deep cobalt-blue sky, warm ivory typography, charcoal clothing, and safety orange as the only accent color.

### Series Concepts

1. SKY PRISM — transparent planes in the air
2. CUT SPACE — a bold slice through the structure
3. TYPE SPAN — letters turned into a bridge
4. OPEN SEQUENCE — depth built frame by frame

### Typography Direction

Images 2.5 is getting really good at perspective typography! Once the type starts following the architecture, the whole poster feels much more alive.

1. HIGH EDGE — low-angle type
2. RAMP SIGNAL — sloped type
3. AERIAL ROOM — floating type
4. VOID CHANNEL — split typography

Great for architecture posters, editorial layouts, typography-led visuals, and concept design references.
