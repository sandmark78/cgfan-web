## Prompt

Create a premium vertical 4:5 editorial travel poster for [COUNTRY], where the outline of the country is formed organically from its landscapes, architecture, culture, and nature rather than using a normal map.

Inside the country silhouette, create a seamless cinematic journey:

* Top: iconic architecture emerging from clouds
* Middle: famous city skyline + traditional buildings
* Lower middle: dramatic natural landscape
* Bottom: wildlife, local crafts, food, flowers, or cultural details
* Small birds, boats, trains, or people can subtly connect the different scenes.

Instead of decorative borders, use one continuous architectural element—an ancient arch, carved doorway, stone wall, temple corridor, bridge, or traditional pattern—that visually holds the composition together.

On the side, use elegant editorial typography:

[COUNTRY NAME]
WHERE [SHORT POETIC PHRASE]

HISTORY • CULTURE • NATURE • CITIES • CUISINE

Add small details:
CAPITAL
AREA
FAMOUS FOR
BEST TIME TO VISIT
ICONIC PLACES

Use a "window into the country" concept: the country silhouette looks like a giant architectural doorway/window, and instead of showing separate boxes, the entire interior becomes one continuous surreal landscape.
