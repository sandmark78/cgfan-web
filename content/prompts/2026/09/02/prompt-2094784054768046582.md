## Prompt

Create a premium cinematic minimalist travel poster of [LOCATION] in a strict 4:5 vertical aspect ratio. Automatically adapt the scene to the destination's authentic geography, architecture, culture, climate, history, natural landmarks, colors, and atmosphere.

Show the most iconic and visually defining representation of [LOCATION] with one strong cinematic focal point, supported by subtle local details. Use a sophisticated editorial composition with generous negative space, balanced geometry, atmospheric depth, refined perspective, and minimal clutter.

Choose authentic landmarks, architecture, terrain, vegetation, people, clothing, and cultural elements appropriate to the location. Never mix unrelated regional styles.

Use cinematic natural lighting suited to the destination — sunrise, sunset, blue hour, mist, tropical light, desert dusk, alpine daylight, etc. Build a restrained 2–4 color palette inspired by the location, with muted sophisticated tones and natural contrast.

Integrate elegant Swiss/modern editorial typography:

[LOCATION] "A JOURNEY BEYOND THE ORDINARY"

Optional small details: coordinates, fine-line borders, map motifs, or subtle editorial labels.

Style: ultra-realistic cinematic travel photography, premium destination campaign, 35mm aesthetic, realistic textures, atmospheric haze, soft film grain, sophisticated color grading, photorealistic yet minimalist.

Mood: Elegant • Authentic • Cinematic • Aspirational • Quietly Luxurious

Avoid: generic stock imagery, inaccurate cultural elements, mixed architecture, distorted landmarks, clutter, oversaturation, cheesy tourism graphics, excessive text, fake-looking people, CGI/plastic appearance, watermark, logo, or misspelled text.
