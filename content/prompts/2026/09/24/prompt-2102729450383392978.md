## Prompt

A woman kneels on a long strip of canvas spread across a courtyard floor, carefully painting large nature symbols by hand with a wide brush while jars of pigment, rags and rough sketches lie around her. Afternoon light falls across the unfinished banner, making the painted strokes, splatters and folds of fabric the focus of the scene. Egg tempera painting style, matte luminous colour, crisp shapes, subtle surface texture and a calm focused atmosphere.
