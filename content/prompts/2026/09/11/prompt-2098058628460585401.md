## Prompt

Create a full-body 9:16 realistic fashion editorial featuring an adult young East Asian woman with a refined K-pop-inspired look, expressive eyes, a slim silhouette, and long balanced legs.

Use the uploaded image as a pose reference only. The model stands directly on real staircase steps, not on a concrete block, ledge, pedestal, or parapet.

One leg supports her weight naturally while the other bends backward, bringing the heel toward the body. One hand reaches back to lightly hold or adjust the heel, shoe opening, or ankle. This movement creates a natural twist through the waist and shoulders as she turns her head back toward the camera.

Keep strong eye contact with an expressive mood — playful, cool, confident, sensual, or slightly provocative — rather than a blank model expression.

Style the model with a fashion-forward mini dress or bodysuit featuring one clear design element, such as a deep-V neckline, sculpted bandeau, asymmetric shoulder, halter construction, or clean geometric cutout. Keep the outfit sharp and minimal rather than adding too many straps or decorative details.

Use one consistent hosiery color with no gradient.

The hosiery must naturally follow the thighs, knees, calves, ankles, and feet with believable stretch and tension.
