## Prompt

Use the uploaded real photo as the source.

Create a vertical 9:16 comparison image:

Top 50% = original photo
Bottom 50% = AI-filtered floating miniature world

Keep the landmark clearly recognizable. Preserve its main structure, silhouette, camera angle, composition, and key visual features.

Rebuild the filtered scene as a hand-painted Chinese floating island using a slightly isometric miniature perspective.

Place the main subject on a compact rocky island and simplify the surroundings into only the most important elements, such as stone platforms, stairs, ponds, rocks, pines, gardens, or pavilions.

Add a small amount of shallow water where appropriate, with 2–4 thin waterfalls flowing naturally from the island edges.

Use ink line outlines, light mineral colors, and a hand-painted traditional Chinese aesthetic.

Keep the composition clean, balanced, and visually readable.

One prompt turns real Chinese landmarks into hand-painted floating worlds.
