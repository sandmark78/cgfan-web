## Prompt

Brand: [BRAND NAME]
Food / Drink: [PRODUCT]
Headline: [2–4 WORD HEADLINE]
Accent Color: [COLOR]
Visual Interaction: [food replaces a letter / passes through type / refracts letters / becomes a divider / reveals hidden text]
Supporting Info: [PRICE / HOURS / INGREDIENTS / ADDRESS]
Aspect Ratio: 9:16

Create a premium editorial food poster for a modern café, bakery, or restaurant brand.

Use a warm cream or ivory background, generous negative space, oversized typography, photorealistic food photography, and a clean European editorial layout.

The key idea is that the food or drink physically interacts with the typography — it doesn't just sit next to it. Chocolate hardens on cold type, butter melts on hot letters, olive oil pools inside the counters, brioche dough grows into every available gap, cheese pull becomes part of the layout, glass refraction bends the letters.

Make the interaction feel natural and physical, not decorative.

Keep the overall composition minimal, elegant, and highly readable.

Great for café branding, menus, food campaigns, and social posts.
