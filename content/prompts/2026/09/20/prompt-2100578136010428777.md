## Prompt

Create one premium 4:3 landscape rubber-stamp travel journal poster for [COUNTRY / LOCATION / SUBJECT].

TEXT-TO-IMAGE GENERATION ONLY. Generate the entire artwork from [COUNTRY / LOCATION / SUBJECT] alone. No reference image or additional visual input is required.

COMPOSITION

Divide the canvas vertically into exactly two equal 50% sections with no visible divider, border, gutter, overlap, or before/after labels.

LEFT 50% — PHOTOGRAPHIC SCENE

Create a realistic, cinematic photograph representing [COUNTRY / LOCATION / SUBJECT].

Automatically determine its most recognizable subject, architecture, landscape, objects, people, animals, composition, lighting, colors, and atmosphere.

Give the scene a subtle aged archival-photography treatment: slightly faded colors, reduced saturation and contrast, warm vintage tones, fine film grain, faint paper texture, and a matte printed finish.

Keep the scene clearly photographic and natural.

RIGHT 50% — RUBBER-STAMP MEMORY

Transform the key visual identity of the scene into one compact hand-carved two-color rubber-stamp print.

Preserve the essential silhouette, structure, pose, orientation, horizon, and major foreground/background relationships so the subject remains immediately recognizable.

Simplify the scene aggressively—remove unnecessary crowds, tiny details, repetitive architecture, complex shading, and photographic textures.

Build the artwork entirely from solid ink areas, not outlines. Use one dark structural ink plus one or two muted colors inspired by [COUNTRY / LOCATION / SUBJECT].

Give the print authentic handmade imperfections: ragged carved edges, broken ink coverage, dry-ink gaps, uneven pressure, subtle pigment grain, slight paper bleed, and 1–2 mm color misregistration.

Turn every destination into a timeless memory - photographed, stamped, and preserved like a page from a traveler's journal.
