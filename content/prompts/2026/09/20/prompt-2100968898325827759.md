## Prompt

A hyper-real premium Asian noodle poster advertisement for a fictional restaurant brand called XINVERA HOUSE, designed as a flagship red-and-gold commercial key visual where product impact, series flexibility, and curated brand polish are fused into one unified composition. The absolute hero is a large black ceramic bowl of glossy beef soba noodles placed in the lower center, while a dramatic bundle of noodles is lifted vertically upward by dark wooden chopsticks near the upper right, creating the main force line of the poster. The lifted noodles must feel springy, weighted, silky, and richly seasoned, forming a strong visual cascade from the bowl into the upper brand zone.

The overall structure preserves a bold Chinese festive poster logic: a premium gold emblem and brand mark at the top center, a large expressive dish title on the upper left, the noodle lift traveling through the middle, a richly filled bowl grounding the bottom, and a tiny brand mascot near the chopsticks adding character without stealing focus. The red background should be built from layered tonal fields rather than flat color: deep crimson, vermilion, ember orange, and subtle cloud-scroll forms arranged in a more refined, graphic, flagship manner. The design must feel more controlled and more premium than a typical restaurant flyer, with clear hierarchy and broad breathing room.

Food design: the bowl contains premium soba noodles, glossy beef slices, soft-boiled egg halves, scallion curls, sesame seeds, nori strips, and a rich amber broth with visible oil sheen. Steam should be subtle and cinematic, not overwhelming. The noodle lift should have a sense of weight and elasticity, with seasoning clinging to the strands.

Typography: large expressive Chinese calligraphy for the dish title, paired with refined serif or sans-serif English text. The gold emblem should feel premium and minimal, not overly ornate.

Richness doesn't mean every red element gets equal contrast. Layer deep crimson, vermilion, and ember-orange fields with low-contrast cloud-scroll and architectural silhouettes. The background feels deep and ceremonial, but it stays behind the food instead of turning into visual noise. Reserve the brightest gold and strongest highlights for the dish title, glossy noodle lift, and lacquered beef. Your eye gets a clear route through the red: title first, noodles second, bowl third.
