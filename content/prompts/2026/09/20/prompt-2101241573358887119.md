# 微缩城市：3D立体模型旅行海报

**作者**: KrishnaG  
**日期**: 2026-09-19  
**评分**: 66/80

## Prompt

Premium luxury 3D miniature travel poster for [CITY_NAME], 3:4 vertical format.

Create an original, sophisticated destination poster combining a raised architectural diorama, elegant vintage travel typography, realistic miniature details, and a beautiful cityscape viewed from a wide three-quarter angle.

CITY IDENTITY
Build the scene specifically around [CITY_NAME], featuring:
- One iconic landmark as the main focal point.
- The city's recognizable river, lake, coastline, or natural landscape where appropriate.
- Local architecture, bridges, streets, and waterfront areas.
- One or two authentic transportation elements such as boats, trains, or trams.
- Small pedestrians and subtle everyday city activity.
- The entire destination should look like a carefully crafted miniature city model sitting on a thick dark stone base.

MAIN DIORAMA
Create a highly detailed raised 3D cityscape with:
- miniature buildings
- iconic landmark
- bridges
- waterways
- boats or local transport
- trees and landscaped areas
- tiny streetlights
- small pedestrians
- warm architectural lighting
- realistic water reflections

Use a wide elevated perspective so the city feels expansive while the stone base clearly shows the three-dimensional construction.

SEASON & ATMOSPHERE
Choose the season that best represents [CITY_NAME].
For winter destinations, use elegant snow-covered rooftops, snowy trees, cool blue water, and warm glowing windows.
For warmer destinations, use lush green trees, golden-hour sunlight, warm architecture, and calm reflective water.

TYPOGRAPHY
Place a small elegant tagline at the top, followed by:
[CITY_NAME]
in large sophisticated serif typography.
Below:
[COUNTRY]
Add one short destination-inspired phrase.
Include a small elegant brass or metallic plaque on the front of the stone base containing:
[CITY_NAME]
[COORDINATES]

ART DIRECTION
Luxury miniature architectural model, premium European travel poster, high-end tourism branding, realistic 3D diorama, elegant editorial design, detailed miniature city, refined architectural modeling, soft natural lighting, sophisticated postcard aesthetic.

COLOR SYSTEM
Use warm ivory and cream for the background, balanced with architectural neutrals, deep navy, soft blue, natural greens, muted gray, and subtle warm golden lighting.

MOOD
Timeless, peaceful, luxurious, refined, romantic, sophisticated, and atmospheric.
