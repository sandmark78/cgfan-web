## Prompt

Concept: BALLET OF LIGHT | Dance in the middle of light

There are movements that only exist for a moment, but are enough to become a work of art.

Inspired by Ballet, light and moving layers of chiffon, YUKI builds the photo set as a journey between reality and dreams. Each frame has its own rhythm: when the body rotates in the middle of the skirt blooming like petals, when seen from above like an installation, when small in the middle of the giant architecture, and when standing in front of the moon in the middle of the surreal landscape.

Light doesn't just shine on the character — it becomes a part of the movement. Ivory white tulle layers, slender borders and charcoal colors create a contrast between fragile and strong, stillness and movement, people and space.

A little ballet.
A bit of cinema.
A little surreal.
And a lot of space for imagination.
