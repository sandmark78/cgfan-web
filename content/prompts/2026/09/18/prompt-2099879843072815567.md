## Prompt

Create a Cannes-level ultra-premium athletic footwear advertising poster for a fictional performance brand called VOLTERRA MOTION, preserving the exact structural logic of a dramatic hero-shoe campaign while merging emotional power with forensic product realism and reducing all nonessential copy. The composition is vertical and highly controlled: one single black performance sneaker floats above a circular illuminated pedestal, lifted by a column of luminous magenta energy-smoke rising from the center, while a compact left-side editorial text block and one minimal bottom tagline complete the layout. The final image must feel like elite sports technology distilled into one precise, high-voltage icon.

Core composition: use a vertical poster with a near-black to deep magenta-black atmospheric background. In the center-right zone, place one large performance sneaker floating at a dynamic diagonal angle, slightly toe-forward, as if suspended in an activation field. Directly beneath it, place one low circular pedestal with premium industrial form and a glowing magenta core ring. From the center of the pedestal, a sculptural energy column rises upward in a twisting, elegant spiral, visually supporting the shoe while keeping its silhouette fully legible. Keep the rest of the frame clean and dark, with generous negative space and tightly controlled smoke spread.

Orbit atmosphere: the poster should feel like the exact moment a high-performance object is being awakened. The shoe is not simply displayed; it is being charged, elevated, and brought to life by contained energy. The magenta force beneath it should suggest speed, drive, and a ritual of activation, but the mood must remain controlled and luxurious rather than chaotic. The emotional tone is sharp, focused, and cinematic, as if the product has entered a state of engineered readiness.

Transit realism: render the sneaker with world-class commercial precision. The upper must show layered engineered mesh, matte-satin synthetic panels, rubberized toe reinforcement, structured lace loops, molded heel support, subtle reflective trims, and believable stitching or seam transitions. The sole should carry strong sculptural geometry, premium foam or rubber density, and a realistic tread structure that feels physically buildable. The pedestal should show fine metal finish, polished edge transitions, and subtle reflected magenta spill. The energy smoke must feel physically stylized yet materially believable, with translucent density, layered edge glow, volumetric curl, and brighter intensity near the pedestal and outsole contact zone.

Typography and layout: reduce the copy architecture while preserving the left-column logic. In the upper-left corner, place one small premium insignia or wordmark for VOLTERRA MOTION. Beneath it, use one bold stacked English headline such as "IGNITE / YOUR / MOTION." or "UNLOCK / PURE / POWER." Add only one short supporting line beneath it, such as "Engineered to elevate every stride." Remove any additional paragraph-style explanation. At the bottom center, include one final minimal tagline such as "Built for the relentless." The typography must feel modern, athletic, premium, and spacious, with no copy clutter.

Lighting: use dramatic studio lighting with controlled magenta rim light and soft cool-black fill. The shoe must receive the sharpest, most precise highlights in the frame, tracing mesh texture, side emblem, lace tension, midsole edges, and outsole form without losing the black materials. The pedestal should pick up a luminous magenta halo, and the smoke should glow selectively rather than blooming everywhere. Keep contrast high but refined, avoiding crushed blacks and muddy shadows.

Material and texture: emphasize mesh weave, synthetic panel layering, rubber edge definition, lace texture, heel structure, midsole curvature, pedestal metal finish, and smoke translucency. Every material must feel tactile, premium, and photographed rather than illustrated. The energy effect should enhance the shoe's performance narrative without overpowering its material credibility.

Color hierarchy: 60% black, charcoal, and deep magenta-black shadow tones; 30% vivid hot-fuchsia energy light and glowing smoke; 10% cool grey speculars and minimal pale-pink text accents. The palette must feel high-performance, seductive, technologically advanced, and luxurious.

Design intent: the final poster must preserve the exact impact logic of the source image—floating hero sneaker, luminous pedestal energy column, left-side headline architecture, dark neon palette, and minimal lower tagline—while merging Orbit atmosphere, Transit realism, and a cleaner reduced-copy system into one resolved luxury sports campaign. The shoe remains the only true hero, and all design elements exist to amplify its power with maximum clarity.

Rendering style: ultra-photoreal luxury footwear advertising, cinematic performance product poster, premium sports-tech campaign, high-end studio product photography, refined neon energy integration, clean editorial layout, world-class commercial retouching, print-ready finish.
