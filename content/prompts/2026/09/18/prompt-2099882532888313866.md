## Prompt

Create a Cannes-level ultra-premium football boot advertising poster for a fictional performance brand called VELOCITA NOIR, preserving the exact structural logic of a high-speed motion campaign while merging emotional velocity with forensic product realism and removing all nonessential support copy. The composition is vertical and highly controlled: a deep magenta-black field, three progressive football-boot motion states stacked through the center, luminous pink-violet energy trails sweeping horizontally across the frame, one compact brand mark at the top, and one bold closing slogan near the bottom. The final image must feel like pure acceleration distilled into one luxury sports image.

Core composition: use a vertical poster with a near-black to burgundy-magenta gradient background and subtle atmospheric glow. In the upper third, show one abstract speed-state silhouette of the football boot formed almost entirely by luminous motion ribbons, as if the shoe has outrun its own body. In the middle zone, show a second state where the real boot begins to emerge from the velocity trails, partially materialized and partially wrapped in light. In the lower-middle zone, show the fully visible hero football boot in sharp photoreal side profile facing right, sitting within a soft glossy reflection while the motion lines continue to pass through it from left to right. The three states must read as one continuous acceleration sequence, not separate objects.

Orbit atmosphere: the poster should feel like speed leaving visible echoes in space. The uppermost state is memory, the middle state is transformation, and the lowest state is full physical arrival. The motion is not chaotic; it is concentrated, elegant, and lethal, like a football boot tuned for split-second dominance. The emotional tone should be sharp, cinematic, and predatory, with the sense that the product is moving faster than perception itself.

Transit realism: render the lower hero boot with top-tier commercial precision. The upper should show a sleek aerodynamic silhouette, precision synthetic skin texture, subtle gloss-to-matte transitions, structured lace system, sculpted speed ribs, fine seam logic, and a premium side emblem integrated into the design. The outsole must show believable football stud geometry and engineered soleplate structure. The mid state should partially reveal these details through the energy field, while the top state abstracts them into pure speed-form. The light trails must feel refined and physically coherent, with layered translucency, edge tapering, directional blur, and controlled bloom. A soft grounded reflection beneath the lower boot should add polish without clutter.

Typography and layout: strip the copy system down to essentials. At the very top, place one compact VELOCITA NOIR wordmark or insignia. Near the bottom, place one bold English slogan in a strong italic performance typeface, such as "MOVE BEFORE THEY SEE." or "BORN TO BREAK SPEED." Remove any secondary descriptor lines or paragraph-style support text. The layout must feel spacious, premium, and immediately readable.

Lighting: use dramatic studio lighting with hot magenta-violet edge illumination and deep controlled shadows. The lower hero boot must receive the clearest highlight structure, tracing toe profile, upper contours, lace tension, side emblem, and sole geometry. The middle state should feel more unstable and radiant, while the upper state remains mostly light-defined. The glow from the energy bands should softly reflect on the background and the glossy floor plane without overpowering the shoe.

Material and texture: emphasize fine synthetic upper grain, soleplate rigidity, stud reflections, subtle reflective trims, and the optical texture of the neon motion trails. The boot must feel like a real elite football product captured in a world-class studio. The light trails must feel immaterial but engineered, elegant rather than smoky or messy.

Color hierarchy: 60% black, deep burgundy, and magenta-shadow tones; 30% hot fuchsia, neon pink, and electric violet motion light; 10% pale lilac highlight accents and restrained light-toned typography. The palette must feel aggressive, fast, luxurious, and highly controlled.

Design intent: the final poster must preserve the exact impact logic of stacked motion states, neon velocity ribbons, compact top branding, and bold bottom slogan, while merging Orbit atmosphere and Transit realism into one resolved elite football campaign. The shoe remains the sole hero. The speed trails exist only to reveal its power.

Rendering style: ultra-photoreal luxury football-boot advertising, cinematic motion-study poster, premium sports-tech campaign, high-end studio product photography, refined neon velocity effects, minimal editorial layout, world-class commercial retouching, print-ready finish.
