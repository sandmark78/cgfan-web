## Prompt

Create a premium vintage travel postcard/editorial poster in a vertical 4:5 format.

Feature [LOCATION / LANDMARK / SCENE] in a beautifully balanced split composition. The left half presents the scene as an ultra-realistic professional travel photograph, while the right half presents the same scene as a handcrafted vintage illustration, with matching viewpoint, composition, architecture, proportions, and subject placement.

On the photographic side, use authentic environmental details, realistic materials and textures, natural cinematic sunlight, believable shadows and reflections, atmospheric depth, refined natural color grading, a 35mm travel-photography perspective, crisp architectural detail, and ultra-realistic photographic clarity.

On the illustrated side, reinterpret the scene as a sophisticated vintage print using expressive ink outlines, subtly imperfect hand-drawn contours, muted gouache washes, faded pigments, visible brush texture, and delicate screen-print character. Keep it elegant, tactile, and handcrafted.

Unify the entire poster with warm aged-cream paper, subtle paper fibers, faint stains, analog grain, tiny printing imperfections, and softly worn edges. Make the division between the photographic and illustrated halves clean and intentional, like a collectible travel journal page.
