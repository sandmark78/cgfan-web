## Prompt

A restrained narrative poster built around any chosen subject object, with the most recognizable core object of the theme extremely enlarged so its clear lateral silhouette occupies most of the frame, the top edge and one side naturally cropped, and the gaze or directional orientation leading toward a wide, bright empty area; retain realistic detail and soft gradation in the key parts of the silhouette while the other side is gradually washed out by high-key diffused light so the object and background blend smoothly together.

Place a second narrative derived from the theme inside the outer silhouette: a markedly smaller scene with movement and spatial progression sweeps horizontally across the lower portion, entering from an edge and passing through the neck or supporting zone, while a distant horizon, motion blur, and pale scenery together form a continuous, memory-like band; only the lower part of this narrative stays clear, and upward it merges into the core object through transparent haziness, a soft-edged mask, and fading brightness, giving the double exposure a definite silhouette without hard collage seams.

Embed typography in the subject rather than floating it outside: place one oversized, high-contrast serif headline horizontally across the lower-middle area, with thin horizontals, thick verticals, sharp serifs, and expansive letterforms, using highlighted lettering pressed over the soft imagery; place two lines of small, narrow byline text above the headline and one line of tiny, widely tracked information below it, creating a strong scale jump between the monumental headline and minuscule text.

A delicate symbolic element derived from the theme may be embedded inside the enclosed space of a letter or glyph, kept understated, complete, and legible. In the bright negative space at the bottom, place a few fine-line emblems and short textual fragments in minimal, precise type, arranged with generous spacing and quiet alignment.
