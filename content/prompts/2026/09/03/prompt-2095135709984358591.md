## Prompt

Create a premium 4:5 vertical travel-poster illustration of [LOCATION] in a distinctive mixed-media style combining handcrafted clay/plasticine 3D forms, watercolor painting, textured paper, organic ink outlines, and subtle paper-cut collage.

Create a cinematic composition centered around [LOCATION]'s most iconic landmark, landscape, or natural feature, with clear foreground, middle-ground, and background depth. Add tactile handcrafted details, soft sculpted textures, visible handmade imperfections, watercolor washes, paper grain, and elegant illustrated depth. Keep it whimsical and miniature-like, but refined and editorial rather than childish.

Interpret the destination through its authentic architecture, landscape, vegetation, transportation, streets, cultural details, and recognizable local elements. Include small environmental storytelling details without clutter.

Use warm golden-hour lighting, soft shadows, atmospheric haze, glowing highlights, and a sophisticated earthy palette of peach, terracotta, cream, ochre, warm yellow, muted teal, sage, and natural tones, adapted to the location.

Create a dreamy watercolor sky with soft clouds and subtle paper texture.

At the top, include:

[LOCATION]
[COUNTRY / REGION]

Add one short, elegant tagline inspired by the destination. Typography should be hand-painted, vintage travel-poster inspired, legible, and naturally integrated into the artwork.

Prioritize authenticity, accurate landmarks, strong focal point, layered depth, tactile materials, cinematic warmth, premium editorial quality, and visual coherence. Avoid generic architecture, distorted landmarks, random symbols, meaningless text, and unrelated cultural elements.

Strict aspect ratio: 4:5 portrait.
