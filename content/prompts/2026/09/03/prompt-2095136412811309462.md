## Prompt

Generate a graphic poster with the character of a travel manifesto around a specific thematic subject: let a theme-derived core scene or symbolic image sit as a narrow rectangular image embedded in a continuous environmental background, with the embedded image echoing the surrounding background in color temperature, horizon, or formal direction while retaining a clear boundary, forming the central visual event of a distant view collected inside a frame while the frame itself floats in the scene.

Below, carry the composition with a dark, irregularly undulating foreground and place within it a very small theme-related figure, marker, or trace of action so the main subject gains expansive space and narrative scale.

Reserve ample dark negative space at the top and use a three-layer title rhythm: at the very top, compact information and a date in small type with wide letter spacing; in the middle, a line of thin connected cursive display lettering with a sense of handwriting speed; directly followed by a wide, thin-serif, high-contrast English or thematic headline; then a larger line of Chinese or supplementary text to anchor the center.

Let the cursive display and the serif headline create a clear jump in weight, leading, and light color, making the typography act as ballast above the image, and do not stack all the information in one typeface.

Arrange low-contrast short phrases, circular bullet-like entries, or fine horizontal rules on both sides, keeping an asymmetrical yet controlled breathing room so the marginal information feels as if floating in the air.

Overlay a theme-related handwritten phrase or small signature at the bottom, paired with a clear, centered set of supporting text.

Use a background built as a multi-layered color field that slowly shifts from a near-black deep blue or theme-derived dark tone at the top to a slightly warmer, muted tone near the horizon, with subtle grain and paper-like texture throughout.
