## Prompt

Build a book-slip exhibition-poster composition around any chosen subject: a deep, low-brightness patterned ground occupies the whole scene, with texture like darkened fabric, old-paper rubbings, or dark printing texture that only reveals its patterning and friction marks at close range.

Break the subject information into multiple narrow, tall vertical color strips that fall from the upper edge of the frame, sitting tightly against one another or leaving hairline gaps, forming a reading rhythm of ancient book remnants, scroll labels, and catalog entries placed side by side.

Place the largest title name slightly right of center, using a lean yet boned calligraphic structure with elongated strokes, open white space, and ink or light color strongly resisting the dark background; some strokes may approach the strip edges so the text appears carved from the black field.

The vertical strips on both sides and above carry derived short phrases, symbols, handwritten traces, or fine annotations with tight letter spacing and long rows, obvious size variation forming multi-level reading from large title to fragment information to bottom notes.

Colors derived from subject content but maintaining character relationship: large areas of low-brightness near-black background, main paper pieces using oxidized paper color, mineral blue, pale cyan, or theme-corresponding low-saturation mid-bright colors, with small amounts of golden or warm tones as narrow accent strips and fine text illumination.

Overall mood restrained, elegant, quiet; bright parts like old paper and mineral pigments illuminated in darkness rather than clean new printing. Paper surfaces retain fiber, spots, fading, local stains, ink bleeding and edge wear concentrated on paper face, text and column boundaries while dark background remains heavy and intact.

Text and texture must interlock: handwritten lines may float, break, press above paper grain; small archival information gathers in clusters; overall maintaining legibility while evoking aged scholarly atmosphere.
