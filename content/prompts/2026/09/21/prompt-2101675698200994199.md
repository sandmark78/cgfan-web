## Prompt

Create a 4:5 vertical contemporary editorial character illustration based on the uploaded reference image. Transform the exact person into a distinctive, sophisticated stylized character while keeping them clearly recognizable.

IDENTITY PRESERVATION: Preserve the person's facial structure, facial proportions, hairstyle, approximate age, skin tone, and distinctive features from the reference. Do not replace them with a generic character or different individual. Their identity should remain unmistakable while being reinterpreted through a refined editorial illustration style.

CHARACTER & STYLE: Use an elongated but natural silhouette, bold graphic color blocks, expressive everyday body language, understated casual fashion, and subtly oversized accessories. Give the character a believable personality through their posture, gesture, facial expression, and clothing.

COMPOSITION: Place the character on a clean white background with generous negative space and a carefully balanced 4:5 portrait composition. Use sophisticated colors, crisp graphic shapes, subtle printed grain and tactile paper texture, refined proportions, and strong editorial art direction.

The final artwork should feel human, personal, contemporary, stylish, and visually memorable—not overly glossy, plastic, cartoonish, or artificially perfect. Maintain a cohesive visual language suitable for a premium editorial character series.

High-end contemporary editorial illustration, sophisticated color palette, bold graphic forms, subtle print texture, clean negative space, refined composition, professional character artwork.
