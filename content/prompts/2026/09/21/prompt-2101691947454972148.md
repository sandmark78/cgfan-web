## Prompt

Create a premium dual-fragrance surreal campaign board for a fictional international perfume house named VELORIQ, designed as one vertically stacked two-panel main visual with thin white framing and museum-grade black spacing. Preserve the structural logic of the reference as pure visual grammar only: two horizontal panels, each built around a real woman holding a real perfume bottle close to the face, but push the composition away from conventional perfume-ad layout and toward an international surreal key visual. The perfume bottles remain the absolute commercial hero in both panels, while the women function as emotional vessels of scent, light, and atmosphere rather than standard beauty models.

Top panel: VELORIQ Ember. A mature woman with dark hair and natural freckles appears in an intimate side-facing beauty profile, holding a square amber-glass bottle beside her lips and cheek with elegant fingers. Her face must feel close, alive, and luminous, but not like ordinary commercial portraiture. Push the image toward sensorial surrealism: warm amber light seems to bloom from within the glass, reflecting across her skin as if the fragrance is tinting the air around her. The bottle is extremely realistic and product-dominant: thick glass walls, rich amber liquid, crisp gold-amber refractions, polished metallic cap, refined printed logo, premium commercial sharpness. The woman’s anatomy must be correct and graceful, with realistic fingers, five digits, natural lips, fine skin texture, visible pores and freckles, no plastic smoothing, no deformation. The background should dissolve into deep shadow and warm fragrance haze, reducing literal environment and making the bottle-and-face relationship feel more iconic and suspended.
