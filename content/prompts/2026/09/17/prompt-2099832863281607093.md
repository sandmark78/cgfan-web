## Prompt

Belle Époque Paris at night, showing the magnificent façade of an extravagant luxury bordello. The ornate multi-story building glows with warm golden light from chandeliers behind tall windows, framed by red velvet curtains, carved stonework and elaborate wrought-iron balconies.

Across several floors, beautiful adult women—blondes, brunettes and redheads—pose on separate balconies in elegant corsets, lingerie, stockings, robes and flowing silk. They lean against railings, lounge in doorways and flirtatiously display themselves to attract passersby below, each with a distinct pose and personality.

On the wet street, well-dressed gentlemen in dark coats and top hats arrive on foot and by horse-drawn carriage, looking up toward the balconies.

Opulent late-19th-century Parisian nightlife, warm amber interiors against a cool dusk sky, sensual and decadent atmosphere, cinematic painterly realism, rich architectural detail, no text.
