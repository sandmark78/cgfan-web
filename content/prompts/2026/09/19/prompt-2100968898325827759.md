## Prompt

A hyper-real premium Asian noodle poster advertisement for a fictional restaurant brand called XINVERA HOUSE, designed as a flagship red-and-gold commercial key visual where product impact, series flexibility, and curated brand polish are fused into one unified composition. The absolute hero is a large black ceramic bowl of glossy beef soba noodles placed in the lower center, while a dramatic bundle of noodles is lifted vertically upward by dark wooden chopsticks near the upper right, creating the main force line of the poster. The lifted noodles must feel springy, weighted, silky, and richly seasoned, forming a strong visual cascade from the bowl into the upper brand zone.

The overall structure preserves a bold Chinese festive poster logic: a premium gold emblem and brand mark at the top center, a large expressive dish title on the upper left, the noodle lift traveling through the middle, a richly filled bowl grounding the bottom, and a series variant label at the bottom right. The background is a deep ceremonial red layered with low-contrast cloud-scroll patterns and architectural silhouettes that feel rich but stay behind the food instead of becoming visual noise.

Richness is controlled by hierarchy, not density: reserve the brightest gold and strongest highlights for the dish title, glossy noodle lift, and lacquered beef. Secondary elements (bowls, garnish, steam, chili oil droplets, sesame, scallion) use softer gold, warm amber, and muted vermilion so the eye gets a clear route through the red: title first, noodles second, bowl third.

Lighting is cinematic and directional: a warm key light from the upper left creates glossy highlights on the lifted noodles, catches individual strands, and gives the beef a lacquered sheen. Subtle fill light preserves detail in the shadows of the bowl. Steam is visible but controlled, backlit to feel atmospheric without obscuring the food.

Typography uses a premium Chinese serif or brush-style typeface for the dish title, with clean sans-serif for supporting text. The gold emblem is refined and minimal, not ornate. The composition feels abundant but curated, festive but sophisticated, with the noodle lift as the undeniable hero moment.
