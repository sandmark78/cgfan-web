## Prompt

Brand: [BRAND NAME]
Food / Drink: [PRODUCT]
Headline: [2–4 WORD HEADLINE]
Accent Color: [COLOR]
Visual Interaction: [food replaces a letter / passes through text / becomes part of layout]

Create a premium editorial food campaign poster where typography and physical food/drink interact in impossible, magical ways.

TYPOGRAPHY TREATMENT:
- Large, bold, modern sans-serif or custom display type
- Letters behave as physical objects in the scene
- Food/drink interacts with type: chocolate hardens on cold letters, butter melts on hot type, olive oil pools inside counters, dough rises into gaps, coffee stains letters, steam wraps around text

FOOD PRESENTATION:
- Hyper-realistic food photography aesthetic
- Visible texture, weight, temperature, and material properties
- Food is the hero: glossy, appetizing, tactile
- Natural imperfections: crumbs, drips, melting, stretching

COMPOSITION:
- Clean, minimal background (solid color or subtle gradient)
- Typography and food share the same physical space
- Interactive moments: food passes through letters, replaces characters, creates shadows on text
- Editorial magazine aesthetic with premium polish

MOOD: Sophisticated, playful, impossible physics, high-end food campaign
