## Prompt

Create a premium vertical 4:5 travel-poster illustration of [LOCATION], following a consistent handcrafted visual identity: bold, clean black outlines, saturated yet harmonious colors, warm golden-hour lighting, richly textured paper/cardstock surfaces, layered cut-paper depth with soft cast shadows, a rounded vintage postcard frame, and a charming illustrated—not photorealistic—finish.

Build a strong cinematic depth hierarchy with destination-specific foreground foliage or details → a winding road, path, or river leading inward → iconic local architecture and cultural elements → layered surrounding landscapes → an atmospheric sky with stylized clouds, sun, and birds. Use 3–5 tactile paper layers with subtle raised or embossed edges, selective handmade imperfections, and one distinctive local material texture—such as washi, tile, textile, woodblock, woven paper, or carved wood—used naturally and without overpowering the artwork.

Make [LOCATION] instantly recognizable through 3–5 authentic landmarks or place-defining elements, while keeping the composition uncluttered. Use one dominant focal point, strong thumbnail readability, generous negative space, and a clear visual journey from foreground to background.

Maintain a cohesive global series system: cream textured border, top-left destination identity panel, bottom-right circular destination seal, and a short evocative travel phrase at the bottom center, with consistent typography, spacing, and graphic language. Keep text minimal and refined.

Avoid photorealism, generic tourism imagery, excessive 3D effects, visual clutter, and random objects. The final artwork should feel like a collectible handcrafted vintage travel postcard from one sophisticated, unified global art series.
