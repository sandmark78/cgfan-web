## Prompt

[CITY] = [....]
[COUNTRY] = [.....]
[ICONIC STRUCTURE] = [Auto]
[SEASON] = [Spring]

Create a whimsical, handcrafted miniature diorama representing [CITY], [COUNTRY], featuring [ICONIC STRUCTURE] as the main focal point. Automatically select exactly 3 real, instantly recognizable landmarks or place-defining elements from the location. Use one as the primary focal point, while naturally integrating the other two into the same continuous scene with asymmetry, overlap, layered depth, and smooth visual flow.

Build a cozy miniature world with tiny streets, architecture, [SEASON] details, local decorations, and subtle location-inspired objects. Use a soft, warm color palette with realistic handmade textures, tiny imperfections, paper-craft elements, and clay-like details. Everything should look physically miniature, carefully handcrafted, and lovingly assembled.

Place the entire diorama on a clean white textured paper background with generous elegant negative space. Add small refined typography reading "[CITY] / [COUNTRY]", along with a few tiny location-inspired signs integrated naturally into the scene.

Use soft, diffused studio lighting, gentle realistic shadows, strong dimensional depth, and a charming storybook atmosphere. The final result should feel like a premium collectible miniature art piece photographed as a high-end product, highly detailed and visually polished.

Format: Vertical 4:5 composition, centered premium product photography, ultra-high resolution, strong 3D depth, clean white background, elegant negative space, no people, no photorealistic background.
