## Prompt

Create a Cannes-level flagship advertising poster for an original premium nutrition bar brand named CRAVEFORM, featuring the product Dark Cocoa Almond Protein Bar. Build the image with refined Orbit logic and a calm, internationally polished campaign aesthetic.

Core composition:
Use a vertical poster with a clean matte taupe-beige background, smooth and minimal, with soft studio depth and generous negative space. In the exact center, place one hyper-realistic wrapped protein bar floating horizontally with a subtle upward tilt. The bar must be the absolute hero, large, sharp, sculptural, and visually dominant.

Orbit structure:
Behind the bar, create two controlled dark chocolate liquid arcs expanding outward from left and right in a restrained symmetrical motion, framing the product without chaos. The arcs should feel like a precise visual orbit rather than a wild splash. Above the bar, place a small number of suspended hero ingredients in a balanced circular rhythm: dark chocolate cubes, one whole roasted almond, and one broken chocolate piece. Below the bar, place a minimal grounded ingredient arrangement that quietly completes the eye-flow back to the center. The full composition should guide the eye in a calm loop: headline, top ingredients, hero bar, lower ingredients, back to hero.

Typography system:
At the upper center, place a large, compact, internationally refined headline in bold editorial typography:
"PURE
CRAVE"
Below the product, add one restrained supporting line in elegant small type:
"Dark cocoa. Clean fuel."
The typography must feel premium, global, and art-directed, with precise spacing and no playful snack-brand tone.

Packaging direction:
Design the wrapper to feel premium and contemporary, with a muted cocoa-brown and mineral-beige palette, elegant brand hierarchy, subtle product descriptors, and highly realistic material detail. Avoid loud claims or crowded front-of-pack messaging. Keep the packaging visually sophisticated and believable, suitable for a high-end wellness snack brand.

Lower information system:
At the bottom center, place three very small icon-led benefit markers in a refined editorial style:
"High Fibre"
"Protein Focused"
"No Added Cholesterol"
They should feel understated, clean, and secondary to the main composition.

Visual style:
Ultra-realistic commercial product photography, premium packaging realism, controlled dark chocolate fluid simulation, believable almond and chocolate textures, crisp edges, soft contact shadows, luxury FMCG launch key visual quality, quiet sophistication, clean negative space, no clutter.

Lighting and color:
Soft controlled studio lighting with gentle highlights and sculpted shadows. Use a restrained palette of taupe beige, dark cocoa, almond brown, muted cream, and deep chocolate tones. Avoid overly warm rustic breakfast tones or loud candy-like contrast. The final image should feel modern, calm, premium, and globally branded.

Mood:
Quiet confidence, refined indulgence, premium nutrition, modern restraint, international flagship campaign, elevated appetite appeal.
