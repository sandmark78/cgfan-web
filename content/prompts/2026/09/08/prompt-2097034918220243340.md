## Prompt

Whimsical folk-art naive illustration in the style of traditional hand-stitched needlepoint and tapestry. A delightfully plump, round rainbow owl with huge sparkling concentric eyes in teal, gold, and violet, stitched with visible yarn texture and cross-stitch patterns. Its body features intricate geometric and floral needlepoint motifs in bright rainbow hues, hot pink, lime, turquoise, coral, butter yellow, and lavender. Perched on a flowering branch with tiny stitched leaves and berries, one wing raised in a cheerful wave. The background is a creamy linen fabric with subtle woven texture and decorative embroidered border frames in complementary colors. The entire composition should look like an actual handcrafted textile artwork with realistic fabric weave, visible stitch holes, thread texture, and the charming imperfections of handmade needlework. Warm, nostalgic, storybook illustration aesthetic with tactile craft quality.
