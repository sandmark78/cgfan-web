## 创作思路

Abundance looks premium only when it has a depth order. A layered bakery spread with one oversized sliced loaf and one celebration cake in the front plane; croissants, rustic loaves, and pastries stepping back in distinct height tiers.

## Prompt

Create a Cannes-level ultra-premium bakery advertising poster for an original artisan French-inspired bakery brand called "Aurelian Oven", designed in a tall vertical mobile-first format. The composition must preserve a strong high-conversion commercial poster structure with a refined bakery crest logo zone, a bold headline zone, a luxurious central product spread, a clean category strip, and a clear order-information footer.

Hero product composition: Build a highly curated central-lower product spread on a warm wooden counter, led by a hero arrangement of premium French-butter bakery products and signature artisan breads. The product field must include: one beautifully sliced milk loaf with extremely soft porous crumb texture, one rustic country loaf with dramatic hand-scored crust and flour-dusted blistering, one laminated croissant with visible buttery layers, one Danish pastry with glossy fruit or custard center, one elegant chocolate ganache cake, one cream-and-berry celebration cake, one refined muffin, and one premium cupcake.

Branding and text layout: Place a large custom crest logo for "Aurelian Oven" in the upper left, designed as a luxurious black-and-antique-gold bakery emblem with elegant wheat motifs. In the upper right, place a powerful typographic headline block such as "Golden Layers, Morning Glory". All typography must feel art-directed, premium, elegant, and compositionally integrated.

Scene and atmosphere: Create a softly blurred upscale bakery boutique environment in the background with warm morning light and a refined amber-cream glow.

Lighting: Use layered cinematic bakery lighting with a warm golden key light from upper left, a soft frontal fill to preserve detail, and a subtle rim light to outline the bread crusts, pastry edges, and cake contours.

Material and color direction: Use a luxurious bakery palette of toasted gold, cultured butter yellow, warm cream, honey beige, caramel brown, cocoa dark brown, baked wheat, soft ivory, and restrained antique-gold graphic accents.

Rendering style: ultra-realistic premium bakery campaign, French-butter patisserie advertising, luxury artisan bread poster, hyper-detailed food photography, elegant boutique bakery branding, cinematic warm lighting, refined editorial typography, polished retail poster design, 8k, world-class image quality.
