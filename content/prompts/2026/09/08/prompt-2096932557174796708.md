## 创作思路

Create a premium travel memory card designed like a beautifully curated page from a vintage explorer's personal collection, combining realistic miniature photography with hand-drawn details, archival photographs, map fragments, and travel ephemera.

## Prompt

Create a premium travel memory card for [DESTINATION], designed like a beautifully curated page from a vintage explorer's personal collection. Feature [ICONIC LANDMARK] as the main focal point, surrounded by authentic local details such as architecture, transportation, food, nature, traditional objects, streets, or cultural symbols.

Combine realistic miniature photography, hand-drawn details, small archival photographs, map fragments, postage stamps, tickets, handwritten notes, coordinates, and subtle travel ephemera into one elegant composition.

Use an aged paper background, natural textures, muted destination-inspired colors, soft shadows, imperfect printing, faded ink, torn-paper edges, and subtle handwritten annotations.

Add a small title: [DESTINATION], along with YEAR, COORDINATES, COLLECTION NO. and a short travel note.

Sophisticated editorial travel-journal aesthetic, tactile handmade materials, authentic imperfections, balanced composition, generous negative space, highly detailed, artistic but realistic, premium collectible souvenir design.

Avoid cartoon style, generic tourist-poster design, excessive decoration, and overcrowding.
