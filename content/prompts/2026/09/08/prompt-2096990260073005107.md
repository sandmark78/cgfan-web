## Prompt

Create a cute minimalist vintage passport boarding pass for [COUNTRY]. Feature a tiny illustrated iconic landmark as the main detail, with a miniature country flag, local flower, cultural symbol, destination code, passport stamp, date stamp, and tiny ticket details. Use warm ivory paper, soft muted pastel colors, thin vintage borders, subtle paper fibers, slightly faded ink, delicate typography, and lots of clean negative space. Make it look like a collectible passport keepsake from a charming journey — elegant, tiny, nostalgic, playful, and premium. Front-facing flat composition, balanced layout, no clutter, no collage, no photorealism.

Ticket details can include:
* DESTINATION: [COUNTRY]
* CITY: [FAMOUS CITY]
* GATE: [01]
* SEAT: [A12]
* CLASS: EXPLORER
* STAMP: [COUNTRY] PASSPORT
* DATE: 2026
* LANDMARK: [ICONIC LANDMARK]
