## Prompt

Create a premium 4:5 vertical vintage travel poster for [COUNTRY / CITY / LOCATION / SUBJECT].

TEXT-TO-IMAGE ONLY — generate the entire artwork creatively from [COUNTRY / CITY / LOCATION / SUBJECT] alone. No reference image required.

POSTER CONCEPT
Create a sophisticated editorial travel poster that captures the visual identity and atmosphere of [COUNTRY / CITY / LOCATION / SUBJECT]. Automatically identify the most iconic local landmarks, architecture, streets, landscapes, cultural elements, and distinctive details, then compose them into one cohesive hand-painted travel scene.

TYPOGRAPHY
Make the location name the dominant visual element using bold oversized vintage editorial lettering. Add only minimal supporting location information and a small poetic caption inspired by the destination. Keep typography elegant, integrated, and restrained.

VISUAL STYLE
Use generous warm cream negative space with a refined palette of muted blue, sandstone, warm beige, earthy tones, and subtle natural accents. Render the destination with detailed hand-painted shapes, simplified architectural forms, atmospheric depth, and elegant graphic composition.

Add subtle paper grain, gentle print imperfections, a minimal sun motif, and understated vintage poster texture. Keep the artwork tactile and sophisticated rather than distressed or overly aged.

FINAL AESTHETIC
Classic European travel poster × vintage editorial travel magazine × hand-painted destination illustration × refined mid-century graphic design.

The composition should feel collectible, premium, calm, nostalgic, and strongly connected to the visual identity of [COUNTRY / CITY / LOCATION / SUBJECT].

Avoid photorealism, 3D rendering, modern digital illustration, excessive texture, clutter, distorted architecture, random landmarks, excessive typography, logos, watermarks, or overly saturated colors.

FORMAT: 4:5 vertical, premium vintage travel-poster composition.
