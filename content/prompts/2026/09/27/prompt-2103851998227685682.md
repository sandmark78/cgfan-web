## Prompt

Create the Port-refined final poster for the fictional international design-material house CHROMAVEIL LARE. Remove all title architecture and all small editorial text completely. The poster must now rely on pure image force alone: one hyper-detailed chromatic chameleon as the absolute hero, liquid-pigment ribbons erupting from its body, one macro flower anchoring the lower-right foreground, and a deep midnight void surrounding them with luxurious negative space. The final image must feel more brutal, immediate, and visually magnetic than the previous version, as if the brand no longer needs words because the image itself carries the full charge.

The composition must be stripped to the essential. Center one extraordinary chameleon in extreme macro commercial realism, shown in three-quarter profile with the head turned slightly toward camera. It dominates the frame through scale, chromatic density, anatomical precision, and eye contact. There is no title wall behind it and no text anywhere. The empty space around the creature must intensify its presence rather than weaken it. The silhouette should feel iconic and instantly legible against the dark field.

The chameleon is the entire hero object and the embodiment of living color. Its skin must be biologically precise and microscopically detailed, yet elevated into a luxury material language: sharply resolved micro-scales, wet iridescent transitions, controlled spectral layering, and tactile reptilian realism fused with premium design sensibility. Control the palette with severity rather than chaos: electric cyan, lacquered cobalt, molten orange, emerald green, ultraviolet violet, magenta fire, and tiny amber sparks, all flowing across the body in deliberate hierarchy. The eye must be jewel-like, alert, and hypnotic, becoming the emotional anchor of the whole poster.
