## Prompt

Create the definitive Orbit + Transit + Port final poster for the fictional international neo-couture label LUCENT ASCEND SYSTEM. Preserve the exact graphic architecture of the reference, but refine it into a cleaner, rarer, more collectible fashion-tech campaign master visual with stricter product hierarchy, sharper silhouette control, and more disciplined light behavior. Keep the same essential structure: a pure violet-lilac studio field, one full-body female muse centered in a poised forward step, a transparent iridescent outer coat layered over a sleek bodysuit, luminous neon piping integrated into the garment construction, sculptural translucent adaptive tubes wrapping the lower body and legs, and only the lightest possible amount of English brand text. The final result must feel like an official global launch poster for next-generation adaptive apparel.

The absolute hero is the apparel system worn by one elegant young adult woman in full-body view at center. She must feel calm, severe, and editorial, with a forward-moving couture stance that suggests motion without losing poster clarity. Her anatomy must be exact and adult: correct head-neck-shoulder proportion, natural mature chest gravity, believable pelvis and leg structure, smooth hip-to-thigh transition, naturally full lower-body volume, subtle skin elasticity, and realistic hands with five fingers. Her face should be sculptural and composed, with dark hair pulled into a tight bun or equally clean updo so the silhouette stays aerodynamic and futuristic. She is the living carrier of the garment, but the garment remains the true commercial focus.

The garment architecture is the central design event. Build a long transparent iridescent trench or shell coat with real panel logic, sharp lapels, believable seam construction, crisp sleeve volume, and subtle reflective edge behavior. The clear material must feel like premium technical polymer or couture-grade translucent vinyl, not cheap plastic. Integrated into and beneath the coat, luminous neon piping in acid lime, electric green, and controlled hot pink traces the garment’s structural lines: lapels, pockets, hems, sleeve openings, torso pathways, and internal contour seams. The piping must feel embedded into the clothing system rather than laid on top, reading as intelligent illuminated apparel design.
