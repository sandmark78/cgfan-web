## Prompt

Create a 9:16 vertical poster series in English with a high-end editorial visual style, combining brutalist architecture, monumental perspective typography, realistic young adult figures, and strong three-dimensional spatial design. The series should feel bold, clean, architectural, and visually sophisticated, with a deep cobalt-blue sky, warm raw-concrete gray, warm ivory typography, charcoal styling for the figures, and safety orange as the only strong accent color. Use concrete, giant type, human scale, and 3D forms all pushed into the same perspective system. SKY PRISM: transparent planes in the air. CUT SPACE: a bold slice through the structure. TYPE SPAN: letters turned into a bridge. OPEN SEQUENCE: depth built frame by frame.

## 备注

字体即图形的编辑设计方向，粗野主义建筑与巨型字体构成空间叙事。钴蓝天空+暖灰混凝土+象牙白字体+安全橙点缀，有限配色克制高级。框架类提示词，可替换主题，策展价值强。
