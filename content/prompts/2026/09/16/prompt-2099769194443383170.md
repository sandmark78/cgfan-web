## Prompt

Along a cobbled lane, a baker with flour-dusted cheeks kneads dough with gusto, sunlight dappling through the open doorway of a charming bakery, loaves of bread stacked high within, the air thick with the scent of cinnamon and warmth, a playful terrier pup, wagging its tail furiously.
