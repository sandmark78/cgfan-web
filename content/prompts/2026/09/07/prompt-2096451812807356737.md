## Prompt

Create a charming handcrafted 3D paper-cut travel poster representing [DESTINATION]. Build the entire scene as one cohesive layered paper diorama, with soft sculpted paper textures, cut-out edges, subtle shadows, gentle pastel colors, and a premium handmade storybook aesthetic.

Place [ICONIC LANDMARK / MOUNTAIN / STRUCTURE] as the large central focal point, surrounded by recognizable local scenery such as [3–5 LOCAL ELEMENTS]. Include winding roads, small houses, local markets, farms, gardens, bridges, rivers, tiny people, vehicles, animals, and vegetation to create a lively miniature world.

Use multiple overlapping paper layers to create strong depth — distant sky and clouds, background landscape, mountains, middle-ground architecture, and detailed foreground activities. Everything should look physically crafted from textured cardstock, with delicate raised edges and realistic miniature shadows.

At the top, add elegant travel-poster typography:
"[DESTINATION]"
"[COUNTRY]"
"[SHORT LOCAL PHRASE / TAGLINE]"

Use a warm cream background, muted sage green, dusty blue, terracotta, beige and soft blush tones, with a nostalgic vintage travel-poster feeling. Keep the composition balanced, whimsical, sophisticated, highly detailed, and visually rich while maintaining a clean handmade aesthetic. No photorealism, no collage, no flat vector graphics — everything must appear as a single handcrafted 3D paper miniature world.
