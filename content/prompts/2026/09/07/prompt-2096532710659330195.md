## Prompt

Create a Cannes-level flagship advertising poster for an original luxury cultural brand named AURELIA SCROLL, featuring a premium collectible masterpiece titled "The Living Landscape Scroll". Build the image as a sacred narrative key visual where a real handcrafted scroll unfurls into a living mountain world, combining epic atmospheric storytelling with museum-grade material realism.

Core concept:
The scroll itself is the absolute hero product. Its physical body must remain clearly identifiable as a premium collectible object: hand-mounted parchment, silk borders, carved finials, mineral ink landscape painting, aged fibers, seal marks, calligraphic inscriptions, and a refined tassel detail. As the scroll opens downward through the composition, the painted world gradually transforms into a real dimensional landscape, as if the object carries an entire mythic realm inside it.

Narrative atmosphere:
The image should feel like a revelation. The upper portion begins in reverent silence, with celestial light breaking through clouds and touching the scroll surface. As the eye follows the flowing paper downward, the scene opens into a living mountainous world of temples, cliffs, waterfalls, mist valleys, stone gateways, and river passages. The emotional arc moves from awe to immersion to serenity, as if the viewer is witnessing a sacred world being unsealed.

Camera and spatial grammar:
Use a cinematic vertical composition with the scroll as the central axis. The upper third shows the scroll's sacred opening with divine light. The middle third transitions from painting to reality. The lower third reveals the full dimensional landscape emerging from the scroll. Maintain museum-grade material realism: parchment texture, silk weave, mineral pigment granularity, aged paper fibers, ink bleeding edges.

Typography and layout:
Large illuminated negative space beside the scroll reserved for refined editorial typography. The headline "AURELIA SCROLL" in elegant serif with generous letter-spacing. Subheadline "The Living Landscape Scroll" in refined italic. Keep the epic scale from becoming crowded. The world feels vast, but the headline still has a calm premium place to land.

Color palette:
Muted mineral tones: aged parchment ivory, silk border crimson, mineral ink black, landscape sage green, waterfall mist white, temple roof terracotta. Limited to 3-4 dominant colors with克制 restraint.

Style:
Eastern aesthetic minimalism meets luxury editorial design. The scroll is both artifact and portal. Every material detail must feel authentic and collectible. No AI plastic sheen, no over-rendering. Maintain the quiet dignity of traditional Chinese landscape painting merged with contemporary luxury branding.
