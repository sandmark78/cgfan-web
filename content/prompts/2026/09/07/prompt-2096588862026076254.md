## Prompt

Create a premium vertical 4:5 travel poster for [LOCATION], inspired by an elegant vintage postage-stamp collection. Use a warm ivory paper background with subtle paper grain, soft shadows, and refined editorial typography.

LAYOUT: Place a large "[LOCATION]" title prominently at the upper-left, with the country name beneath it and a short poetic travel tagline. Surround the composition with 7–9 individually framed postage stamps, each featuring a different iconic landmark, landscape, cultural symbol, food, transportation, or local experience from [LOCATION]. Arrange the stamps in a balanced editorial collage with varied portrait and landscape orientations, realistic perforated edges, slight paper shadows, and authentic vintage-postage styling.

Each stamp should include clean, perfectly legible typography, a small country/postage marking, denomination, and a concise location caption such as the landmark or experience name. Add subtle decorative elements around the poster—botanical illustrations, a small country map, skyline line art, postal cancellation seal, handwritten travel phrase, and elegant divider lines.

STYLE: sophisticated European travel editorial, nostalgic yet luxurious, realistic photography inside the stamps, natural daylight, soft muted tones, refined serif typography mixed with delicate handwritten script, minimal cream/green/blue accents, authentic printed-paper texture, carefully balanced negative space.

TEXT: All visible text must be in English, correctly spelled, sharp, readable, and professionally typeset. Avoid random letters, distorted typography, duplicated elements, or excessive text.

Overall mood: collectible travel keepsake, timeless, cultured, sophisticated, premium destination poster.
