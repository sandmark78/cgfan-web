## Prompt

UPLOAD YOUR IMAGE: Upload your image to use yourself as the fashion model. Preserve your identity, facial features, hairstyle, skin tone and natural appearance accurately while seamlessly placing you into the scene.

Create a cinematic surreal luxury fashion photograph in a vertical 9:16 composition. An adult male or female fashion model rides an enormous horse made entirely from dense, volumetric pink smoke across an endless white salt desert.

The horse dominates the frame in a dramatic three-quarter frontal view, towering over the camera. Its anatomy must remain unmistakably equine, with a powerful head, defined muzzle, ears, muscular chest, raised front legs and flowing mane, but no solid flesh or fur. Its entire body is formed from thick pink vapor, turbulent smoke curls, translucent wisps and rolling clouds, with realistic gaseous detail.

Capture the horse at the peak of its charge. One front leg rises dramatically toward the lens while the other dissolves into an explosive cloud of pink smoke near the ground. Vapor coils around its legs and rises behind the rider into a huge atmospheric plume.

The model wears a futuristic all-white avant-garde fashion outfit with sharp tailoring, subtle metallic details, white footwear and a long lightweight coat flowing in the wind. Add dark angular sunglasses and a calm, confident editorial expression. Adapt the styling naturally to the model's gender and body proportions. Pale smoke-like reins are held naturally in the hands.

CAMERA: Extreme worm's-eye perspective, approximately 30 cm above the salt surface, 24mm wide-angle lens, strong realistic perspective, monumental scale. The horse fills most of the frame while the model is silhouetted against the open sky.

ENVIRONMENT: Endless cracked white salt flat, tiny distant mountains and a saturated cobalt-blue sky. No buildings, vegetation or other people.

LIGHTING: Harsh clean midday sunlight, crisp shadows, bright highlights on the white clothing and subtle pink light reflecting from the smoke onto the model and salt. Realistic sunlight passing through thinner smoke.

STYLE: Ultra-photorealistic RAW luxury fashion photography, physically believable volumetric smoke, detailed vapor particles, realistic skin and fabric texture, cinematic depth, high dynamic range, subtle film grain, premium surreal fashion campaign, 8K detail.

NEGATIVE: No solid pink horse, no pink fur, no plastic or fabric horse, no mechanical horse, no extra riders or horses, no distorted anatomy, no identity change, no buildings, no vegetation, no text, no logos, no illustration, no anime, no cartoon, no artificial CGI appearance.
