## Prompt

Create the definitive Orbit + Transit + Port final poster for the fictional international coffee innovation house CREMA CODE. Preserve the exact macro-teaser architecture of the reference, but refine it into a rarer, sharper, more collectible campaign master visual with stricter material hierarchy, cleaner monochrome control, and a more irresistible crema event. Keep the same essential structure: one oversized premium spoon suspended above the composition, a dense ribbon of velvety coffee crema descending from the spoon, a monumental cylindrical cup cropped in the lower frame, thick foam overflowing the rim in sculptural gravity-led drips, a warm monochrome espresso field, and only the lightest possible English teaser typography. The final result must feel like a luxury coffee poster built entirely from tactile appetite and material seduction.

The absolute hero is the crema itself. Render the foam, pour, spill, and surface texture in extreme macro photorealism so the viewer feels the thickness, softness, and aerated density instantly. The spoon must hold a heavy, glossy layer of whipped coffee foam that stretches downward into one clean central pour, landing on the cup top and pushing over the rim in one or two voluptuous, gravity-real drips. The crema must feel dense, velvety, and freshly whipped, with micro-bubbles, silky sheen, soft peaks, delicate pore structure, and rich caramel-tan depth. It must read as the primary subject before spoon, cup, or text.

The spoon should be cropped large in the upper-right to upper-middle area, angled diagonally downward with elegant tension. It must feel like a real premium utensil: warm brushed metal, soft refined highlights, smooth contour, believable weight, and controlled reflections. The spoon is the delivery mechanism of desire, but it remains secondary to the flowing crema itself.
