# 霓虹复古：3D角色肖像迈阿密风云

**作者**: Saul Goodman  
**日期**: 2026-09-11  
**评分**: 60/80

## Prompt

```
Use the uploaded photo as the sole and non-negotiable identity reference. Preserve the subject’s exact recognizable appearance with maximum fidelity, including face shape, forehead, jawline, cheekbones, chin, eyes, eyebrows, nose, lips, ears, skin tone and texture, hairstyle, hairline, facial hair if present, natural asymmetries, and all distinctive features. The final character must unmistakably be the same person. Do not beautify, reshape, age, feminize, masculinize, or replace the identity with a generic model.

Create a premium stylized 3D character portrait inspired by authentic 1980s fashion and a Miami/Vice City-inspired neon atmosphere. Make it feel like a high-end cinematic open-world game character render: bold, confident, polished, stylish, and visually striking.

Use a close-up or chest-up composition with a natural, powerful pose. The subject should appear calm, self-assured, and slightly rebellious, with the head subtly turned and the gaze directed off-camera or toward the distance.

Transform the subject into fully stylized 3D character art, not a photograph. Keep the face highly recognizable while using smooth sculpted forms, clean geometry, refined facial planes, realistic-stylized skin shading, detailed hair volume, polished materials, and cinematic rendering.

Dress the subject in premium 1980s-inspired fashion adapted to a modern game-world aesthetic: retro bomber jacket, open-collar shirt, fitted tee, satin or leather details, layered chains, bold accessories, or a colorful windbreaker. Keep the styling tasteful, fashionable, slightly luxurious, and appropriate to the subject’s appearance.

Create a neon-soaked Miami/Vice City mood with tropical energy, palm silhouettes, glamorous nightlife, retro architecture, blurred city lights, pastel dusk skies, and subtle neon reflections. Keep the background cinematic and uncluttered.

Use a vibrant retro color palette of hot pink, cyan, teal, purple, sunset orange, electric blue, and warm magenta. Add dramatic cinematic rim lighting, glossy reflections, soft global illumination, strong subject separation, premium contrast, and sophisticated color grading.

Give the hair, clothing, and accessories subtle 1980s influence while maintaining the subject’s original identity and natural characteristics. The overall result should feel like a stylish main character from a neon-soaked open-world crime universe.

Final look: ultra-polished 3D game-character render, cinematic quality, highly recognizable face, 80s fashion fusion, Vice City-inspired retro-futuristic atmosphere, glossy materials, crisp facial detail, premium lighting, clean composition, and scroll-stopping visual impact.

Negative constraints: Do not create a realistic photograph, flat illustration, anime, sketch, or painterly artwork. Do not alter the person’s identity or facial structure. No distorted features, excessive beautification, aging, messy styling, visible text, logos, watermarks, signatures, labels, branding, or official GTA VI logo.
```
