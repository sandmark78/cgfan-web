## Prompt

Prompt:

Create a 9:16 vertical poster in a clean contemporary museum-like architectural space, with minimal editorial typography and a small human figure for scale. The style should feel like a high-end exhibition poster: pale stone or concrete surfaces, strong daylight, crisp shadows, refined composition, and a calm but striking atmosphere. Make the word itself become the installation, fully integrated into space. Add small English micro-copy in the corners like a design study poster, but do not add any copyright marks or page numbers.

1. PRESS
   Use a deformation concept. Show the word “IMPACT” stretched across a large white tensile membrane wall, with a giant red sphere pushing forward from behind the surface so the fabric bulges outward and distorts the typography. The letters should feel pulled, compressed, and tensioned by the pressure. The result should feel physical, dramatic, and instantly readable.

2. TWIST
   Use a torsion concept. Build the word “TWIST” as a row of tall vertical panels or columns that rotate along their central axis. Each element should carry blue and yellow graphic surfaces, and together they should reveal the full word through twisting alignment. The feeling should be bold, graphic, and highly sculptural.
