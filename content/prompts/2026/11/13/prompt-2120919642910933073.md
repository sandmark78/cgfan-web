## Prompt

Create an exceptionally elegant minimalist architectural travel poster featuring [LANDMARK NAME], [CITY], [COUNTRY].

1. ARCHITECTURAL ILLUSTRATION

Create a clean, minimalist line-art illustration featuring 2–4 of the destination’s most recognisable landmarks.

One dominant landmark positioned in the centre.
Two or three smaller iconic structures arranged around it.
Preserve the authentic architectural silhouettes and distinctive features of each landmark.
Use thin, precise, continuous-looking black ink outlines.
Simplify architectural details into elegant geometric shapes.
Keep buildings unfilled, using outlines rather than solid illustrations.

2. MINIMALIST COMPOSITION

Arrange all landmarks along a single horizontal ground line, creating a beautiful architectural skyline.

Allow each structure to have its own height, proportions and visual character.

Maintain generous spacing between buildings and avoid overlapping architectural forms.

3. SUBTLE DECORATIVE ELEMENTS

Add a few delicate minimalist details around the skyline:

Tiny flying birds.
A crescent moon or geometric sun.
Small stars and crosses.
Delicate horizontal lines.
A minimal aeroplane with a dotted flight trail.
Subtle clouds or abstract celestial elements.

Keep these decorations sparse and understated.

4. DESTINATION TYPOGRAPHY

Below the architectural illustration, prominently display:

[CITY NAME]

Use large, elegant uppercase serif typography with generous letter spacing.

Underneath, add:

[COUNTRY NAME]

Use smaller, clean sans-serif lettering.

Typography should be perfectly aligned and visually balanced.

5. COLOUR & BACKGROUND

Use a warm ivory, pale cream or soft beige background with a very subtle paper texture.

Use predominantly elegant black or charcoal architectural outlines.

For each new destination, optionally introduce one distinctive muted accent colour inspired by its cultural identity. Keep the overall design restrained and minimal.

Never repeat the same accent colour across the collection.

6. ARTISTIC STYLE

Scandinavian minimalist illustration, architectural blueprint aesthetics, delicate black-line drawing, modern editorial poster design, geometric simplicity, refined typography and contemporary gallery art.

7. FINAL PRESENTATION

A sophisticated, timeless collectible architectural travel poster.

Strong visual hierarchy, generous negative space, accurate landmark silhouettes, delicate linework and beautifully balanced proportions.

Vertical 2:3 composition, premium print quality.

No photorealism, no 3D effects, no colourful collage, no heavy shading, no unnecessary details and no clutter.
