## Prompt

Create an elegant, minimalist architectural travel poster featuring [LANDMARK NAME], [CITY], [COUNTRY].

1. MAIN ARCHITECTURAL ILLUSTRATION

Feature the selected landmark as the central subject, illustrated entirely with delicate, continuous-looking hand-drawn outline strokes.

Preserve the landmark’s distinctive architectural silhouette and recognisable proportions.
Use clean, thin, slightly imperfect hand-drawn lines.
Avoid solid fills, heavy shading and excessive details.
Make the architecture look like a beautiful architectural sketch.

2. COLOUR PALETTE

Use a sophisticated combination of 3–4 soft pastel colours for the outlines.

Choose colours that complement the destination’s identity, architecture and cultural character.

Examples:

Paris: Dusty rose, lavender, soft blue and muted coral.
London: Navy, muted red, sage and pale gold.
Tokyo: Vermilion, jade, blush pink and warm grey.
Italy: Terracotta, olive, dusty blue and ochre.
Greece: Aegean blue, turquoise, muted coral and lavender.

Every destination must have its own unique colour combination.

3. FLOATING TRAVEL DETAILS

Add a few delicate decorative elements around the landmark:

A small flying aeroplane with a dotted curved flight trail.
Tiny birds, clouds or stars.
Minimal geographical coordinates.
A small travel stamp or destination symbol.
Subtle architectural decorative details.

Keep everything light, delicate and uncluttered.

4. TYPOGRAPHIC ELEMENTS

Add the landmark’s name in elegant, refined typography.

Include:
[LANDMARK NAME]
[CITY, COUNTRY]

Place the destination name in a beautiful decorative ribbon banner near the bottom, leaving sufficient space for the illustration.

Use sophisticated serif or handwritten typography.

5. BACKGROUND & COMPOSITION

Use a beautiful warm ivory or soft cream background with subtle handmade-paper texture.

Maintain generous negative space around the central illustration.

Create a balanced composition with the landmark as the visual focus and small decorative elements positioned naturally around it.

6. ARTISTIC DIRECTION

Minimalist architectural line art, Scandinavian poster design, delicate pastel illustrations, elegant travel stationery, vintage sketchbook aesthetics and contemporary gallery-style graphic design.

7. FINAL FINISH

A timeless, sophisticated collectible travel poster that feels delicate, artistic and beautifully handcrafted.

Vertical 4:5 composition, crisp fine-line artwork, premium print quality, clean spacing and refined visual hierarchy.

Avoid photorealism, 3D effects, heavy colours, thick outlines, crowded compositions and unnecessary decorative elements.
