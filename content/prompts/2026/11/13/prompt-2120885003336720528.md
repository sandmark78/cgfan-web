## Prompt

1. 80 年代香港霓虹街头
35mm film photograph, Kodak Portra 400, visible grain, slightly faded colors, 1980s Hong Kong street at night. A beautiful East Asian woman in her late twenties with shoulder-length wavy black hair and red lipstick stands under a neon sign, wearing a vintage floral qipao-style dress with a beige trench coat draped over her shoulders, black high heels. She looks over her shoulder at the camera with a soft, wistful smile. Behind her, wet asphalt reflects pink and cyan neon, old shop signs in Chinese characters, a tram passing in motion blur, steam rising from a food stall. Shot on a rangefinder, candid, natural skin texture, warm tungsten light mixed with neon, nostalgic, slightly soft focus at the edges.

2. 90 年代日本海边夏日
35mm film photograph, Fujifilm Superia 800, heavy grain, sun-bleached colors, 1990s Japanese seaside in midsummer. A young East Asian woman in her mid-twenties with a high ponytail and sun-kissed skin sits on a weathered wooden breakwater, wearing a white linen shirt over a navy swimsuit, straw hat in her hand. She laughs with her eyes closed, sea breeze lifting her hair. Behind her, a pale blue sea, distant fishing boats, a concrete tetrapod shore, white clouds. Shot from a low angle, candid snapshot feel, harsh noon sunlight, deep shadows, salt haze, nostalgic summer, natural skin texture.

3. 70 年代美式公路黄昏
35mm film photograph, Kodak Gold 200, warm faded tones, 1970s American desert highway at golden hour. A glamorous East Asian woman in her late twenties with voluminous wavy chestnut hair and large sunglasses leans against a cream vintage convertible, wearing a polka-dot halter dress and a thin silk scarf fluttering in the wind. She smiles at the camera, one hand shading her eyes. Behind her, an old gas station with a round sign, endless asphalt, distant mesas, a sky turning orange and pink. Shot on an SLR, road-trip snapshot, sun flare at the frame edge, dust in the air, nostalgic Americana, natural skin texture.

4. 90 年代室内窗光
35mm film photograph, Kodak Portra 160, fine grain, muted warm tones, 1990s living room in late afternoon. An elegant East Asian woman in her early thirties with sleek hair in a low bun sits by a large window, wearing a cream knitted sweater and a long plaid skirt, holding a paperback book. She gazes out the window, chin resting on her hand, soft thoughtful expression. Around her, a rotary telephone, a vase of dried flowers, lace curtains diffusing golden light, a wooden bookshelf, a cat sleeping on the sofa. Shot on a rangefinder, quiet domestic moment, soft window light, gentle shadows, nostalgic, natural skin texture.
