## Prompt

Create a breathtaking, individually crafted European travel illustration featuring [CITY NAME], capturing its most iconic landmark, distinctive architecture, charming streets, and beautiful atmosphere.

Render the scene as an exquisite traditional watercolor painting on warm ivory, antique textured paper. Blend delicate washes of cobalt blue, deep ocean blue, warm golden ochre, soft beige, and subtle blush pink. Incorporate expressive watercolor splashes, organic paint blooms, delicate ink details, and scattered golden accents throughout the composition.

ARTISTIC DIRECTION:

- Feature one instantly recognizable landmark or iconic cityscape as the main focal point.
- Capture the city's authentic architectural character and unmistakable atmosphere.
- Add subtle environmental details such as flowering branches, vintage street lamps, historic buildings, boats, trams, or waterfront scenery where appropriate.
- Use loose, artistic watercolor brushwork with beautiful pigment textures and natural paint bleeding.
- Maintain generous negative space with an elegant warm ivory background.
- Balance intricate architectural details with spontaneous watercolor splashes for an artistic, sophisticated finish.

MOOD & FINISH: Dreamlike, nostalgic, romantic, elegant, and timeless — resembling a premium European travel postcard painted by a master watercolor artist.

IMPORTANT: Every generation must depict one city only, with its own authentic landmarks and architecture. Avoid generic fantasy buildings, photorealism, digital 3D effects, heavy outlines, borders, or unnecessary typography.
