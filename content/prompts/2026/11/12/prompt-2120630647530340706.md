## Prompt

Surreal editorial portrait of a young woman styled as a modern Queen of Hearts, with fair, freckled skin, holding a vintage Queen playing card up to cover one eye while the other eye gazes directly at the camera with a calm, mysterious expression. Her pale green eye is framed by soft peach-orange eyeshadow, a hint of white shimmer at the inner corner, long lashes, and a defined auburn brow. A hand-painted red heart is on her cheekbone. Her lips are a bold, glossy, classic crimson red. Short, voluminous, tightly curled copper-red hair frames her face. She wears an ornate antique gold filigree crown encrusted with cabochon jewels in turquoise, green, amber, and ruby red. Her costume is a regal, baroque-inspired garment with a beaded pearl collar and rich panels of red, mustard yellow, teal, and gold brocade. Playing cards, including hearts and court cards, float and tumble around her in the foreground and background. The backdrop is a muted dusty teal-green, softly blurred.
