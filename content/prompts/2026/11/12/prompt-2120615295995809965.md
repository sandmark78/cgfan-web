## Prompt

Dramatic fashion portrait of a young woman with fair, softly glowing skin, her chin tilted up and her face turned slightly to the side, with a serene, dreamy, confident expression. Her long, voluminous copper-auburn hair is caught mid-air by the wind, whipping upward and outward in wild, flowing strands with vivid coral-red and fiery orange highlights, creating a dynamic halo of motion around her head. She wears small, thin round wire-framed sunglasses with mirrored teal-turquoise lenses that reflect the sky. Makeup is minimal and natural: defined brows, soft peachy blush, and satin rose-nude lips, slightly parted. She wears a simple black high-neck top that contrasts with the vibrant hair. The background is a dreamy, softly blurred sunset sky with a gradient of pink, coral, peach, and pale turquoise.

Lighting and Style:

Warm golden-hour backlight with a glowing rim light through the windblown hair, soft light on the face, saturated warm-meets-cool color palette of orange, coral, and teal, fashion editorial photography with a stylized, slightly painterly digital finish, ultra-detailed individual hair strands, motion-frozen hair, shallow depth of field, shot on an 85mm lens at f/2.0, low-angle composition, vertical 9:16 aspect ratio, ultra-realistic, vibrant cinematic color grading.
