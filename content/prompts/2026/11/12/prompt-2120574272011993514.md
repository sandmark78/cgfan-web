## Prompt

TIMELESS POSTCARD | VINTAGE WATERCOLOR CITY TRAVEL ART

Create a breathtaking, individually designed vintage European travel poster featuring [CITY NAME], capturing its distinctive architecture, historic streets, iconic landmarks, and timeless atmosphere.

Transform the city into an exquisite traditional watercolor illustration, reminiscent of an antique European travel postcard discovered in an old bookstore.

ARTISTIC DIRECTION:

Depict a beautiful, recognizable scene from the selected city, incorporating its most characteristic architectural landmarks, charming streets, rooftops, bridges, canals, gardens, or surrounding landscape, depending on the location.

Use delicate watercolor washes, softly blended pigments, subtle aged-paper textures, warm ivory and parchment tones, muted sage greens, dusty florals, faded terracotta, and gentle golden highlights.

Introduce subtle botanical details such as delicate flowering branches, autumn leaves, or small flowers around the edges, complementing the architecture without distracting from the main scene.

Create an atmospheric composition with soft morning light, delicate mist, painterly depth, and intricate architectural details.

TYPOGRAPHY & LAYOUT:

- Large, elegant classical serif typography displaying [CITY NAME] near the bottom.
- A short, original poetic tagline beneath the destination name, inspired by the city's character.
- A small, understated vintage collection label near the upper section.
- Fine decorative separators and delicate ornamental details.
- Beautifully balanced negative space and sophisticated editorial typography.

COMPOSITION:

Vertical 4:5 aspect ratio, rounded poster corners, carefully balanced foreground and background, premium fine-art print aesthetic.

OVERALL AESTHETIC:

Romantic European travel illustration, vintage botanical watercolor, antique postcards, timeless architectural storytelling, understated luxury, nostalgic atmosphere, and sophisticated travel editorial design.

IMPORTANT REQUIREMENTS:

Generate only ONE city poster per image. The city name, architectural landmarks, scenery, tagline, and decorative details must automatically adapt to whichever city is specified. Preserve the city's recognizable identity rather than using generic European architecture.

Every generation should feel like a unique, independently art-directed vintage travel poster. Avoid repeating the same composition across different cities. No collages, no multiple destinations, no mockups, no watermarks, no logos, and no modern graphic-design aesthetics.
