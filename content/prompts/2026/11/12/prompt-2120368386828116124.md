## Prompt

Create an exceptionally beautiful, detailed ink-wash and watercolor travel art poster featuring [LANDMARK NAME], [CITY], [COUNTRY].

1. ARTISTIC STYLE

Expressive black-ink architectural illustrations blended with sophisticated watercolor washes.
Traditional ink-wash painting combined with contemporary travel-poster aesthetics.
Visible watercolor textures, natural pigment bleeding, delicate ink splatters and handmade ivory paper texture.
A balance of precise architectural details and loose, expressive brushwork.

2. MAIN COMPOSITION

Feature one large, dominant and instantly recognizable landmark.
Preserve its authentic architectural proportions, distinctive structure and defining details.
Use expressive ink outlines, layered watercolor shading and subtle highlights.
Position the landmark prominently, creating a visually striking composition.

3. BACKGROUND & ENVIRONMENT

Include 2–3 recognizable architectural elements or attractions from the destination.
Incorporate authentic local surroundings, streets, waterfronts, bridges or landscape elements where appropriate.
Add atmospheric clouds, distant scenery and natural environmental details.
Create depth through layered watercolor washes and delicate ink drawing.
Avoid unnecessary decorative elements, flowers and floral branches unless they naturally belong to the scene.

4. TYPOGRAPHY

Feature oversized, expressive hand-painted brush lettering:
    “[LANDMARK NAME]”
Add elegant secondary typography:
    “[CITY]”
    “[COUNTRY]”
Use artistic calligraphy, balanced spacing and sophisticated placement.
Make the lettering an integral part of the artwork.

5. COLOUR PALETTE
Choose a destination-inspired palette that complements the landmark. Combine rich dark ink tones with carefully selected watercolor accents, subtle highlights and harmonious background shades.

6. LIGHTING & ATMOSPHERE

Cinematic natural lighting.
Soft atmospheric shadows.
Beautiful watercolor reflections where appropriate.
Subtle contrast between dark ink and luminous painted areas.

7. FINAL FINISH
Premium collectible travel-poster aesthetic, intricate architectural storytelling, expressive ink strokes, authentic watercolor granulation, elegant negative space, refined composition and gallery-quality illustration.

FORMAT: Vertical 2:3 portrait composition, high resolution, exceptionally detailed, visually balanced, professional artistic finish.

Every destination should have its own distinctive visual identity while maintaining the same signature ink-wash watercolor art direction.
