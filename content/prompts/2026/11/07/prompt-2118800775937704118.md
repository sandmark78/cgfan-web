## Prompt

She hangs out on rooftops, falls asleep in gutted cars, and collects tattoos the way other people used to keep photographs.

She's quick to anger, wears a defiant smile, and has this strange habit of laughing at the worst possible moments.

People say she's dangerous. Sometimes, they're right. She's not trying to set an example. She has her contradictions, her bad days, her reasons to throw a punch and her reasons to hold back.
