## Prompt

Use the uploaded photograph as the exact identity and appearance reference. Create a premium 3×2 photography contact sheet featuring the same woman in six distinct, beautiful autumn photoshoot poses.

IDENTITY & STYLING LOCK:
Preserve her exact facial features, face shape, eyes, smile, skin texture, hairstyle and natural appearance. Keep her original burgundy/maroon headscarf, pink outfit and oversized mustard-yellow knitted cardigan. Maintain the same clothing, accessories and overall styling in all six photographs.

THE SIX DIFFERENT POSES:

1. The Signature Smile: Standing in the autumn park, gently resting one hand beneath her chin, looking directly at the camera with a warm, natural smile.
2. The Looking Away Shot: Slightly turning her face towards the golden sunlight, looking thoughtfully into the distance, one hand gently holding her scarf.
3. The Playful Pose: Both hands holding the edges of her scarf, a genuine cheerful smile, slightly tilted head and expressive eyes.
4. The Candid Walking Shot: Walking along a pathway covered in golden autumn leaves, looking back over her shoulder towards the camera.
5. The Cozy Portrait: Sitting on a wooden park bench, hands wrapped around the sleeves of her knitted cardigan, smiling softly at the camera.
6. The Cinematic Close-Up: A beautifully composed close-up portrait, looking directly into the camera with a subtle confident smile, golden sunlight illuminating her face.

ENVIRONMENT & PHOTOGRAPHY:
A beautiful European-style autumn park filled with golden-yellow trees, fallen leaves, warm sunlight filtering through branches and a dreamy natural background.

VISUAL QUALITY:
Ultra-realistic professional DSLR photography, 85mm portrait lens, natural skin texture, beautiful depth of field, warm autumn color grading, soft golden-hour lighting, editorial fashion photography, realistic proportions and authentic expressions.

GRID COMPOSITION:
Arrange all six photographs in a perfectly aligned 3-column × 2-row grid. Each frame must show a different pose and camera composition. Maintain consistent identity, outfit, environment and lighting across all six images.

No duplicated poses, no face alterations, no artificial beauty filters, no text, no watermarks. Every frame should look like a separate photograph from the same professional autumn photoshoot.
