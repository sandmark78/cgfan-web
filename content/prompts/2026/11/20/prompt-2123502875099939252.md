## Prompt

Create an ultra-realistic, high-end fashion magazine cover photograph inspired by Harper’s Bazaar editorial photography.

Subject: A beautiful young woman with long, voluminous, naturally wavy dark brown hair, elegant facial features, flawless natural makeup, soft pink lips, defined eyes, and a confident, sophisticated expression. She is looking directly into the camera.

Outfit: An oversized royal-blue tailored blazer with structured shoulders, worn over a black inner top, paired with elegant high-waisted dark emerald-green tailored trousers and a matching green belt. Add a delicate silver necklace with a small star-shaped pendant and elegant silver earrings.
