## Prompt

Prompt: A charming, sun-drenched illustration in the style of a 1950s French children's picture book depicting a happy tortoise and her friend Céleste who runs a travelling patisserie from inside the turtoise enormous shell, which has been fitted with a miniature copper-domed oven, hand-painted tile countertops, and strings of marigold bunting. She stands on a mossy cobblestone lane in a Provençal village, carefully piping lavender cream onto a tower of choux pastries while a queue of field mice, a hedgehog in a beret, and one very tall grasshopper wait politely beneath a striped awning. Everything is rendered in warm, slightly faded gouache tones dusty rose, soft butter yellow, sage green with clean ink outlines and playful, rounded lettering on a hand-drawn chalkboard menu. The whole image radiates the unhurried, golden contentment of a perfect Tuesday afternoon.
