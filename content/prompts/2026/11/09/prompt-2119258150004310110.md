## Prompt

Create a premium vintage-inspired travel poster for [LANDMARK, CITY, COUNTRY], inspired by the artistic style of retro European travel postcards, illustrated postage stamps and layered paper collage.

CENTRAL COMPOSITION:
Feature the landmark as the dominant central subject, illustrated with beautiful architectural detail, realistic proportions and a subtle vintage engraved or hand-painted appearance.

COLLAGE DESIGN:

A large, torn-edge white paper title banner displaying the destination name in bold, colourful vintage typography.
Multiple overlapping pieces of brightly coloured paper arranged behind the landmark.
An antique city map as the background texture.
Vintage postage stamps featuring local symbols, architecture and cultural elements.
Old postal cancellation marks, passport stamps, handwritten travel notes and tiny airplane illustrations.
Small illustrated local transportation, decorative icons and travel memorabilia.
Irregular torn paper edges, visible paper fibres, subtle shadows and authentic printed textures.

UNIQUE COLOUR IDENTITY:
Give every landmark a completely different, specially curated colour palette. Select colours that complement the landmark’s architecture, history, atmosphere and cultural identity. Use a different dominant background colour, contrasting collage papers, stamp colours and typography for every new destination. Never repeat the exact same colour combination across the collection.

ART DIRECTION:
Retro 1950s travel advertising meets handmade scrapbook collage, vintage postal ephemera, illustrated architectural tourism poster, tactile paper layering, sophisticated colour blocking and charming nostalgic details.

FINISH:
Beautifully balanced composition, highly detailed landmark illustration, crisp typography, warm aged-paper background, authentic vintage print imperfections, premium collectible poster quality.

Vertical 4:5 composition. No modern digital UI, no photorealistic photography, no generic template appearance.
