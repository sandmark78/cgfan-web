## Prompt

Candid analog-style editorial photograph of a young woman laughing with pure joy in a heavy downpour on a European city street. Caught mid-movement, her head thrown back and tilted to the side, eyes squeezed shut, mouth wide open in a big natural laugh showing her teeth. Short, dark wet hair clinging to her face and neck, with loose strands whipping in the wind and rain. Glowing, sun-kissed skin slick with rain, bare shoulders and back, a few droplets suspended on her skin. She wears a strapless blush-peach silk corset gown with a boned bodice and a voluminous, soaked tulle-and-satin skirt, swirling and billowing with her motion. Water splashes and rain streaks fill the air around her, frozen mid-air. Background: a rainy, blurred Parisian-style street with grand stone neoclassical buildings, a hazy overcast sky, and the out-of-focus red taillights of passing cars creating warm bokeh. Soft, diffused overcast light, muted desaturated palette of gray-green and stone with the peach dress as the warm focal point. Shot on 35mm film (Kodak Portra 400), natural film grain, slight motion blur, shallow depth of field, spontaneous documentary feel, nostalgic, romantic, photorealistic, 8K.
