## Prompt

Surreal double-exposure digital art portrait that blends an extreme close-up of a woman’s face with a tropical ocean scene. Her eye, set in deep blue shadow, is partly overlaid by a silhouetted palm tree whose golden-green fronds sweep across the eyelid and brow like lashes. Her lips, in soft lavender-pink with a glossy sheen, sit in the lower left, half submerged by a calm horizon line where the sea meets the face. A glowing pink-and-coral sunset sky fills the cheek area, with the horizon cutting straight across the face at mid-frame. The skin is covered in glittering water droplets, sparkling dew, and tiny luminous particles that catch the light. Glowing turquoise and white bokeh orbs float across the lower right, with thin dark vine-like strands and tropical leaves curving through the frame.

Setting and atmosphere:

A dreamlike, bioluminescent night-ocean mood where the face and the landscape become one. The sea’s surface reflects in the lower face with horizontal ripples, glowing aqua light trails, and shimmering sparkles. Mist and glitter dust add depth, giving the scene an enchanted, underwater-meets-island feel.

Composition and framing:

Vertical portrait (9:16), extreme close-up with the face cropped off-center, so the eye, palm tree, and lips form a diagonal path. The horizon line acts as a strong horizontal divider, with the sunset above and the glowing sea below. Layered depth: sharp sparkles and droplets in the foreground, soft bokeh in the lower right, and a smooth gradient of deep navy in the corners.

Lighting:

Luminous, neon-like glow from within the image, with cool electric blue ambient light, soft magenta-pink highlights at the cheek, and bright cyan sparkle points. Strong specular glints on the droplets, a gentle bloom around the brightest lights, and dark vignette edges to focus attention on the eye and the horizon.

Color palette:

Dominant electric cobalt, sapphire, and deep navy, with turquoise and cyan glows, accented by soft coral-pink and lavender in the sunset and lips, and touches of olive-gold in the palm fronds. The warm pink and gold are the only counterpoints to the saturated blue.

Style and technical details:

Fantasy digital art, photomanipulation double exposure, ethereal and cinematic, ultra-detailed water droplets, glitter and bokeh particles, glowing blue color grading with subtle chromatic bloom, high contrast, 8K resolution, polished surreal beauty-poster look.
