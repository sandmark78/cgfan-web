## Prompt

Style: Dark cinematic fine-art portrait photography, moody editorial aesthetic, vintage film grain.

Subject: A mysterious woman with a captivating, melancholic expression and bold red lipstick.

Setting: Intimate, shadowy, nearly black background with an atmospheric, minimalist environment.

Lighting: Dramatic low-key lighting, soft warm directional light falling across part of the face, deep shadows, subtle highlights and natural light falloff.

Main Prompt: Create an image of a mysterious woman captured in an extremely intimate close-up portrait, with long dark hair loosely falling across her face and partially obscuring her gaze. Her expression is quietly melancholic, intense, and enigmatic, with slightly parted, richly red lips creating a striking focal point. A few delicate strands of hair cross the illuminated portion of her face, adding an effortless, cinematic feeling. Use dramatic chiaroscuro lighting, with a soft golden glow revealing only parts of the face while the remaining areas disappear into deep, velvety shadows. Incorporate subtle analog film grain, soft focus, rich blacks, muted warm tones, and a raw editorial photography aesthetic, vertical portrait format 3:4

Negative Prompt: no text, no watermark, no excessive retouching, no artificial skin texture, no distorted facial proportions, no extra features, no distracting background, no oversaturation, no cartoon effect.
