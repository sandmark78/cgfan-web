## Prompt

High-speed splash portrait of a young woman emerging through a wall of crystal-clear water, her face calm and direct while a violent splash freezes around her. Pale steel-blue eyes with bright catchlights, dark defined brows, and a steady, intense gaze into the camera. Fair, warm-toned skin with fine freckles across the cheeks and nose, a natural flush, and tiny water droplets clinging to her face. Soft, natural rose-nude lips, slightly parted, with a few droplets on the lower lip. Hair pulled up into a loose, messy bun with damp wisps of light brown hair falling across the forehead and temples. Her hands are folded loosely near her chest at the bottom of the frame, partially veiled by the water. A single droplet hovers in front of her nose, with others suspended mid-air around her face.

Water details:

Frozen-in-time splash with intricate, glassy water formations: sharp crown-like splashes, ribbons of water, tiny suspended beads, and translucent foam. Realistic refraction and caustics through the water, with some areas slightly distorting the skin beneath, and a thin wet film over the lower face. Depth is created by layers of water in front of and behind the face.

Setting and background:

Soft, hazy, desaturated blue-teal backdrop that merges into the water, giving a clean, aquatic, studio-like feel with no distracting elements.

Composition and framing:

Vertical portrait (9:16), face in the upper-middle of the frame with eyes in the upper third. The splash forms a natural frame around the face, sweeping from the left edge diagonally across the lower half. Shallow depth of field, with foreground water softly blurred and the eyes and facial water droplets razor-sharp.

Lighting:

Soft, diffused studio lighting from the front and slightly above, with strobe-like freeze-motion clarity. Gentle specular highlights on the water surfaces, luminous skin, a cool-toned ambient fill, and warm skin tones that contrast with the blue water. Subtle backlight rim on the water edges for sparkle.

Color palette:

Cool aqua, ice blue, and steel teal for the water and background, against warm peach, tan, and rose skin tones, with soft chestnut brown for the hair. The blue eyes tie the face to the water.

Style and technical details:
