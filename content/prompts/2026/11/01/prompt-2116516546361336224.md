## Prompt

Prompt: A hyper-saturated, neon-soaked digital illustration of a late-night Seoul street food market in the middle of a summer downpour, rendered with the kinetic energy of 2000s graffiti muralism fused with ukiyo-e woodblock compositional geometry. Rain hammers corrugated awnings stretched between crooked utility poles tangled in electrical wire, while a teenage girl in an oversized yellow rain slicker stands at a pojangmacha cart, both hands wrapped around a steaming paper cup, her face half-lit by the magenta glow of a flickering tteokbokki sign reflected in the flooded asphalt below. Vendors in the background are blurred motion-streaks of orange and red. Every reflective surface puddles, plastic sheeting, wet ceramic bowls contains a distorted duplicate world. The mood is simultaneously exhausted and electric, lonely and alive.
