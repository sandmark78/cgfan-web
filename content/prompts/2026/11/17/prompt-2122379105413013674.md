## Prompt

SECOND WORLD PHOTOGRAPHY | REALITY MEETS IMAGINATION

Transform an ordinary real-world photograph into a creative visual storytelling artwork where reality seamlessly transitions into a whimsical, hand-drawn second world.

COMPOSITION & STRUCTURE

Create a vertically composed artwork divided into two connected sections:

- Upper section: A highly realistic, photorealistic photograph of an everyday scene, such as a city street, modern building, library, park, bridge, landscape, or architectural location. Preserve authentic photography, natural lighting, realistic textures, accurate proportions, and rich environmental details.
- Lower section: Extend the scene into a warm ivory, lightly textured sketchbook-paper surface. Reimagine selected elements of the original photograph as delicate, minimalist black-ink illustrations, creating an imaginative continuation of the real-world environment.

THE SECOND WORLD CONCEPT

Introduce a small, charming hand-drawn human character interacting creatively with the photographed environment. The illustration should create a clever visual illusion, as though the person is painting, building, extending, repairing, or transforming something from the original photograph.

Examples include painting clouds onto a glass building, extending a real street with hand-drawn roads, transforming library stairs into enormous books, or allowing trees, architecture, and other elements to escape their photographic boundaries.

ARTISTIC STYLE

Combine professional architectural photography with delicate minimalist pen-and-ink doodles. Use thin black sketch lines, loose handwritten details, subtle imperfections, and warm cream paper textures. Maintain a sophisticated, editorial art direction with a playful surrealist touch.

STORYTELLING DETAILS

Add one short, poetic handwritten English sentence near the illustration, expressing the imaginative idea behind the scene. Keep the handwriting subtle, natural, and elegant.

IMPORTANT

The transition between photography and illustration must feel visually connected and conceptually meaningful. Avoid random doodles, excessive decorations, cartoon styling, or clutter. The result should feel like a photograph revealing a hidden imaginary world beneath reality.

Ultra-detailed, artistic, visually intelligent, photorealistic, minimalist, surreal storytelling photography, premium creative editorial aesthetic.
