## Prompt

Ultra-realistic vertical portrait (9:16) of an ethereal woman in a modern, sculptural high-fashion beauty editorial style. Profile-to-three-quarter pose, with her bare shoulder turned toward the camera, her head turned back over the shoulder, and her chin slightly lifted. She gazes at the viewer with a serene, poised, slightly aloof expression.

HAIR: Long, pin-straight platinum-silver blonde hair slicked back from the forehead into a very high, sleek ponytail that falls in a glossy, weightless cascade down her back. A lush cluster of three oversized pastel-yellow peony blooms is anchored at the crown, with layered ruffled petals and soft golden-orange centers.

MAKEUP: Porcelain, dewy skin with a glowing champagne highlight on the cheekbone, temple, and nose bridge. Soft peachy-bronze contour and blush. Subtle warm-taupe eyeshadow, defined arched brows, and a fine, sharp black winged liner with long, voluminous, feathered lashes that frame her pale gray-blue eyes. Nude rose-beige lips with a satin-gloss finish and a fuller, softly defined shape.

OUTFIT: An off-the-shoulder butter-yellow gown with voluminous organza ruffles at the neckline, leaving the shoulders and back bare and sculpted. The fabric is crisp and airy, with a soft translucent sheen.

JEWELRY: Long, linear crystal-and-diamond drop earrings with a round stud top, which catch the light and echo the vertical line of her neck. No other jewelry, which keeps the look clean and minimal.

BACKGROUND: A 3D geometric faceted wall in white and pale gray, with origami-like triangular panels that create soft angular shadows and a modern, architectural feel. A subtle depth gradient keeps it from competing with the subject.

LIGHTING: Soft, diffused high-key studio lighting from a large softbox at front-left. Gentle wrap-around fill, luminous skin highlights, delicate shadows in the facets of the wall, and a soft glow on the bare shoulder and back.

COLOR PALETTE: Platinum silver, butter yellow, pearl white, soft gray, warm peach skin tones, and champagne highlights.

STYLE & CAMERA: High-end beauty and haute couture editorial photography with a clean, airy, spring-fresh mood that blends floral romance with modern minimalism. Shot on an 85mm f/2.0 lens with shallow depth of field, tack-sharp focus on the eyes and lashes, and soft falloff toward the hair and background. Hyper-detailed skin texture, soft cinematic color grading, 8K resolution, photorealistic.

NEGATIVE PROMPT: cartoon, illustration, 3D render, plastic skin, over-smoothing, distorted anatomy, deformed face, asymmetrical eyes, blurry, low resolution, watermark, text, logo, oversaturated, harsh shadows, cluttered background, fake-looking flowers
