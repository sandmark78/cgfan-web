## Prompt

UNIVERSAL PHOTO-TO-3D PAPER-CUT EDITORIAL POSTER PROMPT
Use the uploaded image as the primary and exclusive visual reference. Automatically identify the main subject, environment, objects, colors, lighting, composition, and distinctive details. Transform it into a premium, photorealistic 3:4 vertical editorial poster combining realistic photography with an elaborate three-dimensional layered paper-cut book illustration.
OVERALL COMPOSITION:
Create two vertically integrated sections with a seamless transition, without white lines, borders, frames, or visible gaps.
TOP SECTION — ORIGINAL PHOTOGRAPH:
Preserve the uploaded photograph in its original photographic appearance. Maintain the exact subject, pose, environment, colors, lighting, perspective, and fine details. Do not redraw, stylize, replace, or modify the original photograph. Keep the scene visually rich and realistic.
BOTTOM SECTION — 3D OPEN PAPER-CUT BOOK:
Create a beautifully opened, antique ivory-beige hardcover book occupying the lower half of the composition. The book should feature multiple thick, individually visible pages with realistic stacked edges, slightly curled corners, and natural paper texture.
Inside the open book, recreate the exact same subject and environment through intricate, multi-layered 3D paper-cut artwork. Include a large central cutout window revealing the scene, surrounded by delicate hand-cut silhouettes and overlapping paper layers. Preserve the original photograph's colors, recognizable details, and overall atmosphere.
Add contextually appropriate decorative elements such as stars, clouds, trees, flowers, mountains, buildings, or other environmental details depending on the uploaded image. Use realistic paper depth, carefully cut edges, subtle folds, soft side lighting, and natural shadows to create a convincing pop-up book effect.
EDITORIAL TYPOGRAPHY:
Place an elegant vintage parchment banner across the upper portion of the open book. Add a short, meaningful poetic sentence in Traditional Chinese inspired by the photograph, with a smaller English translation directly underneath. Include fine horizontal divider lines and subtle decorative ornaments around the typography.
Ensure the Chinese characters are correctly rendered and visually balanced.
VISUAL STYLE:
Warm vintage paper aesthetic, antique ivory and champagne-beige tones, sophisticated editorial art direction, intricate paper engineering, realistic layered cutouts, delicate handcrafted details, dimensional shadows, soft cinematic lighting, and premium photorealistic textures.
IMPORTANT REQUIREMENTS:
- The original photograph must remain untouched in the upper section.
- The lower illustration must depict the exact same subject and scene.
- Maintain visual continuity between photography and paper-cut artwork.
- Make the book look physically realistic, with visible thickness, layered pages, and genuine cutout depth.
- Avoid flat illustrations, cartoon aesthetics, artificial plastic textures, and excessive decorations.
- Adapt the Chinese poetic headline and decorative elements to the uploaded subject rather than using a fixed theme.
FINAL RESULT: A sophisticated 3:4 vertical vintage editorial poster that seamlessly combines an original realistic photograph with an elaborate three-dimensional, layered paper-cut interpretation presented inside an antique open book.
