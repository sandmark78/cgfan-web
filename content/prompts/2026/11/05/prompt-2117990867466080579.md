## Prompt

For this portrait, I wanted to preserve the soul of traditional Chinese beauty while capturing the natural imperfections of Leica photography — the movement of hair, the softness of light, the texture of silk, and that fleeting moment between stillness and motion.

My goal isn’t to make AI images look perfect. It’s to make them feel real.

Photography at first glance, a cinematic frame at second, and something resembling an oil painting the longer you look.
