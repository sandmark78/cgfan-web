## Prompt

Dark, atmospheric, half-face close-up portrait of a mysterious woman emerging from pure black shadow, with only the right side of her face visible at the edge of the frame. Her amber-gold eye stares directly at the viewer with a fierce, hypnotic, secretive intensity, framed by a heavy dark brow and smudged black kohl. Thin black streaks run down her cheek like melted mascara tears, with fine black speckles and natural freckles across her warm, textured skin with visible pores. Deep crimson-red lips are partly visible, and she presses a slender finger to them in a “hush” gesture, topped with a long, pointed, glossy black stiletto nail.

Wild, messy hair in deep teal and midnight blue, with loose flyaway strands. Long braids are woven with feathers in crimson red and dark teal, with antique silver and bronze bead wraps and metal cuffs. Her hand carries ornate engraved silver rings and faint dark henna- or tribal-style tattoos on the finger and wrist.
