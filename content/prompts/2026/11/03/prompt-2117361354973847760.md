## Prompt

Stylized graphic-novel digital illustration of a young woman with an intense, curious, slightly startled expression, front-facing close-up from head to chest, her chin slightly lifted and lips parted. She wears oversized black thick-rimmed rectangular glasses with translucent pale teal-green lenses that tint and distort her large green-gray eyes, with long dark lashes visible behind the glass and highlights reflecting across the lenses. Her skin is warm cream and peach, rendered in smooth cel-shaded planes, with bold burnt-orange contour lines and shadow shapes sweeping across the cheeks and under the glasses like graphic war paint, and soft coral-orange glossy lips with a bright specular highlight. Her hair is a tousled copper-auburn bob with long wispy fringe falling over her forehead, with golden highlights and loose black-brown strands flying outward at the sides, drawn in dynamic, flowing ink-like strokes. A glossy orange translucent cap or visor shape arcs across the top of the frame with drip-like vertical lines. She wears a high-collared rust-orange jacket with angular folds, rendered in chunky faceted brush strokes with navy shadows.

Background: a deep midnight-navy and black backdrop with abstract cityscape or circuit-like vertical streaks in steel blue and pale gray, floating droplets, paint splatters, small bokeh circles and geometric fragments, with glints of orange scattered through the dark.

Lighting: warm orange key light from the front with cool blue rim light and reflected light on the left side of the face and neck, strong complementary orange-and-blue contrast, glossy highlights on the lenses, lips and hair.

Style: modern sci-fi comic book cover art, cyberpunk graphic novel illustration, semi-realistic digital painting with bold ink linework and expressive brush texture, Sam Spratt meets Ashley Wood and Yoji Shinkawa, limited palette of burnt orange, navy, teal and cream, poster composition, vertical 9:16 aspect ratio, 8k, masterpiece, highly detailed.
