## Prompt

Create an ultra-realistic, high-fashion streetwear editorial photograph of a beautiful young adult Western woman with flawless natural skin, striking facial features, dark expressive eyes, and glossy black hair styled in a high bun. She wears bold red lipstick, subtle winged eyeliner, and silver hoop earrings.

Outfit: An oversized black cropped hoodie covered in vibrant neon-green graffiti typography, matching black pleated mini skirt with neon-green graphic prints, a statement utility belt, black crew socks, and chunky black platform sneakers with bright green laces. She carries a matching black-and-neon-green designer duffle bag.

Pose & Background: Standing confidently with one hand resting casually near her skirt, looking directly at the camera. A dark industrial streetwear studio surrounds her, featuring black graffiti-covered walls, neon-green crowns, bold graphic lettering, metallic barrels, and a glossy reflective floor.
