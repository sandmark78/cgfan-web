## Prompt

Surreal fantasy portrait split into two halves, a double-exposure composition of two women’s faces merging down the center. On the left, a woman with luminous fair skin, piercing pale blue-gray eyes, bold electric-blue and teal glittering eyeshadow with a sharp winged liner, thick dark sculpted brow, and glossy burnt-orange lips. Her face and headscarf are covered in intricate turquoise and gold filigree patterns, like cracked gilded lace and molten metallic paint, with turquoise liquid paint dripping down her cheek and chin. A flowing teal and gold textured fabric veil frames her head. On the right, a second woman with deep bronze skin, warm amber-gold eyes, smoldering burnt-orange and crimson eye makeup, and deep red lips, half hidden behind a dense bouquet of velvety crimson, burgundy, magenta and royal-purple flowers with gold-dusted stamens. Large, richly detailed butterflies with iridescent teal, emerald, amber and orange wings and gold-veined patterns perch across the center of the composition, on the flowers, and around her cheek. Left background in blazing orange and gold with melting paint texture, right background in dark plum and deep violet.
