## Prompt

STILLNESS IN MOTION | CINEMATIC STREETWEAR EDITORIAL

Create an ultra-photorealistic, high-end streetwear editorial photograph using the uploaded image as the identity reference. Preserve the subject's exact facial identity, natural facial features, skin tone, hairstyle, and recognizable appearance without beautification or alteration.

SUBJECT & STYLING:
The subject is standing naturally in a busy modern metropolitan transit station, wearing an oversized premium dark teal-grey hoodie with a minimalist design, relaxed shoulders, and both hands casually tucked inside the front kangaroo pocket. The hoodie should feature realistic heavyweight cotton texture, natural folds, and subtle fabric detailing. Maintain a calm, confident expression with a thoughtful gaze directed slightly away from the camera.

CINEMATIC MOTION & COMPOSITION:
Capture the subject in perfectly sharp focus while crowds of commuters move rapidly around them in both directions. Create dramatic horizontal motion blur across the background, with beautifully stretched silhouettes, subtle movement trails, and realistic directional blur. The subject remains completely motionless, creating a striking contrast between stillness and the chaos surrounding them.

Use a medium three-quarter portrait composition, eye-level camera perspective, shallow depth of field, and a 50mm editorial photography aesthetic. The environment should feature modern glass architecture, muted urban tones, and natural ambient station lighting.

LIGHTING & REALISM:
Use soft, cool-toned cinematic lighting with realistic skin texture, natural shadows, delicate highlights, and subtle atmospheric depth. Maintain exceptional photographic realism, accurate anatomy, authentic fabric physics, and professional colour grading.

SIGNATURE DETAIL:
Include a small, clean rectangular inset of the original uploaded photograph in the bottom-left corner, like a subtle before-and-after editorial reference. Keep it proportionate and unobtrusive.

FINAL AESTHETIC:
Luxury urban fashion campaign meets cinematic street photography. The result should feel like an actual photograph captured on a professional full-frame camera, not an AI illustration. Extremely detailed, naturally composed, visually compelling, and editorial-quality.
