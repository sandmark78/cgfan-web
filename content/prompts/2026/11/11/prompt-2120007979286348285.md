## Prompt

Create an exceptionally creative, colourful, hand-drawn travel art poster featuring [LANDMARK NAME], [CITY], [COUNTRY].

1. ARTISTIC COMPOSITION

Create a playful architectural illustration showcasing the destination’s most iconic landmarks.

Include:

* One large, dominant landmark in detailed black-ink line drawing.
* Two or three smaller architectural landmarks positioned around the main subject.
* Distinctive local buildings, monuments, towers, bridges, domes or historical structures.
* Simplified architectural sketches with expressive hand-drawn outlines.
* Preserve the recognisable shapes and identity of the actual landmarks.

2. COLOURFUL GEOMETRIC BACKGROUND

Create an artistic patchwork background using large rectangular and irregular geometric colour blocks.

Use overlapping sections of different sizes, with visible brush strokes, rough painted textures and subtle canvas grain.

IMPORTANT: Automatically create a completely unique colour palette for every new destination.

Choose colours inspired by the country’s culture, flag, architecture, landscape and artistic traditions.

Do not repeat the same colour combination for different countries or cities.

3. HAND-DRAWN CULTURAL DETAILS

Surround the architecture with charming illustrated elements associated with the destination.

Examples:

* Local transportation, bicycles, trams, boats or vintage cars.
* Traditional food, drinks and cultural objects.
* Local street signs, decorative lamps and architectural ornaments.
* Small people in traditional or locally appropriate clothing.
* Hearts, stars, birds, clouds and whimsical decorative sketches.
* Local symbols, flowers or plants only when appropriate to the destination.

Keep these elements playful, artistic and naturally scattered throughout the composition.

4. DESTINATION NAME & TYPOGRAPHY

Make the destination name an essential part of the artwork.

Include:

[LANDMARK NAME]
[CITY NAME]
[COUNTRY NAME]

Add the destination’s native-language name where appropriate.

Use a mixture of handwritten lettering, elegant cursive typography, playful uppercase text and small handwritten annotations.

Include short destination-inspired words and cultural expressions.

5. ARTISTIC STYLE

Contemporary hand-drawn architectural illustration, expressive black ink outlines, colourful geometric collage, playful editorial art, imperfect sketchbook drawings, mixed-media painting and modern European poster design.

Use slightly irregular lines, visible pencil marks, rough brush textures and charming handmade imperfections.

6. FINAL PRESENTATION

The composition should feel like an original piece of contemporary travel artwork created by an imaginative illustrator.

Bold, joyful, colourful, artistic and visually rich without becoming cluttered.

Square 1:1 composition, premium art-print quality, balanced layout, crisp architectural linework, beautiful colour harmony.

Avoid photorealism, 3D rendering, generic clipart, repetitive layouts and excessive decorative elements.
