🤯 Portrait posters hit different when distortion becomes part of the design!

Instead of adding effects on top, I let refraction, motion, time and scanning reshape both the portrait and the typography.

1. VEIL — glass refraction
2. PULSE — long-exposure motion trails
3. SIGNAL — diagonal optical scanning
4. TRACE — sliced time displacement

✨ Great for fashion, music, exhibitions and editorial visuals!
