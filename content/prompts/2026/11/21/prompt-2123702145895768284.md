## Prompt

Prompt: A bustling flower market tucked away in a narrow cobblestone street of an old European city, illuminated by hundreds of glowing lanterns and warm shop windows beneath a deep indigo night sky. Beautiful overflowing flower stalls display extravagant bouquets of dahlias, peonies, roses, and exotic blossoms in vibrant shades of magenta, coral, tangerine, violet, and electric blue. Elegant old buildings with ornate balconies frame the scene, their weathered facades covered in flowering vines. A gentle evening rain has left the cobblestones glistening, reflecting the colorful flowers and golden lantern light.
