## Prompt

A vertical 9:16 ultra-detailed expressive oil painting portrait of a young woman with shoulder-length, soft wavy chestnut-brown hair with warm caramel highlights, parted in the center and falling loosely around her face. She peeks out from behind a half-open, weathered wooden door, one hand lightly tucking a strand of hair behind her ear and the other resting on the edge of the door. She looks directly at the viewer with large, expressive hazel-brown eyes, strong natural brows, a calm, curious, slightly guarded expression and soft coral-red lips. She has warm, rosy, sun-kissed skin with a natural flush on the cheeks and nose and a small gold hoop earring.

She wears a simple olive-green fitted tank top with a scooped neckline, under a chunky cream cable-knit cardigan that slips off one shoulder, painted with thick, ridged, sculptural brushstrokes to suggest the knit texture.

Setting: an old, rustic wooden door and frame with heavily chipped, peeling paint in ochre-yellow, cream and rust-brown, which reveals the raw wood beneath. A dark iron latch and keyhole plate are visible on the right edge. Behind her is a deep, shadowy interior in near-black green and brown, which makes her face glow.

Lighting: warm, soft, directional light from the upper left, with luminous highlights on the forehead, nose, cheek and collarbone, gentle shadows on the right side of the face and a rich golden glow on the skin. Color palette: olive green, cream, ochre, burnt sienna, warm peach skin tones, chestnut brown and deep black-green.

Composition: vertical three-quarter portrait, the subject framed by the door frame on both sides, the face as the sharpest focal point, with heavy impasto, visible palette-knife and bristle strokes and rough canvas texture throughout.

Style: contemporary romantic realism, alla prima oil painting, bold, tactile brushwork, intimate and cinematic mood, rich warm color grading, museum-quality fine art, ultra-detailed, 8K.
