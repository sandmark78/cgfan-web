## Prompt

A vertical 9:16 ultra-detailed vintage pulp-fiction style oil painting of a striking woman with long, sleek, jet-black hair with straight blunt bangs, half pulled back, the rest falling over her shoulder. She stands inside an old public phone booth, pressing a black rotary-style handset to her ear with one hand while her other arm folds across her waist. Her eyes glance sideways with a startled, intense, dramatic expression and her lips are parted as if mid-sentence, caught in a tense, urgent phone call. She has warm olive skin with a glossy sheen, bold winged eyeliner, heavy dark lashes, sharply defined brows, glossy deep red-brown lips and a small gold drop earring.

She wears an olive-green off-the-shoulder cropped top, twisted and draped asymmetrically across one bare shoulder, with a dark green skirt. A coiled black telephone cord spirals down from the handset across her body.

Setting: a cramped, dimly lit vintage pay-phone booth with a black metal payphone mounted on the left wall, with a coin slot, a small red indicator and a worn label panel. The walls are dark brown wood and muted olive-grey plaster, with a patch of bright cream light falling on the right wall and glass panes at the left edge.

Lighting: dramatic, high-contrast, low-key lighting with a strong warm key light from the upper right, which creates bright highlights on the shoulder, cheek and forehead, with deep brown-black shadows in the booth. Color palette: olive green, burnt umber, ochre, warm bronze skin tones, jet black and cream.

Composition: tight three-quarter portrait, the subject filling most of the frame, her head turned in profile, the curling phone cord leading the eye down, with thick, visible palette-knife and bristle-brush strokes, heavy impasto and rough canvas texture.

Style: 1950s-1960s pulp-paperback cover illustration, film noir, American mid-century illustrative realism, bold painterly brushwork, dramatic and suspenseful mood, rich warm color grading, ultra-detailed, 8K.
