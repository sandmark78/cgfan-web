## Prompt

SNOWFALL LAKESIDE SELFIE | CINEMATIC WINTER PHOTOGRAPHY

Transform the uploaded reference photo into an ultra-realistic, aesthetically beautiful winter travel selfie captured beside a peaceful alpine lake during snowfall.

1. SUBJECT & IDENTITY — CRITICAL
   Preserve the exact facial identity, facial structure, eyes, nose, lips, skin tone, and natural features of the person in the uploaded photograph. Maintain realistic skin texture and natural proportions. Do not beautify, reshape, or alter the person's identity.

2. OUTFIT & APPEARANCE
   The subject wears:

- A black oversized knitted beanie covered with tiny snowflakes.
- A black oversized padded puffer jacket with a cozy winter aesthetic.
- Long, naturally flowing dark hair, slightly windswept, with a few strands falling across the face.

3. POSE & CAMERA ANGLE
   Create an intimate, spontaneous front-camera selfie composition. The subject extends one arm toward the camera, with their hand prominently visible in the foreground, creating a natural wide-angle perspective. The face remains the primary focal point, with a relaxed, slightly playful expression and natural eye contact.

4. ENVIRONMENT & BACKGROUND
   Place the subject beside a breathtaking alpine lake surrounded by snow-covered evergreen forests, rugged rocks, and distant mountains disappearing into soft winter fog.

The lake should have dark, gently rippling water. Snow-covered pine trees frame the left side, while the open lake extends into the background.

5. WEATHER & ATMOSPHERE
   Heavy, beautiful snowflakes fall naturally throughout the scene. Some snowflakes appear close to the camera lens, creating realistic foreground depth. Light snow accumulates on the beanie, jacket, rocks, and surrounding trees.

Create a peaceful, cold, atmospheric winter environment with subtle mist and overcast skies.

6. PHOTOGRAPHY & VISUAL STYLE

- Ultra-photorealistic smartphone selfie photography.
- Natural front-camera wide-angle perspective.
- Realistic foreground hand distortion and depth.
- Soft, diffused natural daylight.
- Muted charcoal, icy grey, and cool winter tones.
- Authentic skin texture and realistic hair details.
- Cinematic depth and atmospheric perspective.
- Candid travel photography aesthetic.
- Beautifully balanced exposure with natural contrast.

7. FINAL OUTPUT
   Vertical 9:16 composition, exceptionally realistic photographic quality, natural imperfections, detailed snow particles, authentic winter atmosphere, and premium editorial travel photography.

The final image must look like a genuine spontaneous winter selfie taken during a snowy mountain getaway, NOT a digital painting, illustration, or artificial AI-generated portrait.

No text, no logos, no watermarks, no borders.
