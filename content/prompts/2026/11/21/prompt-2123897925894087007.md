## Prompt

A vertical 9:16 ultra-detailed vintage oil painting of a striking, confident woman with big, voluminous, long curly hair in golden-brown and caramel tones, tossed back as she leans her head against the chair with her eyes half-closed and her chin lifted, in a relaxed, languid, self-assured pose. She has warm olive skin with a luminous sheen, bold dramatic smoky eye makeup with long lashes, strong defined brows and deep red-wine lips. She wears large ornate gold hoop earrings with dangling filigree details, a thin gold chain necklace, and stacks of thin gold and silver bangles on both wrists.

She wears a bohemian two-piece outfit in deep burgundy-wine cotton: an off-the-shoulder cropped blouse with puffed, gathered sleeves, a ruffled lace-trimmed hem and a tie-front knot, paired with a long, full, flowing maxi skirt in heavy, sculptural folds. She wears high-heeled strappy sandals in glossy chestnut-brown leather with ankle straps and buckles, with her legs crossed casually at the ankle.

She sits back in an antique dark-wood carved chair with a leather seat, one hand resting on her thigh and the other on the chair arm.

Setting: a warm, dim, vintage interior with muted olive-beige walls. On the upper left hangs an antique round wall clock with ornate brass detailing. On the right hangs an old gold-framed painting of two figures in dark, earthy tones. On the left stands an ornate carved wooden cabinet topped with brass vases and dried flowers. The floor has a worn, patterned Persian-style rug in deep brown, rust and gold.

Lighting: warm, soft, low-key lamplight from the left, with a golden glow on the shoulders, collarbone and arms, deep, moody shadows in the corners and a rich satin sheen on the fabric. Color palette: burgundy, wine red, gold, chestnut brown, olive-beige, bronze skin tones and dusty cream.

Composition: full-length vertical composition, the subject slightly off-center in a diagonal pose, the clock and painting framing the top, the long skirt filling the center, with thick palette-knife texture, visible brushwork and canvas grain.

Style: classical romantic realism, Old Masters-inspired oil painting with a bohemian, gypsy-inspired twist, heavy impasto, sultry, theatrical and nostalgic mood, rich warm color grading, museum-quality fine art, ultra-detailed, 8K.
