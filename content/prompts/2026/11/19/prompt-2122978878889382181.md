## Prompt

Prompt: An irresistibly adorable baby seal resting on a smooth, weathered wooden jetty in a quiet northern fishing harbor, having discovered a bright red knitted woollen scarf left behind by a fisherman. The scarf is loosely draped around its round, plump body, with one end trailing into the water, while the seal gazes up with enormous dark eyes and a wonderfully innocent expression.
