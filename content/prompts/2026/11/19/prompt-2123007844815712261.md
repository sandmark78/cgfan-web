## Prompt

Create an authentic 1990s-inspired lifestyle fashion photograph featuring a sporty young woman casually relaxing on a classic wooden park bench. Capture the scene from an unusually low camera position, almost at ground level, looking upward to create a striking perspective with the subject naturally dominating the frame.

She is seated sideways on the bench in a relaxed, effortlessly confident pose, one knee slightly raised while the other leg extends naturally downward. One arm rests casually across the wooden backrest as she turns her head toward the camera, making direct eye contact with a subtle, self-assured expression. Her body language should feel spontaneous and unposed, like an unexpected moment captured during a morning jog.

Dress her in authentic 1990s athletic streetwear: a retro color-blocked zip-up track jacket, sporty fitted shorts, classic white crew socks, and vintage running sneakers. Add understated sporty accessories and natural, effortless hair styling.

ENVIRONMENT & COMPOSITION: A beautiful urban park surrounded by lush green trees, with sunlight filtering through the foliage and a rich azure-blue sky filling the upper background. Frame the photograph with strong architectural perspective, keeping the wooden bench, the subject's full styling, and the surrounding environment visually connected. Allow natural sunlight to create soft highlights and realistic shadows.

PHOTOGRAPHIC DIRECTION: Shot on a vintage 35mm analog film camera using a wide-angle lens, extreme low-angle perspective, editorial medium-wide framing, slightly imperfect candid composition, authentic skin texture, natural proportions, realistic fabric details, subtle organic film grain, gentle color fading, soft contrast, and nostalgic 1990s color science.

OVERALL MOOD: Effortlessly cool, youthful, sporty, nostalgic, candid, and editorial. The photograph should feel like an authentic forgotten frame from a 1996 street-fashion magazine rather than a modern digitally polished fashion campaign.

IMPORTANT: Prioritize realistic anatomy, natural body proportions, believable posture, accurate hands and feet, genuine facial expression, and photographic realism. Avoid artificial skin smoothing, excessive retouching, exaggerated posing, oversaturated colors, and contemporary fashion aesthetics.
