## Prompt

Mesmerizing underwater portrait of a young woman floating just beneath the surface of a tropical sea, her face centered and facing the camera with a calm, serene, direct gaze. Long, dark brown hair drifts around her in the water with loose strands floating across her forehead. Aqua-green eyes with long lashes, thick defined brows, natural freckles across her nose and cheeks, dewy sun-kissed skin, and soft, full rose-nude lips slightly parted. Dappled shafts of sunlight ripple through the surface, casting bright caustic light patterns across her forehead and nose. Around her, clusters of crystal-clear air bubbles of different sizes catch the light, and small tropical butterflyfish in yellow, white and black stripes swim past in the foreground and sides, some softly out of focus. Deep teal and turquoise water that darkens to inky blue-black at the edges, a palette of teal, aqua, warm golden skin and lemon yellow, soft volumetric light rays from above, 35mm underwater lens at f/2.8, shallow depth of field focused on the eyes, hyper-detailed skin, water droplets, bubbles and fish scales, natural cinematic color grading, vertical 9:16 composition, high-end underwater editorial photography.
