## Prompt

Create an exceptionally beautiful fine-art architectural photograph featuring LANDMARK / BUILDING / CITY, COUNTRY.

1. Architectural composition

Feature striking white geometric architecture with clean cubic forms, sharp edges and sculptural volumes.
Incorporate asymmetrical walls, recessed windows, rectangular openings and minimal architectural details.
Use overlapping structures to create interesting depth and perspective.
Highlight the contrast between solid architectural forms and open sky.
Preserve the distinctive identity of the chosen landmark.

2. Colour and atmosphere

Brilliant Mediterranean cobalt-blue sky.
Pure white and soft ivory architectural surfaces.
Subtle cool grey shadows.
Natural sunlight creating beautiful tonal transitions.
A restrained palette with no unnecessary colours.

3. Photography style

High-end architectural fine-art photography.
Minimalist editorial composition.
Strong geometric lines and negative space.
Crisp details and realistic plaster textures.
Beautiful natural daylight.
Slightly dreamy yet photorealistic atmosphere.
Sophisticated contemporary art-gallery aesthetic.

4. Composition

Portrait 4:5 format.
Architectural structure occupying the right and lower portions.
Large uninterrupted blue sky providing negative space.
Interesting close-up perspective, slightly looking upwards.
No people, cars, flowers or unnecessary decorations.

Overall aesthetic: Mediterranean modernism meets contemporary fine-art photography. Timeless, serene, elegant, minimal and visually captivating.
