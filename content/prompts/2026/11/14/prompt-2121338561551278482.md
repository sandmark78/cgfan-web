## Prompt

Cinematic fashion portrait of a striking young woman peeking through a narrow gap between two weathered, antique wooden doors. She has voluminous, tight platinum-blonde and silver curly hair with soft ringlets framing her face. Her skin is glowing, warm bronze-tan with a subtle peachy blush, and she has light gray-green eyes with a direct, intense gaze, softly defined brows, and long lashes. Her lips are bold, matte-satin, vivid cherry red, slightly parted to show her teeth. Her head is tilted slightly, and one hand rests near her collarbone. She wears a black off-the-shoulder long-sleeve top. The doors are cracked and peeling, painted in faded dove-gray and pale blue, with rusted metal latches and handles, chipped paint revealing brown wood beneath, and deep grain textures. Shadows from the door frame fall softly across her features.

Lighting and background:
Soft, moody natural window light coming from the front-left, with gentle shadow falloff on one side of the face and luminous highlights on the cheekbones, nose, and lips. The background interior is dark and out of focus, so the subject appears to emerge from the shadows.

Style and camera:
Moody editorial photography blending beauty and urban decay, shot on an 85mm lens at f/1.8, shallow depth of field, razor-sharp focus on the eyes and lips, hyper-detailed skin texture, individual curl definition, chipped paint, and rust, cinematic color grading with a desaturated teal-gray environment against warm skin and a vivid red accent, 8K resolution, vertical 9:16 composition.

Color palette:
Weathered dove gray, faded blue-gray, rust brown, warm bronze skin, platinum silver-blonde, bold lipstick red, deep black.
