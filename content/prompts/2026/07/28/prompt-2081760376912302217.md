## Prompt

VISUAL OS 007 — Violet Manga Character Dossier

Backstory is the most expensive way to make a character feel real. A stat block does it in one glance.

Four strangers, four files:

- TORQUE — Juno Rhee, dune racer, best lap 03:19.482
- VEKTOR — Nia Kade, signal courier, unit 17
- ABYSS — Mara Sol, salvage pilot, depth 09
- CANOPY — Eli Voss, sky botanist, archive 22

You've never met them, but the paperwork insists they've existed for seasons: lap data, pressure logs, route clearances. Nobody fact-checks a number — they inherit it. Instant canon, zero lore dump.

Two tricks worth stealing:

1. Every codename prints twice — a huge distressed violet word cropped off the top edge (atmosphere), and a tight black headline you can actually read (information)
2. The "scan lines" are just a halftone pattern tilted 15° — cheap to render, expensive to ignore
