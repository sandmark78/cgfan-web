## Prompt

A majestic elephant whose entire body is formed from millions of living flowers, creating an enormous walking floral sculpture. Layers of blooming roses, peonies, orchids, cherry blossoms, magnolias, lilies, hydrangeas, wisteria, and wildflowers seamlessly replace skin, each petal gently moving with the wind. Thick vines wrap naturally around the legs while lush emerald moss and delicate fern leaves fill every gap between blossoms. Giant flowering branches extend gracefully from the elephant's back like a living garden, with cascading wisteria hanging down in elegant curtains. Thousands of colorful butterflies, hummingbirds, and tiny bees orbit peacefully around the elephant, occasionally landing on blooming petals. Soft petals continuously detach, float through the air, and magically regrow, making the creature feel alive. Golden morning sunlight filters through a misty enchanted forest, creating warm volumetric rays and illuminating the vibrant floral textures.
