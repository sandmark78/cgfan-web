## Prompt

An extraordinary supermodel wearing an extravagant haute couture gown constructed entirely from thick solid industrial-grade 304/316 stainless steel. Every visible component is fabricated from authentic engineering materials: heavy brushed stainless steel plates, forged stainless steel panels, cold-rolled stainless steel sheets, laser-cut structural components, CNC-machined stainless steel blocks, precision folded sheet metal, and industrial structural assemblies. The dress is assembled like heavy industrial equipment rather than sewn like clothing.

Massive sculptural corsetry, monumental armored skirts, sharp architectural geometry, articulated mechanical joints, overlapping structural steel scales, exposed reinforcement ribs, engineered support frames, welded gussets, and an impossibly long cathedral train create an overwhelming fusion of heavy industry and haute couture.

The material is unmistakably authentic industrial stainless steel used in factories, chemical plants, shipbuilding, pressure vessels, structural fabrication, food-processing equipment, heavy machinery, and industrial piping. The metal must appear extremely thick, rigid, dense, cold, and immensely heavy. It must never resemble silver, chrome, aluminum, platinum, titanium, polished jewelry, decorative metal, metallic fabric, foil, or painted surfaces.

Every surface exhibits authentic industrial manufacturing details: directional brushed grain, CNC machining marks, cold-rolled mill finish, microscopic scratches, forged textures, visible TIG weld beads, heat-affected blue and straw temper colors surrounding welds, laser-cut edges with realistic heat tint, precision press-brake bends, folded sheet-metal geometry, exposed reinforcement plates, structural gussets, welded brackets, industrial flanges, heavy hex bolts, large steel rivets, mechanical fasteners, precision engineering tolerances, and subtle workshop wear.

Physically accurate PBR rendering of genuine 304/316 stainless steel with correct metallic roughness, anisotropic reflections, realistic Fresnel behavior, authentic stainless steel optical properties, crisp industrial reflections, subtle oxidation, realistic weight, thickness, and structural rigidity. The gown looks fabricated inside a heavy steel workshop by industrial engineers, not by jewelers or fashion artisans.

Paris Haute Couture runway, cinematic lighting, luxury fashion editorial, museum-quality masterpiece, ultra photorealistic, hyper realistic, HDR, 8K, physically accurate metallurgy, extreme macro detail, industrial engineering aesthetics, no fabric, no textile, no leather, no plastic, entirely constructed from thick solid industrial stainless steel.
