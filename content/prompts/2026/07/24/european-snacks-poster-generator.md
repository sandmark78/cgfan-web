# 欧洲小吃海报生成器

## 提示词

input ::= [food_brand] + [food_product] + [promotion_type] + [delivery_or_party_theme]

[concept]
render a vertical promotional poster as a hand-drawn mission map for delivering or sharing [food_product].

[world]
the giant product package is the headquarters.
tiny characters launch from it with delivery vehicles, balloons, ladders, parachutes, bikes, carts, ropes, signs, and party supplies.
dashed routes, arrows, comic captions, and little map icons show the journey from box to craving.

[layout]
- big campaign headline at the top
- giant product package as central headquarters
- offer badge attached like an official mission seal
- food reveal inside the package
- doodle routes spreading outward to small scenes:
  home delivery, picnic, office lunch, party table, late-night snack, celebration zone

[art direction]
advertising poster meets illustrated adventure map.
realistic cardboard/product photography plus playful black ink doodles.
limited brand-color accents.
whitespace remains clean and premium.

[negative]
--no cluttered treasure map
--no unrelated characters
--no fake real logos unless supplied
--no unreadable microtext
--no generic delivery ad

## 策展笔记

将广告海报与手绘冒险地图结合，用虚线路线、箭头和漫画字幕展示产品从包装到消费者的旅程。
