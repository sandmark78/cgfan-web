Create an original English novel cover based on the story above.

First understand the novel, then extract its emotional core, character relationships, key scene, visual symbol, and overall identity. Do not generate a random attractive image based only on the title.

Use the most meaningful scene, object, location, or relationship from the story as the main visual concept. Build clear foreground, middle ground, and background depth, with subtle narrative clues that make the viewer curious without explaining the entire plot.

Choose the visual language according to the genre. It may use cinematic photography, architectural composition, vintage paperback design, editorial illustration, collage, or another suitable publishing style. Different stories should naturally produce different compositions, typography, colors, and atmospheres.

Include:
- Novel title
- Author name
- One short tagline
- Optional small genre or publishing text

Typography must feel integrated into the scene and follow the image structure. Do not always place all text on the same side. Use serif, sans-serif, condensed, handwritten, or display typography according to the story.

The final image should feel like a complete, professionally published novel cover with visual depth and strong storytelling—not a movie poster, generic photo poster, tourism ad, or book mockup.

Avoid excessive minimalism, overcrowded text, meaningless decorative copy, Vol.01, issue numbers, page numbers, repetitive layouts, generic AI aesthetics, and unrelated visual elements.

Generate one standalone cover only. Do not create a grid, collage of multiple covers, or physical book mockup.
