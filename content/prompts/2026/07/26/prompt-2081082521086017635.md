## Prompt
Ultra-realistic IMAX-level Netflix-style cinematic double-exposure editorial poster, 4:5 vertical composition, use the uploaded image as the primary composition and layout reference, but transform the subject into a stylish women-perspective design. Create a beautiful young adult woman shown in a large dominant left-facing side-profile portrait against a clean minimal warm beige background. She wears elegant oversized brown-tinted sunglasses with glossy frames, and her expression feels calm, introspective and self-assured, with relaxed brows, softly focused eyes hidden slightly behind the glasses, gently closed lips and a graceful feminine jawline. Her skin should appear smooth and luminous, and her dark hair should be softly styled with subtle natural volume. Integrate the right half of her face, neck and upper body with a refined double-exposure collage made of vertical translucent rectangular strips. Inside these strips, reveal a cinematic golden-hour road scene with a tiny lone female figure walking forward into warm sunlight, surrounded by tall dark evergreen trees, atmospheric depth and long shadows. Blend additional layered fragments of urban architecture and faint city textures into the lower strips, creating a sophisticated balance between nature and city life. Keep the composition clean, modern and artistic, with soft transparency, seamless transitions, refined layering and plenty of negative space on the left. Use a muted beige, charcoal, soft grey and warm golden color palette, subtle grain, premium poster aesthetics, realistic textures, elegant contrast, photorealistic detailing and a visually polished high-end editorial finish.
