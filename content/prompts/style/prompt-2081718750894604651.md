I have been working on a car design prompt for a while now. I am not saying it's perfect but it does produce some pretty visuals. 

Prompt: 
16:9, do this for nikola tesla -> act as a lead automotive engineer and visionary avant-garde designer. 1. deconstruct the masterpiece look at the attached topic . analyze: the energy (chaotic vs. calm). the shapes (fluid vs. geometric). the light source (natural vs. artificial). 2. auto-select chassis (psychographic logic) use this logic to determine the car type: if turbulent or fast -> generate an aggressive electric hypercar. if stately or royal -> generate an ultra-luxury grand tourer. if landscape or rugged -> generate a futuristic expedition rover. if abstract or cubist -> generate a neo-brutalist cybertruck concept. if surreal (e.g., dali) -> generate a fluid concept pod. if else -> ai infers 3. engineering translation (the anti-texture rule) critical: do not just paste the subject onto the car like a sticker. you must translate the art into car parts. brushstrokes -> become sculpted carbon fiber body panel curves and aerodynamic intake channels. colors -> become multi-coat metallic automotive paint (e.g., midnight blue flake, gold leaf trim). shapes -> become physical vents, spoilers, and diffusers. 4. lighting technology logic (the realism fix) you must translate the artist's motifs into realistic automotive lighting using specific technology: if the art has swirls/lines (van gogh) -> use flexible laser wire or oled light strips that physically curve inside a clear glass housing. if the art has dots/stars -> use a parametric dot-matrix led array set deep behind smoked glass. if the art is geometric -> use blocky pixel-cube lights with sharp acrylic edges. the lights must look like physical glass and electronics, not paint. 5. execute visualization (2x2 grid) generate a single image split into a precise 2x2 grid. top left (the hero shot): a front 3/4 view of the vehicle on a minimalist background. the bodywork is real metal and carbon fiber. the swirls of the subject are represented by the actual aerodynamic curvature of the metal body panels. top right (the engineering rear view): a rear view focusing on the taillights. the lights must look like high-tech manufactured objects. show the depth of the lens, the inner circuitry, and the glass casing. the light design mimics the subjects's key motif (e.g., a swirling oled strip) but looks 100% functional. bottom left (the bespoke cabin): an interior shot. use premium materials. dashboard: milled aluminum or polished wood. seats: perforated leather with stitching that matches the subject's stroke pattern. steering wheel: incorporates the color palette in the grip materials. bottom right (the magazine cover): a high-fashion automotive magazine cover. title: avant-garde auto. headline: a clever title relating to the art . the car is shown driving fast on a road that resembles the subject's background. style: unreal engine 5, automotive product visualization, ray-traced reflections, 8k resolution, feasible engineering.
