## Step 1: 单色粘土渲染 / Greybox 重建

Recreate the reference image as a clean monochrome Blender clay render / greybox cinematic reconstruction. Preserve only the composition, subject placement, architecture, silhouette, and lighting direction. Use neutral matte grey clay materials for everything, with no color palette, no film grain, no cinematic color grading, no painterly texture, no surface noise, no ornate texture detail. The goal is clean geometry and spatial structure: one bowed figure centered in a ruined stone hall, tall pillars, rubble floor, arched opening, and diagonal volumetric light from the upper left. Simplify textures into clean sculptural forms while keeping the same scene design and solemn dark fantasy mood.

Make it high-budget festival cinema shot on 35mm film with an anamorphic lens, moody chiaroscuro lighting, grounded production design, clean cinematic detail, and controlled subtle film grain. The final image should be clean and detailed, not noisy, not painterly, not a game screenshot.
