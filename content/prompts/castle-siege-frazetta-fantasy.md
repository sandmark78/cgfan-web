## Prompt

An ancient mountain castle under epic siege—massive stone walls and towering keep besieged by a gleaming army of knights and siege towers, dragons circling smoke-filled sky as catapults hurl fire and defenders pour boiling oil from battlements. High-fantasy oil painting in the heroic tradition of Frank Frazetta—golden hero charge at the breach, impasto banners and bronze armor, blood-orange sunset, wide cinematic panorama of legendary war and impossible valor.
