## Prompt

UNIVERSAL PROMPT - TEXTURED PALETTE KNIFE IMPASTO OIL PAINTING | 1:1

Transform the main subject in the uploaded photograph into a square miniature oil painting using a bold, heavily textured palette knife impasto technique.

1. PAINTING STYLE — EXTREME 3D TEXTURE

Apply thick, generously layered oil paint using bold palette knife strokes, creating a raised impasto surface that appears to protrude 3–5 mm from the canvas.

- Preserve visible directional knife marks, grooves, ridges, and uneven paint textures.
- Assume light comes from the upper left. Add bright highlights to raised paint and deep shadows to recessed and overlapping areas.
- Introduce subtle shadows along paint edges to create the illusion of individually layered, dimensional paint.
- Preserve the original subject's recognizable silhouette, essential colors, and overall composition, but simplify its form into expressive, thick paint masses rather than realistic photographic details.
- Avoid excessive detailing. Use a restrained number of bold, sculptural palette knife strokes while keeping the subject instantly recognizable.

2. BACKGROUND

Use a consistent warm ivory or creamy-white watercolor paper background with a subtle, slightly rough texture.

Keep the background completely flat and understated to emphasize the dimensionality of the painted subject.

3. TYPOGRAPHY

Add a small black handwritten-style title in a lower corner or the area with the most negative space.

Automatically identify the subject and generate suitable text:

- Food / Drinks: Dish or beverage name (e.g., Pancake, Earl Grey Cake).
- Products: Product name or category.
- People: Name or a short expressive phrase representing the subject.
- Animals: Animal name or species.
- Landscapes: Location name or a short descriptive phrase.

Use natural, slightly irregular, casually handwritten lettering. Avoid overly polished or standard-looking typography.

4. FORMAT & COMPOSITION

- Aspect ratio: Strictly 1:1 square.
- Position the main subject centrally.
- Maintain generous negative space around the subject.
- Show only the original main subject without introducing additional props or decorative objects.
- Keep the overall composition minimal, balanced, and visually refined.

FINAL RESULT: A premium miniature impasto oil painting with dramatically raised palette knife textures, realistic paint depth, expressive sculptural strokes, and a warm minimalist presentation. Preserve the identity of the original subject while transforming it into a tactile, three-dimensional painted artwork.
