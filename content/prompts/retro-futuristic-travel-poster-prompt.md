## Prompt

Create an elegant handcrafted 3D paper quilling and layered papercraft travel poster for {DESTINATION}.

Style: vintage 1950s travel advertisement aesthetic meets modern paper art craftsmanship. The entire composition is built from rolled paper strips, layered cardstock, and intricate quilling techniques.

Composition: vertical 2:3 format, featuring iconic landmarks of {DESTINATION} rendered in dimensional paper layers. Foreground shows characteristic local elements (palm trees, mountains, or architecture), midground displays the city skyline, background fades into a gradient sky.

Color palette: warm vintage tones — burnt orange, mustard yellow, teal, cream, and dusty rose. Each layer casts subtle shadows to create depth.

Typography: elegant serif font at the top reading "VISIT {DESTINATION}" in capital letters, with a smaller tagline below in script font.

Texture: visible paper grain, rolled paper edges, slight imperfections that add authenticity to the handcrafted feel.

Lighting: soft directional light from upper left, creating gentle shadows between paper layers.

Mood: nostalgic, inviting, artisanal — evoking the golden age of travel while showcasing modern paper art techniques.

Technical: 8K resolution, photorealistic paper textures, shot with macro lens to capture fine details of paper fibers and rolled edges.
