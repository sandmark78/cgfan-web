## Prompt

Create an ultra-realistic luxury fashion portrait inspired by the concept of "Crimson Luxury." A breathtakingly beautiful woman embodies timeless elegance, passion, sophistication, and old-world opulence. She wears an extraordinary deep burgundy haute couture gown crafted from layers of velvet, silk satin, and flowing couture fabrics. The gown features dramatic sculptural silhouettes, a sweeping cathedral-length train, exquisite embroidery, hand-sewn ruby details, subtle garnet embellishments, and refined haute couture craftsmanship.

Her jewelry is exceptionally luxurious, featuring flawless rubies, rose gold, dark garnets, and antique-cut diamonds. She wears elegant long velvet gloves, a sophisticated couture headpiece, and timeless luxury accessories.

The setting radiates old-world wealth and refined elegance: polished dark wood floors, breathtaking classical architecture, tall arched windows, warm candlelit ambiance, deep red velvet curtains, premium dark leather furniture, an antique grand piano, fresh red roses, crystal decanters, subtle reflections, ornate chandeliers, and an atmosphere of dramatic intimacy. A vintage burgundy luxury vehicle may appear naturally in the background without dominating the composition.

The entire image follows a monochromatic palette of burgundy, wine red, deep crimson, dark garnet, and warm gold highlights. Every detail communicates exclusivity, craftsmanship, timeless beauty, and dramatic luxury rather than extravagance.

Luxury editorial photography, Vogue cover aesthetic, cinematic warm lighting, volumetric light rays, dramatic atmosphere, high dynamic range, ultra-realistic skin, flawless makeup, elegant pose, intricate fabric textures, premium craftsmanship, masterpiece composition, hyper-detailed, photorealistic, 8K, fashion campaign, award-winning photography.

Avoid bright colors, gold dominance, excessive glitter, fantasy costumes, cartoon style, cheap luxury aesthetics, text, logos, watermarks, low quality, oversaturation, distorted anatomy.
