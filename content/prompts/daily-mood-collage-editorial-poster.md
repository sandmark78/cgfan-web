## Prompt

Create a 9:16 vertical editorial collage poster.

Theme: [THEME]
Series title: [TITLE]
Subtitle: [SUBTITLE]
Main subject: [PERSON / PRODUCT / SCENE]
Setting: [CITY STREET / COAST / FLOWER SHOP / CAFÉ / OTHER]
Main colors: [COLOR 1] + [COLOR 2]
Accent color: [ACCENT COLOR]

Style: editorial collage, mixed media, paper cut-out aesthetic, vintage magazine clippings, hand-torn edges, layered composition, typographic elements, minimal color palette, clean white space, modern graphic design

Mood: [CALM / ENERGETIC / NOSTALGIC / PLAYFUL / SOPHISTICATED]

Layout structure:
- Large hero image or central focal point (40-50% of composition)
- Supporting collage elements arranged in asymmetric balance
- Typography integrated as visual element (not just text overlay)
- Negative space for breathing room
- Subtle texture overlays (paper grain, film grain, or fabric texture)

Color treatment:
- Dominant color: [COLOR 1] (60% of composition)
- Secondary color: [COLOR 2] (30% of composition)
- Accent color: [ACCENT COLOR] (10% of composition)
- Desaturated, muted tones with one vibrant accent
- Avoid pure black; use deep charcoal or navy instead

Typography:
- Headline: Bold sans-serif or elegant serif, [UPPERCASE / sentence case]
- Subtitle: Lighter weight, complementary typeface
- Text placement: Integrated into composition, not floating on top
- Consider text wrapping around images or overlapping elements

Final aesthetic: Museum-quality editorial poster, sophisticated and timeless, suitable for gallery exhibition or high-end brand campaign.
